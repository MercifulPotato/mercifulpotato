Item(by='kristjansson', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612312256, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001373, text='The batteries are going to be a consumable if users continue to demand a wireless experience, which most of them seem to prefer, regardless of whether that’s delivered as a dongle or integrated into the headset.  It would be nice if we didn’t demand so many things with batteries in them, but that isn’t IMO an Apple problem, that’s a demand pattern.  Encasing them in the product might even keep batteries out of landfill.  An old AA or laptop battery is easy to pitch, a $550 headset is much more likely to be repaired by someone in a position to recycle the battery.<p>Bluetooth and the integrated form factor is a risk, but I think that’s not a ten year concern.  It would be nice if they had a physical port for the eventuality though.')
