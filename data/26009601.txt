Item(by='nwallin', descendants=None, kids=[26009712], score=None, time=1612316464, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26009197, text='I can&#x27;t say I agree.<p>They&#x27;re developing this thing in stages; they&#x27;ve developed and tested the engines, they&#x27;ve developed and are testing launching the thing, they&#x27;ve developed and are testing the re-entry flop. They have partially developed the whole &quot;landing&quot; thing and if you&#x27;re going to be testing the belly flop, you might as well put whatever you have with regards to landing through its paces as well.<p>By having a small team develop and test individual parts in stages, you avoid needing a giant team, and you avoid needing to have all of the schedules line up to be ready to go all at once. And it&#x27;s the engineers who develop the rocket that is expensive, not the manufacturing.<p>IMHO the benefits of having a completely flexible schedule greatly outweigh the expensive of assuming failure.')
