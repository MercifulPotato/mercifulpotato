Item(by='upofadown', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612374018, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26011215, text='There is a convention to the use of the E2EE term. All encryption involves a party that encrypts and one that decrypts. When the German U-boat operator used an Enigma machine to decode a message off the radio no one then or now would call what had happened E2EE. That is even in the face of the fact that many operators in many U-boats were decoding the same message. EteSync works in the same way.<p>E2EE is a term normally only used when some distinction from a default case is important. It is in a sense a weaker term than just &quot;encryption&quot;. It is normally only used for particular cases in messaging.<p>Something like: &quot;All data is strongly encrypted at all times when off the users devices.&quot; strikes me as stronger, better and easier to understand in this case.')
