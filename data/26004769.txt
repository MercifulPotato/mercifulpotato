Item(by='shireboy', descendants=None, kids=[26005795, 26005005], score=None, time=1612291092, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004634, text='Many Christians I know are aware of and even study these myths and other religions, and there is evidence of early Christians doing so as well.  In general, I understand their view on them is that God works through those myths and in some cases real events.  In the case of Zoroastrianism, an ancient Christian hymn goes &quot;For by Your birth those who adored stars were taught by a star to worship You, the Sun of Justice, and to know You, Orient from on High.&quot;  So, this is an early example of Christians acknowledging other religious experiences, but still believing they point to a &quot;true myth&quot; about Jesus.<p>You may well disagree with their conclusions, but I wouldn&#x27;t assume Christians haven&#x27;t thought about these things.')
