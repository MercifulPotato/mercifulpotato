Item(by='kmeisthax', descendants=None, kids=[26023332], score=None, time=1612411663, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26022114, text='The reason why subdomains work is that you can make the root domain go to your own site, while the subdomain goes to your ad provider. If you use a CNAME it&#x27;s literally just a matter of publishing another DNS recod and you&#x27;re done. Moving to a subdirectory requires setting up a reverse proxy on your existing infrastructure, and keeping it up to date every time your ad network switches domains. Hell, on a lot of platforms you don&#x27;t even have the ability to reconfigure the server in such a way as to proxy through an entire ad network, so you&#x27;ll have to use a platform or technology specific plugin if available.<p>That&#x27;s why the ad industry used third-party domains in the first place, BTW - it&#x27;s a zero-setup solution. Subdomains are minimal-setup; subdirectories are a huge hassle.')
