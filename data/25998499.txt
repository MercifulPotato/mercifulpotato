Item(by='nicbou', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612254206, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25997027, text='Good writing is valuable. People just aren&#x27;t used to pay for it. We just take it for granted, even when it saves our bacon. We&#x27;ll read expert advice for hours, and don&#x27;t think twice about even thanking them.<p>I often get messages from readers saying that they depended on the content I write and read it like the bible. They used it to start a business or get a residence permit.<p>There&#x27;s a donation button under every post, out of 100k visitors, around 3-6 will donate. I get more emails with extra questions than I get donations. When I patiently answer those, maybe half will donate 5-15€, and only since I added a line about it in my signature.<p>By comparison, affiliate income pays roughly 100x better <i>if you stay neutral</i>. If you can&#x27;t get paid to sell your content, you get paid to sell your audience. This shouldn&#x27;t be a surprise to anyone.<p>I want to be uncompromisingly helpful, but the incentives are often at odds with this. It&#x27;s also not surprising that many websites get greedy and fall apart.<p>If the maintainers of critical open source projects have to beg for enough money to keep the lights on, I don&#x27;t think the problem is the lack of value, but the expectation that digital content is free.')
