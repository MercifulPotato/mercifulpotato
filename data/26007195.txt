Item(by='dhnajsjdnd', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612302122, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26006786, text='I think there’s a common misunderstanding about how tax write-offs work. If you donate <i>x</i> dollars, and your marginal tax rate is <i>t</i>, you end up losing x - xt dollars. That means you have less money than if you didn’t donate, even after accounting for the write-off. Arguments that somebody only donated money for the tax write-off usually don’t make sense.<p>Possibles exceptions to this include hard-to-value assets like art, where someone could potentially exaggerate the value by at least 1&#x2F;t to defraud the tax authorities, but this doesn’t seem relevant to donating publicly-traded Microsoft stock. Bill Gates would be richer if he didn’t make these donations.')
