Item(by='jacurtis', descendants=None, kids=[26024256], score=None, time=1612392525, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018478, text='The fact that Wordpress adds it automatically is a huge win because half or more of all websites are Wordpress. So there are a lot of content creators that don&#x27;t even know what RSS is and yet you are able to subscribe to them because Wordpress automatically provides a full-text RSS feed for every Wordpress blog.<p>I built my personal blog on a static site generator (Gridsome) and had to add RSS to my site intentionally. Luckily there is a plugin that provides this functionality easily now, but you still have to know about RSS, decide to offer it, and then install the plugin and configure it to compile your RSS feed with each site build. So it is something the developer or maintainer of the site has to think about (at least initially, now that it is configured it updates automatically).<p>I am not a huge fan of Wordpress, but I am grateful that they continue to offer it &quot;automatically&quot; for all Wordpress sites. This means that I am able to subscribe in my feed reader for small sites that might not even think about RSS.')
