Item(by='danpalmer', descendants=None, kids=[26015743], score=None, time=1612370077, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014831, text='A close friend of mine has worked there for many years. We&#x27;ve spoken a lot about the tech.<p>I don&#x27;t know the answers to all your questions, but I do know that the emphasis is on research, not consumer (or hobbyist) use. I believe the devices are ~free, but each run requires using a consumable part that has to be either disposed of or returned for refurbishment, and I believe these are hundreds of dollars each.<p>The big advancement is the size and cost of the devices, the fact that a lab can have one on every desk rather than a communal machine that you have to queue your samples up for, or a device you can transport in field kit.<p>They do have cloud services that do much of the processing for you, but I suspect you&#x27;d want to be able to manipulate the data so you&#x27;d need your own data processing tools locally. It&#x27;s not going to give you a 23andMe style report, it&#x27;s more likely to say &quot;yep, that&#x27;s a human&quot; vs &quot;you&#x27;re ecoli&quot;. I believe they do have training for how to do this data analysis, but I suspect this is targeted at customers on large contracts.')
