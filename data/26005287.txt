Item(by='yters', descendants=None, kids=[26006763], score=None, time=1612293531, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002616, text='It could also be the case the human mind transcends computation, i.e. a transfinite, yet limited, halting oracle, and as such is logically impossible to reduce to a Turing machine.  To insist this is not the case merely based on materialism is begging the question.  It is a very simple and formally rigorous point, but gets obscure by high fallutin philosophical language like &quot;reductionism&quot;.')
