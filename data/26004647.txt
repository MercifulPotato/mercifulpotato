Item(by='DominoTree', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612290450, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25997506, text='At one point, building Go and running its test suite on OpenBSD would replace &#x2F;dev&#x2F;null with a standard flat file. After some time, the disk would fill up because everything piped to &#x2F;dev&#x2F;null was now being stored on disk. Not to mention the additional I&#x2F;O happening.<p>Go already uses libc by default on many platforms. But there are issues - sometimes the libc behaves differently than Go&#x27;s documented APIs, but this is primarily a documentation issue. Contrariwise, sometimes, Go&#x27;s native APIs don&#x27;t behave on systems due to platform-specific implementation bugs.<p>I think this is a good move overall.')
