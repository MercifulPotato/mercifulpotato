It has nothing to do with reduction of symptoms.<p>I think this is unintuitive because the &quot;real&quot; solution that laypersons want is, what percentage of vaccinated people exposed to a disease get infected? We can&#x27;t measure that directly because we can&#x27;t just expose a bunch of folks to Covid and hope for the best. But if the sample of placebo and vaccinated people is truly random, this would appear to arrive at the same ratio. (re: when I said &quot;ignoring all uninfected&quot;?  in a good sample, the ratio of unexposed vaccinated and control units should be about the same)')
