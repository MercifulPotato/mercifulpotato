Item(by='bob1029', descendants=None, kids=[26018749], score=None, time=1612384384, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018102, text='I had the same perspective on this. I spent some time building up a 2d graphics library from zero, primarily for drawing very simple UIs on resource-constrained devices.<p>I initially tried using D3D&#x2F;Win32 APIs to perform the high-level drawing operations for me, but found that for the scope of functionality that I required, these interfaces were far too heavy-handed. These also have lots of platform-specific requirements and mountains of arcane frustrations to fight off.<p>I didn&#x27;t need to interface with some complex geometry or shader pipeline in hardware. Raytracing is hilariously out of scope. I really just needed a dead simple way to draw basic primitives to a 2d array of RGB bytes and then get that to the display device as quickly as possible. What I ended up with is something that isnt capable of very much, but can run on any platform and without a dedicated GPU. I also feel like this was a much better learning experience than if I had slammed my head into the D3D&#x2F;opengl&#x2F;et. al. wall.')
