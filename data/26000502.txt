Item(by='adrusi', descendants=None, kids=[26001768, 26000794], score=None, time=1612271199, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999415, text='<i>why doesn&#x27;t it gain more momentum?</i><p>It has plenty of momentum. The two main desktop environments have wayland ports. Several distros ship wayland by default.<p><i>without providing a compatibility layer</i><p>Wayland <i>does</i> provide a compatibility layer, XWayland, which runs an X server that does all the internal processing that Xorg does, and then send the final frame to wayland to put on the screen. It works very well, just with some quirks around mixed-DPI setups, which X can&#x27;t handle well to begin with.<p>When people complain about wayland breaking their set-ups, they&#x27;re talking about tools like xdotool, which Wayland deliberately does not allow because they&#x27;re security risks. Old applications, the kind that put a window on the screen, rather than manipulating other applications&#x27; windows, work just fine.')
