Item(by='Person5478', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612358170, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007785, text='From my perspective, that back and forth is a natural part of software development.  If they wanted red and once they see it they decide they want a different shade, to me that&#x27;s good software development (as long as it doesn&#x27;t turn into bikeshedding).<p>Often people aren&#x27;t sure themselves what they want until they see it, or they get new&#x2F;better ideas after seeing it in motion (or decide they don&#x27;t like it).  Good software dev is like boiling water.  It&#x27;s tumultuous with a smattering of chaos, but in general drives towards the goal.<p>It also allows you, as a developer, to take more ownership of the software as a solution.  At the end of the day you&#x27;re helping solve someone&#x27;s problems.  For me that&#x27;s satisfying, even if it&#x27;s a small problem.<p>And it&#x27;s been my experience when you do that well, people love you for it.<p>And to be clear, I put customer in quotes for a reason.  Sometimes the problem you&#x27;re solving is that batch job needs to be realtime, or that one query is slow as the data got too large, ad nauseum.  It&#x27;s not always non-technical people.<p>What I never want is a PM to just tell me to make a query faster without being able to discuss with the people it affects what their use case is so I can start optimizing for the specifics rather than just in general (although sometimes that&#x27;s the right approach).')
