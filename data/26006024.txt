Item(by='_kst_', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612297011, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004598, text='The numbers, as in &quot;bash(1)&quot; refer to sections of the manual.<p>&quot;Info&quot; is a different documentation format, most commonly used for GNU tools (including bash). The section numbers don&#x27;t generally apply to info documents.<p>For some tools, the man and info documents have the same information. For some, the man page is a brief summary that directs you to the info documentation. For some, there&#x27;s only a man page. (And for some there isn&#x27;t even that.)')
