Item(by='swiftcoder', descendants=None, kids=[26015531, 26017024], score=None, time=1612358707, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26013326, text='&gt; Computers may be ridiculously fast and have enormous memory today and maybe you can live with the default<p>Often these hardware improvements exacerbate problems with memory management. For years the runtime of the default JVM garbage collector would blow up past 48 GB of allocations. Which was probably fine for 99% of software out there, but the day your honking great server blew past that... was a bad day all round.<p>It pays to know your allocators and garbage collectors inside and out for modern cloud scale computing.')
