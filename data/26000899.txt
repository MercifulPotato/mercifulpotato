Item(by='Clewza313', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612273773, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000013, text='Tediously complicated explanation:<p>Burmese is tonal, and has both the noun &quot;Myanma&quot; and the related but not identical adjective &quot;Myanma&quot;.  The noun has a low tone and a long &quot;a&quot;, which reflecting British pronunciation was spelled &quot;Myanmar&quot;.  The adjectival form uses the creaky tone with a short &quot;a&quot;, hence it&#x27;s supposed to be spelled &quot;Myanma&quot; without the r.<p>In practice, though, nobody cares and everybody says &quot;Myanmar&quot; for both, even the official English translation of the constitution, which says &quot;Myanmar language&quot;.<p>&quot;Myanman&quot; or &quot;Myanmarese&quot; are both wrong though.')
