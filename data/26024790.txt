There are current research explorations of whether dark matter and normal matter interact in ways other than via gravity:<p><a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.sciencedaily.com&#x2F;releases&#x2F;2018&#x2F;07&#x2F;180713093545.htm" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.sciencedaily.com&#x2F;releases&#x2F;2018&#x2F;07&#x2F;180713093545.h...</a><p>So if the two kinds of matter can interact it may be possible that the mind is either partially made of dark matter or influenced by dark matter.  Maybe our minds experience dark matter as a kind of &quot;weather&quot; that has some effect on our thoughts.  Maybe it is responsible for times of widespread rationality or irrationality.  As (or if) we learn more about dark matter it will be interesting to think about how it may interact with our reality, including our minds.')
