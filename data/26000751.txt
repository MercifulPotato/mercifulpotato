Item(by='graeme', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612272820, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999831, text='If the australian law applied to the web broadly, hacker news would have to pay for all the links.<p>In reality, <i>being linked to</i> on the web is extremely valuable. Google has had to ban the practices of sites <i>paying to be linked to</i>.<p>The Australian law completely gets the value chain backwards, forcing tech companies to pay for a specific subset of links, and not allowing them to decline to link either.')
