Item(by='wolfwern', descendants=1, kids=[26009665], score=1, time=1612315273, title='Total Emission = total of chain emissions', item_type='story', url=None, parent=None, text='If considering only emissions at the power plant, then all chain emissions need to be considered: production, parts, including fuel development.
