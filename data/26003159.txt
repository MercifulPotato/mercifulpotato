Item(by='chousuke', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612284094, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002964, text='Right, and nothing prevents you from writing a compositor that talks to an external process to handle window management. It just happens that no-one does it, because it&#x27;s insane and difficult to make work properly. The compositor&#x27;s job literally is to juggle the clients&#x27; buffers, which includes deciding what gets drawn on the screen and how.<p>There are other processes involved though where the compositor and the client <i>do</i> talk the Wayland protocol via IPC, such that the compositor hands off various responsibilities to external processes.<p>As far as I understand, X11 ended up with the window manager architecture pretty much by accident; initially, window managers didn&#x27;t even exist.')
