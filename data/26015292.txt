Item(by='mumblemumble', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612369817, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014596, text='I&#x27;m guessing it&#x27;s a blip in history, not a trend.<p>What we&#x27;re seeing is a first cohort of people who get in and build their careers while the niche is still young, and before everyone knows about it. Then there&#x27;s a much larger cohort of second movers who see the work as aspirational and want to get into it. They have a <i>much</i> more difficult path to success. They have to compete with each other, and with the established players, for a piece of a pie that is no longer growing. Very few end up succeeding.<p>Jobs where I&#x27;ve previously seen this happen include lawyer, craft brewer, restaurantier, data scientist, academic, airline pilot, and games developer. I&#x27;m not sure I see any particular reason to think that it will be different for professional gaming.')
