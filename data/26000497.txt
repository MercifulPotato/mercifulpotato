Item(by='CoffeeDregs', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612271139, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999071, text='Just in case someone RTFA and gets worried: this is breathless FUD and could be handled much more usefully in some other fashion that invites discussion of pros&#x2F;cons (such as is happening on HN but I doubt the gist will be updated...).  Wayland is an 0.99:1.00 replacement for x11.  I would appreciate a succinct &quot;Wayland changed X, Y and Z and that will affect applications A, B, C&quot; but TFA isn&#x27;t that.<p>Much like systemd, Wayland sounded as though it might have some serious wrinkles but those, as with systemd, never really materialized for me (a power user on a powerful machine).  Most issues I experienced were transient as applications caught up with the switch (mostly screen sharing).  Even though it&#x27;s not too important to me, games (wine and native) are working better than ever on Linux (and Wayland).<p>I think the two persistent &quot;issues&quot; I&#x27;ve seen are: graphical ssh (X-over-ssh); and, maybe, others screen drawing on my screen in Slack calls.<p>I trust the arguments from the x11&#x2F;Wayland developers and, AFAICT, Wayland has been a Good Thing but some eggs were broken to make the omelette...')
