Item(by='jamesponddotco', descendants=None, kids=[26006848], score=None, time=1612300000, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005038, text='I use EteSync both at my company and personal life to share events with the team or with my wife, and to manage my contacts and tasks on my &quot;no Google allowed&quot; phone.<p>I can integrate with calcurse[1] to stay up to date during the day or with aerc[2] when I need to send an email to someone, because they include a Dav bridge[3], but I can also integrate it with the GNOME environment because a module for evolution exists[4].<p>Plus, the developer[5] has been genuinely nice and handled all support, and billing questions like a champ, in a speedy manner, and always in a polite tone.<p>My only problem with EteSync so far is that sharing an event can only be done through the web application right now, so I need to switch from calcurse to a browser, share the event, and then come back. Minor issue in the grand scheme of things, though.<p>Well worth the price, in my opinion. Might be one of the few services that I do not migrate to a self-hosted instance instead.<p>[1] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.calcurse.org&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.calcurse.org&#x2F;</a><p>[2] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;aerc-mail.org&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;aerc-mail.org&#x2F;</a><p>[3] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;etesync&#x2F;etesync-dav&#x2F;blob&#x2F;master&#x2F;README.md#installation" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;etesync&#x2F;etesync-dav&#x2F;blob&#x2F;master&#x2F;README.md...</a><p>[4] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.etesync.com&#x2F;user-guide&#x2F;evolution&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.etesync.com&#x2F;user-guide&#x2F;evolution&#x2F;</a><p>[5] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;tasn" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;tasn</a>')
