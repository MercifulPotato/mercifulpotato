Item(by='beckingz', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612285539, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001277, text='Double masking - using a N95 and a cloth mask - works better because it protects you and others.<p>The N95 protects you, but many N95 masks have exhalation valves that blast particles away and make it easier for you to breath.<p>The cloth mask on top of the N95 protects others, by reduce the amount and distance of particles. This is especially true for N95 masks that have valves.')
