If people I upvote a lot upvoted it, show it to me.  There is lots of interesting crypto work on anonymous verifiable voting that would be relevant here.<p>If you&#x27;re worried about an echo chamber, add random noise.  If you think people won&#x27;t do that, well, if they want to make echo chambers for themselves you can&#x27;t stop that either.  Offer them a way out and the smart people will take it; then you can use their upvotes. Give the people knobs and they will play with them.')
