Item(by='brailsafe', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612266064, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25992390, text='For others new to this subject on HN, go to Algolia and search for ADHD.<p>Here&#x27;s a question I posed a while back, with some helpful responses. <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;news.ycombinator.com&#x2F;item?id=21716306" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;news.ycombinator.com&#x2F;item?id=21716306</a><p>Also, it&#x27;s worth diving into the literature on a number of conditions if you&#x27;re unsure or curious. For me, I only started to think something was really different when I compared my interests and how obsessively I&#x27;d learn about them, with the friends I&#x27;d be talking with over coffee. There was something seemingly absurd about how much I&#x27;d think about an idea or subject, compared to the most I was able to get someone talking about anything they were compelled by. Usually this had to do with systems or cities or the like, and so at first I was leaning more towards high level autism. It&#x27;s hard to analyze yourself from the outside, so a practitioner who can ask hopefully specific questions about your family and history is very helpful.<p>Otherwise, if you&#x27;ve found something that works for you, then it probably doesn&#x27;t matter what your label is, just roll with it.<p>Lastly, I do appreciate the sharing of personal experience. I&#x27;m a SE who has been out of work for 7 months and doesn&#x27;t have a  damn clue how to succeed or how to keep pushing. Have lost 6 jobs over 8 years for varying reasons, and it&#x27;s a tough life for someone with ADHD symptoms. It&#x27;s extremely easy to burnout, because to be motivated means to be very motivated to a fault, among other reasons. Then when you do burnout, you realize that nobody actually gives a shit about the work you do, and you sink to the bottom when you get fired.')
