Item(by='Arainach', descendants=None, kids=[26011854], score=None, time=1612294554, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999022, text='Car units are integrated with your car&#x27;s controls.  I almost never need to use the touchscreen because of my in-steering-wheel controls.  A smartphone with Bluetooth only covers the basic scenarios where you know where you&#x27;re going before you leave - and that only if you spend far MORE effort hooking up a charger, a mount, launching the app you need, and you never need to switch apps (such as between maps and music) during your drive.<p>A good mapping solution enables all sorts of things:\n* Seeing alternative routes you don&#x27;t know about or expect along with their anticipated time difference\n* Easily adding stops on the way (gas stations, restaraunts, you name it)\n* Letting you know what you need to do soon (such as seeing a pattern of four lanes on the freeway and learning that you need to be in the second from the right)\n* Giving you a solid idea of when you have to be watching for your next turn and when you can simply focus on the road without reading street signs.  &quot;Next turn in 3 miles&quot; means look at the road, &quot;next turn in 0.2 miles&quot; means you need to start looking at street signs\n* Automatic rerouting.  If you DO miss a turn or get stuck behind traffic from an accident, you don&#x27;t need to immediately start thinking about how best to get back on course.  You can focus on the driving instead.<p>In the music scenario, it gives both voice controls that let you focus on the road (&quot;Play Modest Mouse in Spotify&quot; rather than having to dig through albums or radio stations), integration with your car&#x27;s controls (next track involves lifting my thumb rather than reaching for a knob in the middle of the car or my phone).<p>Saying &quot;no one should ever hear a text message while driving&quot; is just abstinence-only education at this point, and the message works about as well as it does for sexual education.  That ship has sailed and it&#x27;s simply not a valid line of argument.  &quot;No one should reach for a touch screen&quot; isn&#x27;t any better - it&#x27;s not a good model, but it&#x27;s  the reality in a huge percentage of vehicles sold today.<p>This isn&#x27;t over-optimization - it&#x27;s simply a quality of life increase.  Most people don&#x27;t want to have to micromanage every detail of their trips - they want to get from point A to point B safely, reliably, and rapidly.  Good car systems make that simple.  If you want to sit your Rand McNally atlas next to your manual transmission and your 8-track, that&#x27;s your right, but other people want to focus on the parts that they HAVE to be involved with, not the parts technology can help with.')
