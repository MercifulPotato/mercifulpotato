And I only got &quot;screen&quot; sharing i.e. not &quot;app sharing&quot; or &quot;monitor&#x2F;virtual-monitor sharing&quot; working. Latter part could well be some more config or tweaking I need to do, regardless it&#x27;s not the users fault if they can&#x27;t figure this out in Wayland when it works fine everywhere else out of the box. I&#x27;ve gotten it to work well enough for my personal use case but it&#x27;s definitely not far enough along for the typical use case.<p>X11 isn&#x27;t that great with monitor setups with multiple DPI. It&#x27;s certainly one of the biggest user facing &quot;wins&quot; for Wayland but much like you dismiss playing WoW as something you don&#x27;t do most users don&#x27;t deal with mixed DPI setups so the biggest &quot;win&quot; really doesn&#x27;t mean much to most. That doesn&#x27;t mean it can&#x27;t be a great reason for you to prefer Wayland though.<p>Sway won&#x27;t even launch on my Nvidia computers so I&#x27;ve just got them running Windows with WSL. I don&#x27;t think it&#x27;s X11&#x2F;Wayland&#x27;s fault on this one, Nvidia is just a bit too locked down when it comes to Linux preventing good support from anybody. At the same time the professional hardware is just better so you don&#x27;t always have a choice to just use an AMD card even if you were willing to change hardware for better support.<p>I really like my Wayland setup but it&#x27;s important to understand the tradeoffs I was willing to make and the efforts I was willing to put in don&#x27;t mean Wayland was perfect it means it was better for my situation. For another user it&#x27;s probably a HORRIBLE fit right now. That&#x27;s not their fault, nor anyone else&#x27;s fault, it&#x27;s just a difference in use case.<p>I think Wayland is getting pretty close (particularly for the less stubborn willing to let xwayland run as well) to being ready for most users but that&#x27;s what many have been trumpeting for 5 years so it&#x27;s created a bit of pushback when you say it is now.')
