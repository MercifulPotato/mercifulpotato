Item(by='kiwidrew', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612304148, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002363, text='RISC-V is just an instruction set specification.  It says absolutely nothing about the peripherals that need to be attached to a RISC-V core to produce a usable system.<p>Whereas the IBM PC was delivered as a complete system (including all the peripherals and glue logic) and to achieve 100% compatibility the clones were exactly that:  they copied absolutely everything about the PC, down to level of the individual logic gates in the &quot;glue&quot; that interfaced the CPU and memory and peripheral controllers.')
