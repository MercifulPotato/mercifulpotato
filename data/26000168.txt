Item(by='martinald', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612268809, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999870, text='The thing is though for many workloads (I&#x27;d even say most) applications get I&#x2F;O constrained way quicker than CPU, so scaling CPU will not give you the boost that you expect on AWS and the other big cloud providers.<p>Keep in mind that PCIe4 NVMes can do 5-7GB&#x2F;sec (gigabytes - not gigabits) read and write, with up to 500K IOPS.<p>Compare that to RDS on AWS which charges a comical $0.10 per IOPS-month, so just for IO it would be $50k&#x2F;month (and for many DBs IOPS are more important than CPU) to meet the performance of one NVMe SSD, which can be bought retail for probably $200.<p>I don&#x27;t think I&#x27;ve ever seen such ridiculous pricing. I do understand AWS can be more expensive, but it is just outrageous margin for IO performance (and external bandwidth, but that&#x27;s a whole other story!).')
