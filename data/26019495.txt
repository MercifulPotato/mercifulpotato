Item(by='sbauch', descendants=None, kids=[26021775], score=None, time=1612388322, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018752, text='I don&#x27;t think I&#x27;ve changed the meaning unilaterally, others here seem to agree that the meaning is evolving.<p>I can concede that we are guilty of wanting the wave of goodwill, but I have a hard time agreeing that this is a bait and switch. And I certainly hope nobody is going to die because we use &quot;open source&quot; instead of &quot;source available&quot;.<p>The part of this conversation that leaves me wanting is that it all seems so positive rather than normative, and doesn&#x27;t consider the benefits of choosing this license over a closed source product.<p>Our goals here are to make SAML SSO more accessible and run a business that helps some good customers use the software we&#x27;ve created. There&#x27;s a lot of net positives in that IMO, and this whole &quot;but it&#x27;s not ACTUALLY open source&quot; is a distraction borne of inflexibility and a lack of creativity.<p>So how do we as an industry move forward in a way that allows individual use of source available software, but prevents companies from unfairly profiting off of that? Is that desirable? What do we call that? Maybe business source, maybe source available is fine. That&#x27;s a lot more interesting of a conversation to me. If you won&#x27;t have that conversation until we strike open source from our website I understand.')
