Item(by='arghwhat', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612267232, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999755, text='This is indicative of glibc&#x27;s bad design more than anything else.<p>stat(2) is not a single syscall. The changes are exposed as new, isolated syscalls (sys_stat, sys_newstat, sys_stat64), with glibc switching internally between them as it sees fit, surprising developers in the process.<p>This makes stat a great example of the syscall being easier to work with, more stable and more reliable than the glibc wrapper.')
