Item(by='rswail', descendants=None, kids=[26014905], score=None, time=1612343924, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007570, text='You&#x27;re confusing two different things.<p>One is the payment capability of the customer to pay RH when the purchase is settled (T+2).<p>The other is the relationship and payment capability of <i>RH</i> to the clearing house (DTCC) for its orders that it has placed with other brokers.<p>RH is required, as a broker, to provide its own collateral to the clearing house so that the clearing house can guarantee to <i>other</i> brokers that all their trades will be settled at T+2.<p>RH is <i>not allowed by law</i> to use their customers&#x27; money as collateral to DTCC. If RH goes broke, then DTCC does the following:<p>a) takes all their collateral and uses it to make the other brokers good on their trades (which makes the counterparties good on the trade)\nb) takes the customers money and uses it to complete the trades (which makes the customers good on their trade).<p>RH is left to be picked apart.<p>That&#x27;s the purpose of the clearinghouse.')
