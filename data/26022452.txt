Item(by='dhosek', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612411625, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014253, text='Having this list really helps to refresh my memory, but I do have an uncanny knack for remembering things I read. Perhaps the most notable case of this was that while taking a theology class in 1999, I was able to remember a passage in a book I&#x27;d read in 1993, but <i>where</i> in the book the passage was and could go to my bookshelves, pull down the volume and find the passage in about ten minutes. It&#x27;s really handy when it comes to creative writing workshops in that I can often pull up a useful novel or story for a writer from my memory.<p>As for volume, a lot comes from what I prioritize. I don&#x27;t watch much television (especially by American standards), and back when commuting was a thing, that was 60–90 minutes a day of reading time.<p>And, alas, I still fall victim to the buying more books than I can read trap (for a brief while I had the unread books down to a single IKEA Billy bookcase, but I fell off the wagon and now there are piles of books on most flat surfaces stacked with books that I intend to read.')
