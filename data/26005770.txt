Item(by='brandall10', descendants=None, kids=[26008895], score=None, time=1612295882, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005703, text='If you use your Airpods enough (ie. all day at work, on phone calls, etc) - you can easily wear out the battery in that amount of time.  2-3 charges per day in 15 months gets to about the 1000 charge area where the battery begins to drop quickly.<p>The Pros also had a design defect that led to a crackle.  I had both my Pro earpieces replaced a few months back - independently, for that issue.<p>In any case, Airpods are meant to be disposable.  Apple does not replace the batteries on them for battery service issues, you just get new ones.<p>The Maxes are different, the battery can be serviced, and it will last considerably longer - at about 5x the life per charge, you will likely exhaust the shelf life of the battery before it dies from usage.')
