Item(by='halfmatthalfcat', descendants=3, kids=[26003399], score=9, time=1612284510, title='Ask HN: Is the dev community too toxic toward aspiring developers?', item_type='story', url=None, parent=None, text='I was just having a discussion with some fellow devs about the toxicity levels aspiring or junior developers face as they try to break into the industry.<p>While there is an insane amount of information readily available to become self-taught, people within the community often gatekeep, unduly criticize or by pure ego, are toxic toward those trying to learn.<p>I personally feel that there are reasons on both sides for the toxicity:<p>- An unknown unknowns problem that creates false confidence in junior devs that grate more established developers.<p>- A dev marketing ecosystem that rewards learning frameworks and not languages.<p>- Dev tooling that obfuscates in the name of developer friendliness&#x2F;onboarding.<p>I&#x27;m sure there are others but just curious as to everyone&#x27;s take.')
