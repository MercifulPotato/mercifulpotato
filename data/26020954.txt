Exclusive talks with people they find interesting is one way to do that. As a company I would put that in my recruitment material. Any company who doesn&#x27;t do this is now at a disadvantage. They&#x27;re going to want to get their own exclusive talks now. You want top employees, you have to offer top perks, and you&#x27;re competing with other very wealthy firms who have the ability to pay essentially unlimited money for said perks.
