Item(by='cle', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612378621, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017356, text='I don&#x27;t think the author is trying to say &quot;don&#x27;t try anything new unless you have evidence&quot;, but &quot;don&#x27;t use something without understanding why&quot;. Experimenting with a practice to understand it better is a perfectly valid reason. Adopting a practice and expecting it to magically solve your productivity problems, without understanding why and whether the tradeoffs are appropriate, is not a valid reason...that&#x27;s cargo culting.<p>Another way to look at it is...what are your expectations? If you are using something with the expectation that it&#x27;ll magically solve your problems, and you don&#x27;t really know why...you&#x27;re cargo culting. If you&#x27;re using something and <i>suspecting</i> that it&#x27;ll solve your problems, and you expect to watch it and gather feedback and evidence on whether it&#x27;s actually helping or not, and potentially axe it in the future if it&#x27;s not working out...that&#x27;s not cargo culting at all, but effective process.')
