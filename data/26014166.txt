Item(by='perardi', descendants=None, kids=[26014238, 26015594, 26014373], score=None, time=1612364552, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26013535, text='I asked this downthread, but to rephrase: are there any semi-objective case studies on blockchains being useful for physical items, such as the food supply tracking that IBM is doing?<p>(I mean, I see plenty of blockchain startups promoting this use case, but they inevitably end up with a pitch to get on a blockchain.)<p>I do not understand how, in the case of food supply, that a blockchain is somehow better than a good ol’ database. You can verify the ledger, but I don’t see how that’s the primary issue. It seems like the issue would be getting everyone up and down the supply chain <i>to get on board with tracking everything</i>, and not the integrity of the ledger. If a meat packing plant is going to adulterate the pork, the problem is going to be getting any verification <i>at all</i>, and not them hiring some hackers to mess with a database.')
