Item(by='caffeine', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612381779, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014590, text='I get what you&#x27;re saying and I think your terminology makes sense.<p>But the way to understand this is to reframe your view of &quot;money&quot; from being some special, neutral thing to just being another asset.<p>At any given moment, you could own $1000, or some gold, or some bitcoin, or whatever else.   There is nothing special about the fact that it&#x27;s $ you&#x27;re holding rather than DOGE or SPY.<p>So imagine a 2-asset world, that has SPY and $ in it.  You are holding $1000 right now, and the market goes from 1 SPY = $1 to 1 SPY = $1000.  That is a loss.  Denominated in SPY, you just lost 999 SPY.')
