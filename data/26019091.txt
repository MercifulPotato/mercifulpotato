Item(by='bayindirh', descendants=None, kids=[26019695], score=None, time=1612386319, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26016167, text='I haven&#x27;t worked in full time labor job, however I helped or worked with laborers on volunteering basis. OTOH, just because I worked as a volunteer, I didn&#x27;t work less when compared to them.<p>Similarly, on my current job, I have worked off-site, in hazardous sites, in off-hours (including all-nighters), in movement restricted conditions (think as soft imprisonment inside a building) or extremely stressful circumstances. These weren&#x27;t &quot;Oh, there&#x27;s a deadline, so let&#x27;s crunch!&quot; stuff. It was against all odds, against the clock rescue jobs where nobody including your teammates were believing that it&#x27;s possible, but we (me and a friend) have prevailed.<p>On the hardware mounting stuff, it was not again &quot;let&#x27;s playfully install&quot; this stuff too. 100+ servers, left in boxes in front of the building. Carry, unbox, install rails, mount, make all the cabling (incl. electricity, from distribution boxes if req&#x27;d) and make them work, in 2 days most.<p>Yesterday, I was carrying servers to make some part replacements on them.<p>I never expect not to become one of these laborers. I never think of them as servants or invisible people. On the contrary, they are forming the backbone of some of the stuff that we never see consciously. These systems become invisible because of their hard work. They deserve a lot of respect.<p>Trash is picked up 1am in my street. I always think of the man, their families and circumstances.<p>Similarly, when I go to a carrier&#x27;s office, I try to prepare all my answers and papers before to take their time less (they&#x27;re literally drowning in packages to ship, so if I&#x27;m faster, they can finish their other work faster). I actively try not to reflect myself onto them since they see hundreds of people every day. I try not to be one of the people which makes them feel like a useless&#x2F;replaceable cogs.<p>Just because I can play with a lot of cutting edge servers, not sweat everyday and wear my body down to earn a bit of money, it doesn&#x27;t mean that I can not or don&#x27;t want to understand and romanticize the burden they&#x27;re going through.<p>Similarly just because I have a nicer job, it doesn&#x27;t mean that all my friends or people I know <i>and care</i> are in the same circle or in higher places.<p>Just because I know how to manage an OS and write high performance code, it doesn&#x27;t mean I live isolated in my high castle.')
