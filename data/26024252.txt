Item(by='habosa', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612434541, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26016505, text='This is a really short timeline and a huge pain. I maintain 3 popular (thousands of github stars) Android libraries hosted on JCenter and published via Bintray. These libraries are stable and I update them only a few times a year.<p>Now I have 4 months to re-do the publishing pipeline for them <i>and</i> figure out how to republish older version artifacts on MavenCentral <i>and</i> educate my users.<p>I think few who haven&#x27;t tried it realize what a <i>massive</i> pain it is to publish a library on Android. Compared to Cocoapods or NPM, Android library ecosystem is a joke. Every library I know of has their own hacked-together build script. This is why most Android apps use only libraries from Google and Square, the two dominant library publishers.<p>There was never much love between JFrog and the Android community so this puts an end to that saga, but it&#x27;s not clear who will step up. Bintray &#x2F; JCenter was never great but it did attempt to make publishing easier and it was the default.')
