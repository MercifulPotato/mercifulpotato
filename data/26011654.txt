Item(by='fooblat', descendants=None, kids=[26013452, 26012285, 26011871], score=None, time=1612340091, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26011391, text='For me the difference is stark.<p>At my last three companies it worked like this:<p>Product Manager is responsible for the product strategy and feature roadmap. They spend most of their focus on internal and external customers. They report up through Product leadership.<p>Product Owner is part of the engineering squad. They spend most of their focus on the engineers building the product. The PO works on refinement, prioritizes the backlog, runs the sprint planning and retros, and is the one who can decide if a sprint should be broken. They also keep stakeholders up to date on the progress of the squad. They report up through Technical leadership.<p>In my experience it is a rare person who can do both of these at the same time.')
