Item(by='subjectsigma', descendants=None, kids=[26005764], score=None, time=1612288920, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002130, text='Vtubers and even Western streamers do exactly this, I never stopped to imagine there was a word for it and I never made the connection to wrestling.<p>I&#x27;m only really familiar with Hololive, but their streamers exist in this very strange state of pseudo-acting. They have an avatar and a theme associated with that avatar, but there&#x27;s absolutely no way they can be expected to stay true to a script when they&#x27;re doing hour long streams (Korone has streamed for 26 hours straight before, only stopping for bathroom breaks). So a lot of their individual personalities shine through in what they say and do. The better the streamer can mix their own real-life personality with the character, the more engaging the streamer.<p>Probably the best examples of this are Kiryu Coco and Houshou Marine. Coco has had both real life and scripted controversies and it&#x27;s sometimes hard to tell which is which. She is supposedly Japanese but speaks English with a southern accent. With Marine it has become a running joke that her character is supposed to be 17 when it is blatantly obvious that she is in her late twenties &#x2F; early thirties. Additionally, she plays the role of a playful and devious pervert, but has so many very strange and sometimes raw stories about pornography and sexual experiences it&#x27;s really hard for me to believe that it is all scripted; it seems more like someone got to know her really well and wrote a fantasy character around her real personality.<p>Overall I find the more genuine streamers (Like Amelia, who barely plays a character at all) much more entertaining, but there is a real sense of playful mystery when people&#x27;s real lives get onscreen alongside their character.')
