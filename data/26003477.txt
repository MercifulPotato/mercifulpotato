Item(by='lmilcin', descendants=None, kids=[26003974], score=None, time=1612285325, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002778, text='I have ones that are 19 years old (Beyerdynamic).<p>The materials for the main part of headphones would last probably indefinitely if kept in dark, dry place, undisturbed.<p>On the other hand the foam that is part of the pads lasts months to maybe couple of years depending on use.<p>If you bought ones with leather it will last for many decades <i>IF</i> you know how to take care of leather.<p>If you avoid throwing, dropping or otherwise banging headphones on things, don&#x27;t come outside in them and don&#x27;t stretch the headband more than is absolutely necessary to put on your head I estimate they can be maintained practically forever (definitely more than user&#x27;s lifetime).<p>Maintanence:<p>- keep them clean with dry cloth,<p>- use something to clean&#x2F;protect leather from drying out,<p>- replace pads maybe every year or two,<p>- replace pads on headband maybe once every 10 years,<p>- disassemble completely to remove hair and other detritus, probably every 5 years<p>- fix the cable that tends to break after prolonged use, I am gentle so maybe once in 7 years.<p>This is history of maintenance of my headphones which I use every day, entire day.')
