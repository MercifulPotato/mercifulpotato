Item(by='nanis', descendants=None, kids=[26013113, 26012380], score=None, time=1612347083, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012094, text='&gt; It might also be true that neither the buyer not the seller, in any transaction, are happy about it as such.<p>&quot;Happy&quot; here is used in a very specific sense: If two parties engage in a transaction without coercion, it must be because they are both made better off by the transaction or they would both be less happy without the transaction.<p>This is where &quot;gains from trade&quot; come from.<p>The ability to make this statement disappears the moment either or both parties are required to participate in the transaction or are required to engage in different transaction.<p>The argument of the grandparent is that just like in any transaction, there are two parties to an option being traded at a given price. One party believes it&#x27;s a good deal because they think the price they paid is low enough to accept the risk-reward balance and the other part believes the price they received is high enough for the certain payoff to cover the reward-risk balance they are giving up.<p>They might both be right because the reward you seek and the risk you can bear is a function of your current endowment in human and financial capital.')
