Item(by='searine', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612374810, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014998, text='It should be noted that the &quot;errors&quot; in this case are gaps in sequence. Sometimes the DNA strand slips through the pore and some bases aren&#x27;t called.<p>The actual base calling is on par with Hi-seq in my experience. In software terms, you are missing chunks of code, but aren&#x27;t flipping bits.<p>This is important because in certain experiments, you care less about those gaps (scaffolding for example). So you can get a lot of cheap utility out of nanopore sequencing.')
