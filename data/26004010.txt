Mid summer is quiet and winter has none.<p>This relates to the times at which a queen can mate - there are no male drones in winter (the females kicked them out!) and so any queen that hatched would be a virgin, and couldn’t lay.<p>Sometimes swarms will occur due to bees absconding to get away from\nHigh levels of disease.
