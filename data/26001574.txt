Item(by='sandworm101', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612277527, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001503, text='Well, I do credit this guy for doing an experiment that isn&#x27;t in the cloud.  So much of physics these days is about detecting a signal out of some giant noisy data set, or divining some truth out of a massive mathematical problem.  This guy was looking for a measurable force.  Spin a big diamond, throw some liquid helium on it, hit it with a magnetic hammer, and if your theory is correct it floats off the table.  The yes&#x2F;no answer is plain to see.  That&#x27;s a fundamentally <i>physical</i> experiment reminiscing of those done in centuries past.')
