Item(by='jcranmer', descendants=None, kids=[26005837, 26005789], score=None, time=1612294842, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005041, text='They&#x27;re not completely capricious. Laws don&#x27;t operate under the confines of a strict propositional calculus: you don&#x27;t follow strict axiomatic derivations to a contradiction and declare yourself finished. Instead, it operates generally on a basis that cases with facts in common should have the same decision (hence the name &quot;common law&quot;). And the law is written with this process in mind: if legislators don&#x27;t like the judicial interpretation of their text, <i>they can change it by changing the law</i>.')
