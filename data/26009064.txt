Item(by='Layke1123', descendants=None, kids=[26009191], score=None, time=1612312044, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26009014, text='Free will does not exist, so telling people they have a form of free will called &quot;making choices&quot; is a misdirection. You aren&#x27;t actively making choices even if you feel like you are. You are only reacting to the stimulus you receive from the environment. Change the stimulus, and the behavior changes.<p>Your definition of free will is useless because it doesn&#x27;t accurately reflect the reality. I make what &quot;feels&quot; like a choice, but it isn&#x27;t really a choice.<p>This kind of reasoning actually does lead to better consequences, not worse.')
