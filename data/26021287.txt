Nothing short of starting to read the book can do that.<p>Interesting. I found that when selecting books, it&#x27;s helpful for me to see how people have tagged them. That way I avoid some of the cliches I really don&#x27;t care for and can narrow focus a bit. It&#x27;s unusual for that to be wrong for me, where I actually like one of the books I had initially excluded based on category.<p>For example, if you really aren&#x27;t into steampunk noir, I think it&#x27;s helpful to know that the book you&#x27;re about to read is a steampunk noir. Same goes for space opera, litrpg, gun porn, hard sci fi, hack and slash, harem stories, overpowered heroes, etc.<p>When you&#x27;ve got the best version of something you generally dislike, you&#x27;ll see that in reviews.')
