I prefer right-handed, but for some reason most of computer graphics seem to have adopted left-handed.<p>That was the opposite of fun when I was noodling with writing an OpenGL-based rendering engine for a &quot;fly a spaceship around a 3D maze&quot; game, decade and a half ago.<p>As to why I prefer right-handed? The lin-alg textbook I had at uni uses a right-handed coordinate system for what it calls &quot;the perceivable room&quot; (yes, it is &quot;the book with the cow on&quot;, for those in the know).')
