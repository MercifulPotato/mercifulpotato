Item(by='speedgoose', descendants=None, kids=[25998551], score=None, time=1612251474, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25998050, text='It very likely asks the location permission because it wants to scan Bluetooth devices. Scanning Bluetooth devices can be useful to detect the speaker, but it&#x27;s also often used to track user locations especially indoor where the GPS doesn&#x27;t work or is not precise enough.<p>You can place Bluetooth emitter beacons around an area and by scanning the Bluetooth devices on the smartphone and some triangulation, you know precisely where the smartphone is.<p>By the way even Apple, which is not that privacy friendly, sells such Bluetooth beacons to track peoples&#x27; location. <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.m.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;IBeacon" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.m.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;IBeacon</a><p>This technology can have good use too, in retirement homes or hospitals for example. But it&#x27;s mostly used to know where you are in a mall.')
