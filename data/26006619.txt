Item(by='brucehoult', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612299895, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004938, text='There is plenty of evidence (rather than opinion) that with a decent compiler and hardware implementation the more complex addressing modes don&#x27;t gain you anything worth the bother in either code size or performance.<p>RISC-V is extensible. If someone really believes that more complex addressing modes are worth it then they can add them as a custom extension. If their chips work out to have better performance&#x2F;price, performance&#x2F;area, performance&#x2F;energy then everyone else will want to copy them and those more complex addressing modes will become a standard extension for RISC-V.<p>There is no sign of the RISC-V community (as opposed to ARM or x86 fans who aren&#x27;t using RISC-V anyway) moving in that direction.')
