Item(by='IfOnlyYouKnew', descendants=None, kids=[26004185, 26002468], score=None, time=1612280549, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000611, text='This is a relatively minor event. For most any actual Wikipedia author, nothing will change. That&#x27;s because these global rules merely codify a minimum standard that any of the top-50 or more wikimedia sites have long had on the books.<p>The purpose is to expand these rules to some of the small properties (Gaelic Wikinews or whatever) where they lack due to omission: admin teams are small, resources scarce, etc. No community has ever decided &quot;no, we believe harassing women is great&quot; (is there a gop.wikisomething.org?). The just haven&#x27;t gotten around to putting it in writing.<p>It&#x27;ll also make it easier to work across projects, because you won&#x27;t have to familiarise yourself with the nuances of local rules in easy cases. Sort-of like the EU&#x27;s harmonisation of customer rights across jurisdictions makes it easier to order from France or Italy.<p>But, mostly, a non-event. I&#x27;m sure there&#x27;ll be hot debates and open letters and then everyone will forget about it.')
