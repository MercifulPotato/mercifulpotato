Item(by='wtallis', descendants=None, kids=[26021079], score=None, time=1612394252, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26019590, text='SSDs never shrink the reported capacity in response to defective&#x2F;worn-out blocks. Instead, they have spare area that is not accessible to the user. The SMART data reported by the drive includes an indicator of how much spare area has been used and how much remains. When the available spare area starts dropping rapidly, you&#x27;re approaching the drive&#x27;s end of life.')
