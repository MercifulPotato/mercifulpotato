Item(by='hinkley', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612297374, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25990561, text='My favorite example of this is image sharing for large extended families.<p>If most people thought about it, they don&#x27;t actually want pictures of their kids all over Instagram, but it&#x27;s a way to distribute them and, after a fashion, back them up. We don&#x27;t have to worry about the matriarch&#x27;s house burning down and taking out our photo archive with it. Or her clicking on a malware link and wiping it all out.<p>If they had a piece of software that wasn&#x27;t designed for building a startup (deeply technical, highly fiddly), Uncle Bob and you and your nephew Timmy could host geographically redundant copies of the data that the group has access to.')
