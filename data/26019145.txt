I did not consider the &#x27;burn out&#x27; of donors before, for instance (always thinking from the perspective of the dev team).<p>&gt; Put another way, we each get to say, &quot;I&#x27;m not willing to sacrifice all I can while everyone else freerides… but I am willing to be part of the crowd and part of the movement, if enough others help too!&quot;<p>&gt; It is also low risk: if very few patrons join a project, very little resources will be spent.
