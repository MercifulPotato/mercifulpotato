Item(by='richeyryan', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612352917, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008970, text='I&#x27;m kind of resigned to it. There&#x27;s places where I imagine I&#x27;ll end up using it and others where I&#x27;ll do without.<p>Someone who talks intelligently about the strengths of dynamic languages is Rich Hickey. His talks challenge some of the ideas where static typing is superior to dynamic typing. He is arguably biased in his position but it makes for a refreshing contrast to the static types argument.<p>Clojure is a great example of the qualities required to make dynamic typing really powerful, the main one being the large library of functions that can operate over all collections. This reduces the amount of custom code you write for data munging, reducing surface area for bugs.<p>The complete counterpoint to that is Go with no utilities for doing common data transformations, forcing users to re-implement similar logic repeatedly. It may as well be statically typed at that stage to avoid mistakes from writing the same code over and over.<p>The more powerful statically typed languages with generics then fall somewhere in the middle where you don&#x27;t have to repeat yourself as much.')
