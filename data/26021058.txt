In other states with popular initiatives, the People pass laws on the same footing as the Legislature; they just do so through the ballot box.  The elected Legislature still has the authority to modify those same laws (though most states require a &quot;cooling off&quot; period of a year or two before a passed initiative can be modified, and even then a supermajority in the Legislature can still do it).<p>A popular initiative being sacrosanct and inviolable absent a second initiative seems wrong to me.
