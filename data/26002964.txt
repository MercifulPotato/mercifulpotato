Item(by='mondoshawan', descendants=None, kids=[26003159], score=None, time=1612283268, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002210, text='Xorg didnt define policy, which is why things like window management was offloaded to the window manager process. Look and feel was also not part of the policy of Xorg, and was defined by the widget sets, window manager, etc.<p>In the wayland model, these previously modular components that communicated through process boundaries to cobble together those policies that make a desktop are typically subsumed into <i>one</i> process called the compositor. Look and feel is still the domain of the widget set, but window policy is not.')
