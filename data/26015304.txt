Item(by='throwaway0a5e', descendants=None, kids=[26015534, 26015514], score=None, time=1612369856, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26015209, text='I&#x27;m really sick about this whattaboutism that every internet discussion about electric cars invariably devolves into at least in part.<p>Fire requires fuel, oxygen and ignition (heat). Liquid fuels require external ignition. Batteries do not. But you also have to pump liquid fuels around and generally handle them more to make them do work.<p>Batteries are more dangerous on a fundamental&#x2F;conceptual level because they have everything they need to release their energy all at once. So we build them with hard casings for the same reason we build large safety factors into pressurized gas containers so in practice it doesn&#x27;t really matter.<p>Batteries and liquid fuels aren&#x27;t really better or worse than each other from a &quot;much safety or &quot;won&#x27;t somebody think of the children&quot; point of view. They just require slightly different engineering considerations for an equivalent level of safety.<p>This is directed just as much (ore maybe even more) at the parent comment as it is at you.')
