Item(by='majewsky', descendants=None, kids=[26022126], score=None, time=1612394177, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26019722, text='These are two entirely different concepts: `any` is a compile-time construct, `interface{}` is a run-time construct. At any specific callsite, `any` resolves into one specific type that gets passed at that specific callsite. Buy `interface{}` can hold values of different types at the same callsite.')
