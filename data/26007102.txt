Second, the monopolist must have used its power in a prohibited way.<p><a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;United_States_antitrust_law" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;United_States_antitrust_law</a><p>I don&#x27;t think Google meets the second requirement.
