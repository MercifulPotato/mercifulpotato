Item(by='AshamedCaptain', descendants=None, kids=[26000507, 25999833], score=None, time=1612265550, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999676, text='&gt; [Xorg] limitation is, that all screens have to have same DPI.<p>The limitation is not true. Yes, the core protocol gives you a single, global DPI value. However, who cares about the core protocol? You have XRandR, which gives you a per-screen DPI. Alternative, you could have a freedesktop standard&#x2F;convention where the DPI is stored as a global property of the root window, in a array of values, one for each display, so that you can override them.<p>Problem solved! I am actually running Gtk+ slightly patched to support that... trouble is, most of the toolkit is hardcoded to assume a fixed display or even process-wide global DPI, or at least it was when I last looked at it. Dunno what they did for wayland.<p>I really don&#x27;t understand why we need to throw away an entire 30 years of software development just because the current property for storing the DPI only has space for 1 value instead of 1 array of values, one per monitor. Just use a new property.')
