Item(by='Daho0n', descendants=None, kids=[25999924, 25999767, 26004919], score=None, time=1612262981, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25998699, text='&gt;Since 1995 about a billion people have been lifted out of extreme poverty<p>That is a great soundbite for the news but is it actually as great as it sounds? If we take it as fact that &quot;one billion humans have been lifted out of extreme poverty&quot; what about how many are <i>in</i> extreme poverty? Note that how many is outside extreme poverty doesn&#x27;t say anything about the amount in extreme poverty, just that more are now above it. The world population grew by 1.6 billion between 1990 and 2010 according to the UN. So on one side we have the &quot;one billion have been lifted out of extreme poverty&quot; but on the other we have &quot;1.6 billion more humans&quot; with the greatest growth in poverty stricken countries like Nigeria. I don&#x27;t know the numbers but I don&#x27;t believe that the &quot;about a billion people have been lifted out of extreme poverty&quot; is the whole truth since AFAIK the <i>amount in extreme poverty have grown</i>.<p>EDIT: \nLooking up the numbers it hasn&#x27;t &quot;grown&quot; but &quot;extreme poverty&quot; is seen as &quot;statically less than 1.9 international dollars per day&quot;. Is it a lift to $3 or $30? $3 is still living in squalor and destitution if you ask most people in the West I&#x27;m sure. IMO extreme poverty haven&#x27;t declined outside statistics.')
