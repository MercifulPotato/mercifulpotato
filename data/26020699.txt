Item(by='marsdentech', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612395342, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26020312, text='A single flow cell contains a few thousand pores (I think this is what you mean by &quot;holes&quot;) that are all at different stages of passing different molecules, with signal data being captured from a few hundred at any given time. In practice you&#x27;d never expect (nor could you arrange) for two pores to be at the same stage of processing the same (or any pre-determined) molecule at the same time, so correlation information like that is out. The &quot;clock rate&quot; is determined by the so-called motor protein that &quot;pulls&quot; the nucleic acid molecule through the pore, if you fancy going down the reading rabbit-hole...')
