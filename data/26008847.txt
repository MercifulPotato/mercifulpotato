In some cases that money could have helped the company land, in others it was going towards something doomed and possibly fraudulent, but either way it doesn’t come from the companies coffers directly unless the company does a buyback from the shorts. The people losing money are other investors.<p>What if smart money had to bet on a competitor or growing some other investment instead?')
