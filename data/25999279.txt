Item(by='roenxi', descendants=None, kids=[25999560], score=None, time=1612261607, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999071, text='Wayland represents an interesting and possibly classic software situation - a beautiful standard has been developed. It blocks the user from using a screenshot application in the name of security.<p>Now when the standard gets rolled out  kludge will be developed to allow users to still take screenshots. The beauty of the standard will be compromised or everyone will spend a bunch of time pretending the kludge isn&#x27;t de-facto part of the standard.<p>Wayland&#x27;s model of user security was a mistake if the goal was getting typical users to use it. It isn&#x27;t secure, it just means screenshots will be done with a kludge. I think literally everyone I know who owns a computing device (I&#x27;m counting phones and tablets) wants to be able to take screen captures to show people things.')
