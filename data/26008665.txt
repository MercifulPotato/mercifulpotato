Item(by='yters', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612309250, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007755, text='Programming is concrete.  Programmers have to reliably determine if their code will halt or not to make functioning software.<p>Mathematics is concrete and mathematicians have to reliably create new consistent axioms to expand our mathematical knowledge.<p>Notice that the fact you too are subject to Gödel&#x27;s theorem does not entail you are a Turing machine.  There is an oracle hierarchy where each level has its own halting problem and a higher level oracle.  This does not cause the hierarchy to collapse.  So even though humans have their own halting problem does not entail they are reducible to Turing machines.<p>It is a subtle point of logic, but one I see many people miss, even philosophers and computer scientists who attempt to refute oracle arguments.')
