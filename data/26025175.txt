Item(by='Twisol', descendants=None, kids=[26025211], score=None, time=1612445793, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26025148, text='&gt; The things which are instances of Shape are functions that accept circles or rectangles, not actual circles or rectangles.<p>The naming <i>is</i> confusing, but it&#x27;s misled you in the opposite direction. Things that are instances of Shape are functions that accept handlers of circles or rectangles. The handlers, unfortunately, are named `circle` and `rectangle`. I would prefer `onCircle` and `onRectangle` in this context, because we&#x27;re lacking the context of a true `match` expression to disambiguate the naming.<p><pre><code>    type Shape = &lt;T&gt;(handlers: {\n      onCircle: (args: Circle) =&gt; T;\n      onRectangle: (args: Rectangle) =&gt; T;\n    }) =&gt; T;\n</code></pre>\n&gt; Also, you seem to have created functions and types with the same names (Circle and Rectangle) which seems dangerous.<p>I think this is an idiom for defining a canonical constructor for a type. It&#x27;s a little funky here because they&#x27;re returning Shape, not Circle or Rectangle, and the latter are not subtypes of Shape. But it mostly tracks alongside the rest of the encoding.')
