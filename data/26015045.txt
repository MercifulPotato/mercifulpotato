Item(by='Closi', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612368822, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014537, text='I work in logistics consulting - it’s pretty common to receive orderline data that is a few hundred thousand rows and excel is great at analysing it (eg to build a material flow).<p>Excel for this is significantly faster and more flexible than any other tool I’ve used.<p>Also transport data and models can often use many columns. Google Docs limit is actually the active cells used so this then reduces the row limit further.<p>If we receive data that is a few million rows on bigger formats, we will still use excel, just with PowerQuery &#x2F; PowerPivot and using measures which works pretty well up to 10 million records or so.<p>Bigger than that and we will use PowerBI.')
