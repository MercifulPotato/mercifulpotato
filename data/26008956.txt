We had Yahoo&#x27;s search engine at the time and worked well enough. And to a lesser extent Lycos&#x2F;Hotbot&#x2F;Inktomi&#x2F;DMOZ. The pre-Google web was pretty interesting and a bit more sophisticated than gets credit today. I also feel you were less likely to discover retailers on the web randomly. You&#x27;d more than likely hear about them first on the news or from friends and go from there. I don&#x27;t think it was common to just discover some random e-tailer like we do today, put in our credit card, and expect the package in a couple days. You learned about Amazon from 60 minutes or your friend who subscribed to Wired magazine or the guy at the software store, not from a search engine.<p>I think the whole pets.com and travel.com is just the usual marketing logic at work and not really related to the limitation of search engines. Its just shady marketing tricks, a bit like how we see the .biz and other gimmicky tlds today.')
