Item(by='toomuchtodo', descendants=None, kids=[26005077, 26004884], score=None, time=1612288848, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004201, text='This is not a call for fatalism. This is a call for trust infrastructure, trust anchors, and proven underlying identity and trust mechanisms.<p>What happens when you try to fake being an Estonian who holds a national ID card that utilizes cryptographic primitives? Along those same lines, this infrastructure is the very same needed for business to transact (think document&#x2F;agreement execution, bank accounts, brokerage accounts, payment processing, real estate ownership record systems, etc).<p>If I can&#x27;t prove who I am, that&#x27;s a gap to be solved for by government (and many have already solved for this, it is a well worn path [1]). If you require me to attest to my identity, that&#x27;s a regulatory, governance, and oversight issue. As glib as is sounds, fight misinformation&#x2F;disinformation with trust (infra). Make trust the default, not the exception.<p>EDIT: Login.gov [2] provides authentication services for DHS&#x27; Global Entry. Why can we not use that to attest identity facts elsewhere? Why can&#x27;t any citizen get a CAC [3] to use with this system? (I frequently see Login.gov is hiring for SREs, but no internal advocates&#x2F;champions; why?) Why can I pay with Apple Pay but can&#x27;t prove my identity without a paper birth certificate and social security card (or a passport if you&#x27;re among the well heeled)?<p>&lt;&#x2F;soapbox&gt;<p>[1] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;List_of_national_identity_card_policies_by_country" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;List_of_national_identity_card...</a> [2] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;login.gov" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;login.gov</a> [3] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.cac.mil&#x2F;common-access-card&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.cac.mil&#x2F;common-access-card&#x2F;</a>')
