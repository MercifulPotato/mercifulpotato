Item(by='AdmiralAsshat', descendants=None, kids=[26003819, 26003547, 26003515], score=None, time=1612285001, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002335, text='I always thought about writing a GNU&#x2F;POSIX utility called &#x27;pp&#x27; (prepend), that would simply take an input file and insert it above the first line of a target file. So, essentially, if I have a CSV file without a header and want to quickly insert the header file, I would run:<p><pre><code>  pp header.txt spreadsheet.csv\n</code></pre>\nAnd it would be the equivalent of:<p><pre><code>  cat header.txt spreadsheet.csv &gt; temp\n  mv temp spreadsheet.csv\n</code></pre>\nBut also support all of the expected POSIX niceties, reading from stdin, etc.<p>But I never got around to it, and it&#x27;s been a few years since I had to use C at my day-job (which was never great to begin with), so it fell to the wayside.')
