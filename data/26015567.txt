Mass Effect is fairly soft sci-fi and more of classic space opera.<p>All these things happen in Mass Effect:<p>Sounds in space, spaceships bunched way too close together, impossibly slow projectiles for space (especially the classic &quot;blob of light&#x2F;plasma thingy&quot; traveling at what appears to be subsonic speeds), giant 18th-century infantry-style spaceship firing lines, in general heavy reliance on &quot;spaceships as just extensions of naval warfare&quot; trope, all alien communication neatly glossed over with universal translation, nearly universally bipedal humanoid aliens, etc.<p>None of these are bad things! Mass Effect is simply much more similar in tradition to something like Star Wars or Star Trek than the Expanse.')
