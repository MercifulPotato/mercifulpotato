Item(by='mdip', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612398258, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018551, text='The &quot;Teen Dance Club&quot; was one of those good ideas &quot;in theory&quot; that blows up during execution.  As a kid, we had a suburban business that provided exactly that.  Unfortunately, many of the parents of children who you&#x27;d <i>want</i> to have patronizing your Teen Dance Club are parents who won&#x27;t allow their children to hang out at any place that has the appearance of being a &quot;club&quot;.   This leaves the rest of the kids.  And those kids cost more money to serve than they provide in profits.<p>This club did not serve alcohol, but from personal experience, alcohol&#x2F;weed were not hard to come by.  People would throw shots of hard liquor into the $5.00 cokes, there were regular &quot;fights&quot; usually originating from some barely-past-puberty-knob deciding some other kid was looking in the general direction of the female to which said knob was attracted.<p>This is a problem at most businesses catering to teenagers, unfortunately.  But it&#x27;s more of a problem at certain kinds of businesses.  We have three skating rinks within about 20 miles (all were closed and not sure all survived).  One of them was around when I was a kid and there were occasional problems, then, but really nothing compared to what I saw at one of the two &quot;Teen Clubs&quot; I visited.  I think part of it is that rinks (roller&#x2F;ice) cater to families <i>and</i> teenagers.  I have 4 kids.  If we&#x27;re out and some teenager is misbehaving, the words &quot;knock it off&quot; have been known to fly out of my mouth with immediate effect.  It doesn&#x27;t matter that there&#x27;s a large number of unsupervised teenagers, the number of parents -- especially ones that aren&#x27;t uncomfortable stepping into things -- offsets the grief the &quot;bad teens&quot; can create for everyone else.<p>I hear ya, though.  We used to hang out at malls, rinks, hell we had a whole abandoned stadium that we could roller blade on for $5 on the weekends[0].  Now the few malls that remain are outdoors, which is awesome when it&#x27;s 16 outside... it&#x27;s as if they&#x27;re <i>trying</i> to get me to buy everything online.  We have a laser-tag place that feeds you and let you play unlimited from 8:00-midnight for $20[1] and a few others, but they&#x27;re all closed for the moment.  It is <i>awful</i> for kids right now.<p>[0] The Pontiac SilverDome actually rented out skates&#x2F;inlines and let you roller blade around the interior walkway.  Even when it was <i>busy</i> it was hard to tell because of the wide walkways designed to serve depressed Lion&#x27;s fans.<p>[1] Oddly it was owned by a local (Christian) cult run by a chiropractor; I took my kids there nearly every weekend of the winter for two years and <i>everyone</i> who patronized the place, teenagers, kids and adults, all enjoying themselves and never an issue.  The only hint about the religion of the owners was that they played Christian Pop&#x2F;Rock over the speakers -- makes me wonder if blasting Jesus music calms teenagers? :)')
