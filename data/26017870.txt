It&#x27;s not going from 14 to 1 <i>only</i> because of laws against further consolidation in the space. Regulators would simply deny their request to consolidate further. Stopping at 14 isn&#x27;t some magical constant or some natural end state.<p>My point is that the natural direction in each of these markets is consolidation, then at a point it hits a dead stop.
