Most of the valuable packages in Python in fact C++ projects (like Tensorflow, Pandas, etc.).  When you are talking about Python dependency management you are talking about the Cartesian product of the packages you are depending on, their dependency in terms of compilers and header files. It is not hard to run into missing .h files while trying to install these dependencies. This situation was improved significantly a while back with the WHL files.<p>Anyways this whole article does not make too much sense, I am using Python for 10 years professionally and never run into the concept of nested venvs let alone somebody trying to use that in production.')
