Item(by='danans', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612329331, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004272, text='A couple years ago I relocated a piece of driftwood from my small backyard to the front yard because a carpenter bee couple had taken up residence in it and the male was routinely dive-bombing us in the backyard (they don&#x27;t sting, but are just aggressive).<p>I was amazed to see that the male kept returning to the same spot for what seemed like weeks looking for its partner in the now relocated piece of driftwood.  I naively had expected it to track down the piece of driftwood just 40 ft away in the front yard, and it was kind of heartbreaking to see it searching for it in desperation.')
