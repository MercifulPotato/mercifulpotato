Item(by='dekhn', descendants=None, kids=[26021618], score=None, time=1612390619, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26019162, text='it didn&#x27;t turn out to be harder than <i>I</i> thought it was going to be.  I came into this in the 90s fully prepared for the idea of polygenic risk.  In my opinion, most people who did molecular biology first think that way, while most people who learned mendelian genetics don&#x27;t.<p>I had my genome sequenced a few years ago by Illumina.  They had a big slick presentation, blah blah blah, ApoE1, etc.  When the genetic counsellors came to my genome they said &quot;huh.  you don&#x27;t have any risk factors&quot;.  I checked and each of their risks was from an existing gene panel, so the WGS wasn&#x27;t valuable (it&#x27;s on PGP, if you want to work with it <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;my.pgp-hms.org&#x2F;profile&#x2F;hu80855C" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;my.pgp-hms.org&#x2F;profile&#x2F;hu80855C</a>).<p>I talked in more detail with the counsellors.  Turns out, whenever they saw a novel variant that wasn&#x27;t covered by a gene panel they were googling the variant and skimming the abstracts of papers.<p>It was at that point I realized the difference between research, PR, and actionable medical data.')
