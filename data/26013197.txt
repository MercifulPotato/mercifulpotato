Item(by='sighingnow', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612356999, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012868, text='GraphScope addresses on computations on extermely large graphs, e.g., the friendship networks of all facebook users, the hyperlink relations between all webpages around the world, and so on.<p>Typically the graph may have billions of nodes and 10x billions of edges. Obviously the graph data cannot be fit into a single machine to run alogrithms like SSSP or pagerank. And a single machine usually doesn&#x27;t have enough cores for the computation, e.g., an interactive query couldn&#x27;t return within milliseconds. That why we need distributed graph processing system for such big graphs.')
