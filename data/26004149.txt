I am thoroughly convinced that if he weren&#x27;t currently jobless and lacking bonus money, he would&#x27;ve bought GME at 300, thinking it was going to the moon.<p>I think the core issue is, there is a significant number of people who only get their news from what is upvoted on Reddit, and what is upvoted on Reddit not only has no correlation with actuality, factuality, or reality, it also follows a lot of really nasty biases due to the upvote system.
