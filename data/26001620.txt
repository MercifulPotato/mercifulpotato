Item(by='quotemstr', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612277751, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001345, text='So we should endure technical mediocrity forever because we couldn&#x27;t get our act together socially?<p>There is a way out. I&#x27;ve previously proposed on LKML that the Linux kernel team provide an official userspace system call library that sits below libc and that all libc implementations would share. We&#x27;d forbid new system calls being called except through this library. Optionally, we&#x27;d enforce this constraint on all system calls.<p>This is how Fuchsia works, by the way: all Fuchsia system calls must go through one giant vDSO.')
