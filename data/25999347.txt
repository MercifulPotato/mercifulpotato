Item(by='glenstein', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612262129, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25997583, text='This isn&#x27;t just &quot;gee, technology sure comes with tradeoffs.&quot; It was literally claiming the dollar to be worthless, a global class war, and for most of the continent of Africa to be uninhabitable. It was claiming all three would happen.<p>I feel like people come into these questions wanting to treat them like trick questions, and wanting to show off that they can&#x27;t be tricked. And so, if a bet like this has an obvious answer, well, maybe that&#x27;s a trick! And you aren&#x27;t fooled because, hey, technology has tradeoffs, and global warming is real. Therefore the guy predicting Africa would be uninhabitable was right!<p>There&#x27;s a satisfying quality of switcheroo. But it&#x27;s also nuts because it&#x27;s not a trick question, and those observations are true but irrelevant to assessing the terms of the bet.')
