Comparing it to Netflix&#x2F;Blockbuster? What?<p>Stadia is currently on the verge of flopping and we&#x27;re not even sure where other streaming services may go. Streaming video games are literally constrained by the laws of physics and the need for extremely responsive user input. It&#x27;s exciting but it&#x27;s also a whole different engineering problem than what Netflix solved in order to edge out their advantage over Blockbuster.<p>Steam, the Playstation&#x2F;Xbox Stores are the correct comparisons. That said, I doubt Gamestop has been struggling these last few years simply because there has been a movement towards digital downloads.<p>People seem to forget that these digital stores have existed for the longest time and are not at all new innovation. Take a look at the sentiment towards digital only consoles - overwhelmingly negative. Newer AAA games require so much storage space that the progression of networking bandwidth aren&#x27;t keeping up with the demands at all.<p>I&#x27;m not going to speculate where GameStop is headed because they haven&#x27;t announced those changes yet. However, the speculation of evaluating their future based on digital downloads or the Blockbuster story is absolutely foolish.')
