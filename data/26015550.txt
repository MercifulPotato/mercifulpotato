Item(by='anjc', descendants=None, kids=[26024535], score=None, time=1612370914, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014982, text='Do you always need to trust something in the process, for every product? You have a secure trustless mechanism for consensus, and can assess counterparty risk for a given product by auditing the smart contract (e.g. for Compound, borrowers have to deposit collateral).<p>A simple business case might be interest earned by lending. Could you use traditional banking for this? Can any lay person lend? Is traditional banking accessible to anybody in the entire world with just an Ethereum wallet? This is just the beginning of defi and deposits have gone from ~0 to $30B in a year. Not much, in the grand scheme, but there&#x27;s clearly demand for the products.<p>No doubt this market will be regulated in due course, but I think this bell can&#x27;t be un-rung.')
