Item(by='danaliv', descendants=None, kids=[26003996, 26005875], score=None, time=1612287144, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002908, text='I understand where this thinking comes from but I still think it&#x27;s kind of a simplistic attitude. People depend on the technology they use. When you convince someone to rely on something you&#x27;ve produced (and, crucially, continue working on), I think you do actually create a duty to be responsive to them. They depend on your work. Are we really so limited in our ethical thinking that we require an exchange of money to create obligation?<p>I understand that people can be aggressive, rude, and downright hostile. I&#x27;m not suggesting that such behavior be rewarded. Nor am I suggesting that every open source project has to do everything any user says. I do, however, object to the notion that there is <i>no obligation whatsoever</i> between the producer of a technology and the users of that technology, regardless of whether money has changed hands.')
