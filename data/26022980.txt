Item(by='Const-me', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612417394, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26019151, text='&gt; there are far better ways to handle them<p>I did some research a year ago. Found a few alternatives, yet none of them was good enough. Here&#x27;s the issues I remember.<p>1. It&#x27;s complicated. Cubic segments can self-intersect or contain singularities.<p>2. Stroked curves are used a lot. To build stroke edges from the curve, need to offset the curve by half width of the stroke. When you offset a polyline you gonna get another polyline. Yet the offset of Bezier splines is not generally representable as another Bezier spline.<p>3. In some use cases, hardware-implemented MSAA is the best way of AA. Polygon pipeline gives you that for free. Producing SV_Coverage in pixel shaders to achieve same effect for mid.points of triangle is hard and inefficient.<p>4. Games have been pushing GPUs for high polygon count for couple decades now, GPUs became really good at that. Also GPUs have early Z rejection that drops pixels or even larger blocks before pixel shader stage. You can&#x27;t have that if you sending large primitives and computing curves in pixel shaders. Polygon pipeline is not necessarily slower.')
