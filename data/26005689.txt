Item(by='dathinab', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612295532, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001972, text='RISC-V is targeting embedded customized chips and research and does quite well in that area.<p>In that area sometimes having easier design is much more worth then putting certain optimizations into the ISA spec.<p>But from what _I_ know RSIC-V is neither targeting nor suited for general purpose computing, like general purpose servers, desktops and any phone&#x2F;tablet but low end ones.<p>The reasons for this are manifold, including manny thinks I can&#x27;t assess and thinks like that for such targets you like want a bunch of changes. Like e.g. requiring support of one load between a LR&#x2F;SC pair which might be required to be based on the LR.<p>But then if it would have targeted general purpose computing platforms I believe it would have failed.<p>The fact that RISC-V allows extremely simple implementations is what makes it successful I guess.<p>So they just need to bring out a tweaked RISC-V spec which requires more ISA extensions, more support for LR&#x2F;SC and similar and maybe does also some ISA changes  and voila we it can move into that space, likely reusing a good amount of tooling.')
