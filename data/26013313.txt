Item(by='onion2k', descendants=None, kids=[26013344], score=None, time=1612358198, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26013296, text='The frontpage of HN seems to be a sort of &#x27;holy grail&#x27; for some HN readers, but you always have to remember that it&#x27;s only a good thing if &quot;HN readers&quot; are your potential customers. If they&#x27;re not then it&#x27;s just a distracting spike in traffic that will do very little for your sales. And HN readers are a pretty diverse bunch (geographically, financially, and as far as interests go) so it&#x27;s unlikely that many of us are likely to be buying unless your product has a weirdly broad appeal.<p>It&#x27;s far better to get 50 page views from potential customers than 50,000 page views from people who aren&#x27;t truly interested in buying what you&#x27;re offering.')
