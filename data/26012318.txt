Item(by='ChrisMarshallNY', descendants=None, kids=[26012792], score=None, time=1612347603, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012151, text='In the 1980s&#x2F;90s, a medical degree was the “sure bet” way to a six-figure income.<p>The result was a huge flood of doctors that were...<i>not the best</i>, and some fairly dodgy medical schools.<p>Of course, this also ended up pushing malpractice insurance (and tuition) into six figures, so I guess we achieved balance.<p>We’re starting to see some similar stuff, going on, now, in software development. There’s some...<i>not the best</i>, engineers, out there.<p>I know that E&amp;O insurance isn’t cheap, but it’s a tiny fraction of medical malpractice insurance.<p>That may change, as Big Data, terrible security practices, and ML&#x2F;AI are starting to make the old adage <i>”To err is human; but it takes a computer to really screw things up.”</i> kick into overdrive.')
