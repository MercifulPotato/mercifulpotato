What was funny about this project was that I started it with basically a thought of: &quot;there will be probably some annoying roadblock at some point that will make it unviable to continue; I accept that and will be ok with stopping once I stumble upon it; but I don&#x27;t see one clearly from the start [I did some quick initial research how the formats &amp; the bytecode look and they seemed rather simple], and I&#x27;m <i>really</i> curious how far I can get if I decide to not think about this possible roadblock&quot;. Turns out I was able to get all the way to the end :D')
