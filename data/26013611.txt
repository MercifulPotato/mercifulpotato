Item(by='JasonFruit', descendants=None, kids=[26013677, 26014354], score=None, time=1612360702, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26013131, text='I&#x27;m not happy about any tool that increases the ability of online mobs to direct their momentary rage at individuals, even if those individuals have done stupid things.  There&#x27;s no check on a mob, online or otherwise, nobody in a position to impose restraint: once it&#x27;s in motion, it goes until its momentum is exhausted.<p>I don&#x27;t have a solution to this problem; I philosophically don&#x27;t agree with legislation to prevent publicly available facial recognition, because I think it&#x27;s maybe even worse to have a government monopoly on it, but I realize we can&#x27;t rely on people&#x27;s good judgment and self-restraint.  Maybe we have an awkward time until we develop a mature perspective on this technology.')
