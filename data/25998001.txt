Item(by='nullsense', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612248109, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25994004, text='ADHD is tough to understand and appreciate for those who don&#x27;t have it for this exact reason. Everyone experiences all most of the ADHD (except the interal chatter arising from your Default Mode Network &#x2F; Task Positive Network anti-correlation being broken), so they often dismiss it or say &quot;but everyone has a little ADHD&quot; or &quot;everyone feels that way&quot;.<p>The difference is in frequency, severity and duration.<p>In my case I&#x27;ve spent north of 1000 nights in the past 5 years doing the dishes at either 1am, 2am or 3am because I cannot stop doing what I want to do and direct my attention towards the dishes until it&#x27;s the absolute last possible minute of the day. Often then it&#x27;s just so painful to me to do it. It nearly caused my marriage to break down because I would leave them undone. No matter what I try I can&#x27;t get myself to do them straight after dinner. Night after night I would wonder to myself &quot;I wish I knew what this was called&quot;. I didn&#x27;t seriously think there was something wrong with me, I just couldn&#x27;t understand why I couldn&#x27;t , despite my best efforts, get myself to behave in a different way.<p>And that&#x27;s just one facet of my life and how it affects me. There&#x27;s more and they just pile up. When you add it all up together and you consider pound for pound how I&#x27;m doing you start to appreciate the frequency, severity and duration that can be considered pathological.<p>It presents pretty differently across people, but by and large a diagnosis is a huge penny dropping revelation.')
