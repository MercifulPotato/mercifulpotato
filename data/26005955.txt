Item(by='zippergz', descendants=None, kids=[26007172], score=None, time=1612296676, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004088, text='After many years at big tech companies, I am now working at my first that has &quot;Architect&quot; as a separate role. While many of my colleagues in that role are great people and very smart, after a few years of living with it, I strongly believe that it&#x27;s an &quot;org smell&quot; and I will seek out companies without it in the future. It creates completely the wrong dynamic to have the hands-on engineers, no matter their seniority, not be fully empowered and expected to make these decisions. And no matter how experienced and smart the architects are, if they aren&#x27;t working hands-on with the systems and code on a regular basis, they can&#x27;t make the best decisions. Software architecture as a separate job from software engineer is bad for everyone involved.')
