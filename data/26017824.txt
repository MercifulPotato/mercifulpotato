If you desire evidence-based management, you have to seek out case studies (and actually consider them, not discard them when brought to you like one manager did to me, &quot;Show me the evidence.&quot; &quot;Ok.&quot; &quot;Whatever&quot;) or run your own experiments. Even if you do seek out case studies, you will still want to run your own experiments because what works for X may not work for you.<p>Find a low impact project or a project that can absorb a potential setback and try it out. If it fails, you at least gave it a shot. If it succeeds, promote the idea among other teams and projects. You can always apply a sanity check on ideas before trying them, just like in other things.<p>&quot;I heard that having a foosball table in offices increases productivity by 25%.&quot;<p>&quot;That doesn&#x27;t sound right, but we can try to create better break areas.&quot;')
