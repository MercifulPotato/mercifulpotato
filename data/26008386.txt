Item(by='Jtsummers', descendants=None, kids=[26008546], score=None, time=1612307649, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008096, text='For me it&#x27;s a bit of a Catch-22. I don&#x27;t watch much YouTube content anyways, but I&#x27;ve reduced my consumption over the years <i>because</i> of the ads. From what I can tell, there&#x27;s nothing I <i>want</i> to watch anymore because I no longer know what&#x27;s there (and of value to me). So the prospect of paying is absurd for me as I&#x27;d be buying something with no real value except to remove an annoyance on the few occasions each month I bother to watch something on YouTube. If I had a clearer reason to increase my YouTube consumption, I&#x27;d entertain paying.<p>Other video services have clearer value <i>to me</i> in the content they provide so I pay for them. I suspect I&#x27;m not alone in this.')
