Item(by='hinkley', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612294247, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005093, text='This is not a rhetorical question:<p>Do you still want to live close to SV when you&#x27;re not working there anymore?<p>Your burn-down rate for your retirement fund is predicated pretty substantially by the standard of living you maintain after you retire. If a lot of your day to day activities become indefensible once you can&#x27;t justify it based on work concerns, then your costs may be lower.<p>Look, for instance, at all the fancy cars that real estate agents have to drive to exude competence. They end up leasing a high end car, when maybe all they want is a 2013 Subaru Outback for activities and half a closet of clothes from Duluth and Patagonia that last forever. Once you pull on that thread, then moving to an exurb might make sense too.')
