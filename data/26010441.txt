I&#x27;d assume that tablets would leave &quot;pointer&quot; unchanged even if a mouse is plugged in, though I haven&#x27;t verified that.<p>And aside from that, shouldn&#x27;t a tablet where a mouse is being used as the primary input show buttons sized appropriately for a mouse, rather than larger ones? The whole idea of making buttons bigger on mobile is to accommodate thumbs, so it seems like this media query is <i>exactly</i> what we should be using.')
