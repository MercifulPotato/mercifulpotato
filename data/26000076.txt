Item(by='zbuf', descendants=None, kids=[26000739], score=None, time=1612268284, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999071, text='Nobody has said it, but it seems we are reaching a fork in the desktop?<p>For users, not &#x27;right&#x27; and &#x27;wrong&#x27; here, just two groups whose goals differ.<p>Those supporting X.org are using X very much in the spirit of it; custom window managers, network transparency, and possibly bitmapped fonts, BSDs.<p>And those in support of Wayland have arrived there via Linux with eg. GNOME and dominated by GTK and QT desktop apps; looking for an open-source offering with the same functionality as Mac and Windows desktop.<p>Really, this has been brewing for a while. It&#x27;s been a fluke that the underlying X has allowed these two groups to co-exist.')
