It exists to serve Homebrew, or more accurately, Homebrew’s other formulae. The primary purpose of Homebrew’s Python formula is to enable other Python-dependent Homebrew packages to work.<p>If you want to develop Python (likely Ruby&#x2F; PHP&#x2F; whatever), you are best keeping your dev environment separate from the interpreters installed for the purpose of running apps managed by Home-brew (or apt&#x2F; rpm&#x2F; etc).')
