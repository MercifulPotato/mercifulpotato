Item(by='Escapado', descendants=None, kids=[26012844, 26012717, 26012617], score=None, time=1612346542, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26011105, text='Used to have panic attacks and constant anxiety (without knowing what they are and what was wrong with me) every day for about 2 years triggered by a bad cannabis experience . Turned out I was predespositioned and it runs in my dads family. It started to mix with depression. 7 Years later I still sometimes get panic attacks and anxiety and dystonic thoughts, especially when my perceived stress is high (I work as a freelance SE). \nI have identified following long term factors that help bringing frequency and severity down significantly (talking about going from daily to maybe once every few months):\n- Exercise 4 times a week (preferably running)\n- Eat healthy (I cut sugar and eat according to an app 90% of the time)\n- Being outside every day and exposing myself to sunlight\n- Balancing alone time with social time according to my needs\n- Walk away from things that stress me out for no real benefit (that also means walking away from people)\n- Do not overpromise<p>Factors that help me coping when it hits anyway:\n- Breath slowly, deeply and on exhale remind myself that its only temporary\n- Put my feet on a cold floor\n- Smile and nod\n- Eye movement desensitization and reprocessing (google it)\n- Walk outside')
