Less &quot;burn 100m&#x2F;s retrograde&quot; and more &quot;intercept target&quot;, &quot;pull alongside target&quot;, etc.<p>I believe this reflects the creator&#x27;s conclusions regarding the nature of space combat. that is, the outcome of the battle is more or less determined by the weapons loadout and approach speed&#x2F;direction. so the real &quot;fight&quot; is maneuvering for a favorable approach and&#x2F;or forcing the enemy to waste their delta-v, which the game definitely stresses. there aren&#x27;t a lot of meaningful tactical choices once you&#x27;re within gun range.<p>could be a case of being too realistic for its own good.
