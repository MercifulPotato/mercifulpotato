Item(by='jabbany', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612304835, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007518, text='I don&#x27;t think that the OP is arguing for &quot;always&quot; sticking to entrenched players, but rather for people to face the reality that the agility of startups come with drawbacks in their service (even though it&#x27;s often not apparent).<p>It all comes down to making informed decisions. If you&#x27;re just using RH for &quot;playing around&quot; with some surplus money, this lack of liquidity shouldn&#x27;t be as important for you (as compared to, e.g., the ease of use, or low fees...). However, if you&#x27;re using RH in a &quot;serious&quot; capacity, this (reliability) should definitely be something to seriously consider, since you may not have the guarantees that you can take for granted with traditional players.<p>The true problem with the RH situation is the lack of transparency throughout. It really does no good (and comes off as very &quot;slimy&quot;) if third parties have to expose their business model or why they&#x27;re having issues. A little transparency would have gone a long way for people to be sympathetic to the startup. But then again, I guess that harms the &quot;magic&quot; factor of a startup.')
