Item(by='FloatArtifact', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612267799, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999071, text='One of my main gripes unfortunately Wayland does not function very well with accessibility software. Areas include but not limited to :<p>-simulated keyboard&#x2F;mouse input (some progress recently with some composers)<p>ability to inspect window attributes (e.g. window title, executable and handle)<p>ability to manipulate windows (e.g. maximise, close, etc)<p>So just as with automation software this has significant impact on accessibility. The current composers available provide very limited functionality. Not to mention there are many different composers that would have to be supported to make many different system configurations accessible.')
