Item(by='quotemstr', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612254531, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25998079, text='There&#x27;s no reason for an operating system to have a stable system call ABI. A stable ABI means that the kernel support boundary grows forever and that userspace shims are impossible in the general case. And what&#x27;s the point? Calling through libc or ntdll or whatever is appropriate on a given system is no great burden. Go&#x27;s libc avoidance is just hubris.')
