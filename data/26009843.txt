Item(by='godelski', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612319072, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26003263, text='The StyleGAN2 paper[0] actually addresses some of the biggest clues. They didn&#x27;t completely solve everything but symmetry is one of the biggest indicators. Specifically ears and eyes. But also really pay attention to the teeth. If you go to ThisPersonDoesNotExist[1] you&#x27;ll see that there&#x27;s still phase issues with teeth and eyes. It&#x27;s an improvement on the original paper but it is still weird. For eyes you&#x27;ll notice things like them not pointing in the same direction (this isn&#x27;t always super obvious). Another big thing to look at is the neck. Sometimes women will get Adam&#x27;s Apples and men will lack them. Collars won&#x27;t be symmetric and necks can have weird wrinkles. Lastly pay close attention to the background because that&#x27;s where you&#x27;ll likely find monsters. Lots of portrait photos have blurred background so &quot;lack of detail&quot; isn&#x27;t a great metric. But backgrounds that are a bit disjoint are great indicators. None of these metrics alone is a great tool and you gotta use them together (unless someone messed up big time when selecting the photo). Poke around through [1] a bit and you&#x27;ll start seeing some of the features I&#x27;m discussing. But I should also note that these flaws are known and being worked on so this information&#x27;s usefulness will degrade with time (it&#x27;s still good information because it helps us know how to look and what&#x27;s difficult about faces).<p>These of course go along with standard identification techniques like lighting, shadows, etc.<p>[0] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;arxiv.org&#x2F;abs&#x2F;1912.04958" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;arxiv.org&#x2F;abs&#x2F;1912.04958</a><p>[1] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;thispersondoesnotexist.com&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;thispersondoesnotexist.com&#x2F;</a>')
