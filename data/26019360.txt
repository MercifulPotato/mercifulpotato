Item(by='foobiekr', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612387692, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017754, text='From a manufacturing perspective, the equipment was there because they&#x27;ve been working on it 20Y; whether the vaccine worked is a separate question.<p>By analogy, it&#x27;s one thing to have a fab, a working fab can make 5nm chips as designed at some yield, however, the fab is not responsible for whether the chip designs work or are valid.<p>My point here really was that tech people, and especially people in the software industry, ignore that the bulk of the work isn&#x27;t the equipment, it&#x27;s the long tale of supply chain, manufacturing process and QA.  All of the 5nm equipment exists today, it&#x27;s the other parts that are the hold up.<p>Essentially, in our own domain we know better, and we should really understand this and stop making this mistake in other domains.')
