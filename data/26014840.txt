Item(by='valuearb', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612368046, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014475, text='A Buffett story you might find interesting. He refuses to look at the stock price of any potential investment while he is doing his valuation for it.<p>His thinking is if he falls in love with the business while reading their financial reports, and he knows the stock is trading at $10, he’s subconsciously influenced to bias his valuation estimate over $10, because he wants to buy it.<p>So he waits to ensure his valuation is as unbiased as possible, and only when finished checks the price. Kind of like the excitement of opening a present at XMas, not knowing if it’s a lump of goal or new AirPods.')
