Item(by='thow-01187', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612385115, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017940, text='I believe one of the major reasons why Huawei is so vilified is that it&#x27;s the first Chinese corporation that not only outprices its Western competitors, but also outtechs and outmanages them. This goes so counter to the Gated Institutional Narrative that cognitive dissonance kicks in - media insist there must surely be something dishonest and fraudulent about Huawei.<p>Both Ericsson and Nokia barely make any profit, even amidst of what should be a 5G bonanza. They&#x27;re famous for their perennial layoffs, constant cost-cutting, bland working conditions, outsourcing, infighting, insane level of bureaucracy and proliferation of management positions.<p>Huawei on the other hand, is well known for paying above-market wages (though long working hours), &quot;poaching&quot; skilled people from competitors, generous employee share scheme, valuing engineering above middle management, contributing to open source projects, and relative freedom their R&amp;D personnel enjoys in tackling technical challenges. And their B2B offerings are the best value. And their consumer electronics is among the best value. And on top of that, they&#x27;re highly profitable.<p>Even if you assert there&#x27;s some secret money pump from CCP to Huawei, you cannot deny the fact that Huawei is a well-oiled machine that delivers. Pump billions into not only Nokia&#x2F;Ericsson, but also IBM, SAP, HP, Oracle - the money would just get sucked into a black hole with very little to show for it. Huawei is portrayed as evil, because the alternative is to confront our weakness.')
