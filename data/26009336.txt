Item(by='fossuser', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612314138, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26009220, text='It sounds like we probably agree on most things and use fidelity in roughly the same way.<p>I’d argue Craigslist’s design is ugly, but quite good - it’s dense&#x2F;high bandwidth, does exactly what people want without fluff. It’s fast. They also benefit a lot from network effects, but even ignoring that I think the website does a pretty good job doing what it&#x27;s supposed to and is easy to use.<p>Fidelity is ugly and bad. Hundreds of hidden menus. Many things can’t be done via the app. Some actions require a phone call. Options trading sucks.<p>For an example, I wanted to wire my rent. The place to do this is via transfers.<p>Specifically: Transfer -&gt; to bank account -&gt; that I do not own.<p>That’s the flow to wire money to an external person. It’s not obvious and buried and it can only be done on a desktop.<p>Automated deposits are similarly cumbersome.<p>The app is also quite slow and the login flow is painful.')
