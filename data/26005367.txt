Item(by='ed312', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612293907, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004329, text='When I&#x27;m managing junior folks, I generally advise them there are three main paths:\n1. Keep focusing on IC&#x2F;software work. You keep growing and having larger impact, capping off with an architect type role.\n2. Lean into project management, full stack, and business a bit. Become a tech lead for increasing large&#x2F;important projects. This is a great general path that still lets you be pretty close to the code.\n3. Focus on business &amp; people. Gain experience mentoring teammates, then interns, then become a people manager. At some point you&#x27;re going to be trading cutting edge coding skills for mid level management skills. You can branch off to focusing on CTO or engineering leadership from here.<p>There are definitely a lot of other paths, but that above three are the most &quot;well worn&quot; and achievable in my experience.')
