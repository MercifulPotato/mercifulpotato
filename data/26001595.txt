Item(by='espadrine', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612277613, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999118, text='The way I see it, Scaleway vs. AWS is the old tradeoff: enterprise-grade hardware vs. commodity hardware with resilient software.<p>You can set up one AWS EC2 backed by an RDS.<p>Or you can set up two Scaleway servers in failover, backed by three CockroachDB Scaleway nodes.<p>In the second approach, you need more cheap things, but the total price can still be lower. On the other hand, you should not expect the same reliability from a single Scaleway node, compared to a single EC2.')
