Item(by='madeofpalk', descendants=None, kids=[26003704], score=None, time=1612275742, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001074, text='Screen resolution is, more or less the best approach.<p>Layouts need to fit a <i>size</i>, not a device, so that&#x27;s why media queries based on the screen size are usually the best approach. If I resize my browser to make it smaller, I should get a layout to match that. If I&#x27;m using Safari on iPad, docked to the side of the screen, I should get a layout to match that. These are all about screen sizes, not &quot;mobile vs desktop&quot;.<p>For testing interaction cabailities, there are the @media (pointer: coarse) and @media (hover: hover) media queries for testing touch vs mouse, and hover capabilities, but it&#x27;s important to not rely on these for mobile vs desktop layouts otherwise tomorrow a MS Surface user will post &quot;Just because I have a touch screen, doesn&#x27;t mean I&#x27;m on a phone&quot;.')
