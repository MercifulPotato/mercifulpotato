It&#x27;s also a better stable ABI than libc, and easier to maintain than pure kernel ABI (like Linux does), because nobody forbids the kernel to inject different vDSOs to different binaries, if a need arises.<p>vDSO is also a language independent construct, so there&#x27;s no special treatment for any favorite language, be it C (OpenBSD), C++ (Windows) or Oberon OS (Oberon).<p>vDSO: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;VDSO" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;VDSO</a>')
