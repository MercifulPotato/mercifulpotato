Item(by='cryptoquick', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612294288, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001681, text='Reading more of it, it looks an awful lot like the business model behind YCH auctions-- Your Character Here. That&#x27;s a very successful model, and it often results in prices $400+, which is about 2-3x more than what artists often charge otherwise.<p>That said, YCH is a more traditional auction over a single piece, not a Vickrey auction over a specific supply. According to this model, a Vickrey model would be:<p>- Scope the work to a specific standard that the artist specifies, in terms of colors, quality, characters, background, etc. That way, the bid is on a roughly equivalent amount of labor per piece.<p>- People put in (blind) what they&#x27;re willing to pay<p>- When demand is exceeded, the nth+1 highest is what everyone pays.<p>Very fair!<p>I&#x27;d be curious if there&#x27;d be a way to price something more custom; for example, putting a price on the number of hours required for each feature (more color or shading, more characters, etc.), how would that affect such a pricing dynamic?')
