Item(by='avh02', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612342692, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004552, text='I used to be a blackberry developer, granted it was my first job, but nevertheless: I&#x27;m glad blackberry died it&#x27;s slow, painful death.<p>Developing for blackberry was a disaster, I can even say it was the single worst programming experience I&#x27;ve been through. To name issues off the top of my head:<p>- signing code with BB&#x27;s SLOW servers every time you wanted to deploy to a physical device (I&#x27;m talking waiting times of 50+ seconds). These servers regularly keeled (there were websites to tell you if they did), meaning you couldn&#x27;t deploy to a device to test on, which was important because:<p>- emulators were so far off the actual devices you needed to deploy to multiple devices regularly to make sure you code worked<p>- devices would restart regularly, and blackberries took a long time to start<p>- each device, with the same OS, had different quirks, and different APIs would give different outputs depending on a combination of device&#x2F;OS-version, which was important when those outputs were for things like screen size.<p>- the developer tools were just not there: things like getting to logs, etc<p>- heroics were required to do things like override the default blue highlight in a list selector (i forget the name) in order to get our application&#x27;s design to work right.<p>I used to do dual Android&#x2F;Blackberry development, and I hated projects on BB. I am however talking BB OS 5&#x2F;6&#x2F;7. Our company wouldn&#x27;t even invest in developing natively for BB10, we&#x27;d just port android.<p>Good riddance.')
