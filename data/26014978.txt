Item(by='wegs', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612368597, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004724, text='There isn&#x27;t a robust economics argument for advertising. We&#x27;re dumping a bunch of economic resources (lost otherwise productive time) into an industry that serves to distort free markets.<p>There are claims of letting people know about products, or &quot;driving consumer demand,&quot; but they fall apart as you dig deeper.<p>It&#x27;s not clear how it ought to be regulated, but it ought to be regulated. It ought have been regulated a long, long time go, actually. When banner ads popped up on the Internet in the nineties was a much easier time than 2020, when we have trillion-dollar businesses pushing back.')
