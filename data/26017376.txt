Item(by='hxtk', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612377699, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26016692, text='In the theoretical sense what you care about is whether you can safely assume those hashes will always be unique.<p>In the practical sense what matters is whether you can safely assume the hash for a particular user in a particular system will ever be unique.<p>E.g., if I had a snapshot of a database mapping identities to password hashes and a log of all the hashes that had been computed by the auth server, I could make very reasonable guesses about who logged in at what time.<p>With that said, I am not a lawyer and I have no idea what the legal significance of all that might be.')
