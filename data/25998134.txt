Item(by='nullsense', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612249829, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25996227, text='Although I feel like my childhood was OK, I dropped out of highschool and from the age of 17 to age 27 I worked a _total_ of 1 year split across 5 different jobs.<p>If you&#x27;re worried about the effect medication might have, I understand, I&#x27;m a parent and I&#x27;m 99% sure my boy has it as he&#x27;s exactly like me. I would implore you to consider the abject danger that untreated ADHD presents to its sufferers. Shorter life span by on average 12 years. It&#x27;s a much worse diagnosis than people realise. The transition into adulthood took me a decade. It was rough. Really, really rough. I only got diagnosed last year at 32. Medication or not, what you should endeavour to do is monitor if it goes into remission in early adulthood for them or not, and keep them educated on the topic so they can understand how&#x2F;why they experience life the way they do. I&#x27;m not medicated myself at the moment (because thus far I haven&#x27;t responded to it) but being aware of it has made a big, big difference.')
