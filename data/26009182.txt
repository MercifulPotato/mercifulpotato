Item(by='throwawaysea', descendants=None, kids=[26009556, 26009682], score=None, time=1612312920, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26006940, text='It&#x27;s crazy that these companies can grow at this speed even though they are larger than any that existed historically. We&#x27;re talking about multiple companies with market caps north of <i>one trillion USD</i> with double-digit growth and massive profit margins. We need to have a revamp of anti-trust regulations to break them up, because with their immense financial warchests and the protection of network effects, they are not going to face real competition from anyone. An economy made up of many smaller companies will not just create better outcomes for customers but also distribute wealth better. But right now, I don&#x27;t get the sense that the competition mechanism works well against these giants.')
