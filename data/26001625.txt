I&#x27;m not making a judgement call, I&#x27;m just explaining why people do that. As an atheist (well, I identify as an ignostic [1] which I view it so be a subcategory of atheism) I don&#x27;t go convince people to be atheist. This is partially because all my close friends are already irreligious; and partially because religion is inflammatory so the cost&#x2F;benefit trade-off isn&#x27;t in my favor. However, if I had a very close friend, say my wife, who believed in God and spent a good amount of time and energy I would consider talking about these issues with them. This certainly doesn&#x27;t sound crazy to me, as long as you&#x27;re being civil, polite, considerate etc all that jazz.<p>[1] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;Ignosticism" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;Ignosticism</a>')
