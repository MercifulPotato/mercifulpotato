Item(by='harshreality', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612387674, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018246, text='It was plotted and written in the era where a plot of the week had to carry an entire episode.  Main characters can no longer be allowed to get entangled in a non-critical event for a full episode just to reveal something important about them for later.  Writers have to be more clever than that.  There&#x27;s no room for &quot;this time on B5, a rebellion&#x2F;alien&#x2F;telepath problem&#x2F;encounter.&quot;<p>With 22-episode seasons like the original, I bet a good writing team could do something like this:<p>Season 1: building up the world, revealing the Shadows at the end<p>Season 2: Shadow war<p>Season 3: proxy wars<p>Season 4: Loose ends &amp; Consequences, including Thirdspace and the telepath war. If there&#x27;s enough material, maybe even move the telepath war to a 5th season. I always wanted to see what happened to the Vorlons&#x27; favorite telepath.<p>There should still be room, in seasons 1 and 2 especially, for <i>some</i> cute but essentially irrelevant subplots like Green vs Purple.')
