Item(by='sradman', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612280448, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000998, text='The Russian Sputnik V vaccine [1] distinguishes itself from other two-dose Adenovirus vector vaccines [2] (Oxford–AstraZeneca and Chinese CanSino) by using different vectors (Ad26 and Ad5) for each dose. The Johnson &amp; Johnson vaccine [3] is an Ad26 single-vector single-dose vaccine.<p>In my opinion, the outstanding question is production capacity.
