Item(by='notJim', descendants=None, kids=[26007853], score=None, time=1612302464, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007194, text='That&#x27;s not what national total wealth refers to. It refers to the wealth of all citizens.<p>&gt; National net wealth, also known as national net worth, is the total sum of the value of a nation&#x27;s assets minus its liabilities. It refers to the total value of net wealth possessed by the citizens of a nation at a set point in time.<p>Given that multi-national corporations exist, it seems obvious that companies will have greater wealth than nations.')
