Item(by='kuschku', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612284950, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26003185, text='The goal isn’t to prevent escalation to root, but to prevent escalation out of a sandbox.<p>If you sandbox an application today, while it can’t access the filesystem at all, and nothing under your user account, as long as it can show you a window under X11, it can log, manipulate, and inject any input events or window contents of any other windows as well.<p>That’s the one single big hole in todays sandboxes under linux, and there’s only two options to solve it: put a whole separate X server into the sandbox of every single application with X-on-X, or replace X. Which is one of the things wayland fixes.')
