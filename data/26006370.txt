Item(by='elonmollusc', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612298693, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005157, text='I worked for a few years and then went back to a Ph.D. program.<p>It&#x27;s now possible to do an M.S. while you&#x27;re working. There are now a number of good online programs, with Georgia Tech&#x27;s OMSCS at the top of the pile. You might want to take a year or so in your current position and then evaluate whether an M.S. would be a career enhancer. The culture of Master&#x27;s programs nationally is shifting towards working professionals doing their degrees online, so the case for quitting your job to go back to a full-time residential program is going to get weaker and weaker in the coming years.<p>Ph.D. is a totally different game and a bigger conversation. Short answer: unless you really, really want to work in academia it&#x27;s not going to be worth the opportunity cost.')
