Item(by='curryst', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612280121, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001812, text='&gt; Weak security does not bestow the right to steal data.<p>It does not, but entirely absent security does give the public the right to view the data.  HiQ vs LinkedIn [1] found that scraping of publicly available assets did not constitute a violation of the CFAA.<p>That court upheld that in order to trigger a CFAA violation, there needs to be some form of access control in place.  Otherwise the data is considered public.  Not only are you allowed to scrape it; Parler is not allowed to try to stop you from scraping it, unless they are willing to put the data behind a log in wall.<p>[1] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.eff.org&#x2F;document&#x2F;hiq-v-linkedin-ninth-circuit-decision" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.eff.org&#x2F;document&#x2F;hiq-v-linkedin-ninth-circuit-de...</a>')
