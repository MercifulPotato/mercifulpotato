Item(by='SpicyLemonZest', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612276512, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001066, text='The problem with these kinds of discussions is that many people are morally scandalized by answers like &quot;the mall is very important to me&quot; or &quot;I&#x27;m aware of and accept the marginal risk&quot;. So there&#x27;s a lot of social pressure to come up with reasons why whatever you&#x27;d like to do is &quot;safe&quot;, and those reasons don&#x27;t necessarily reflect the actual thought process. Most likely your friend would be going to the mall regardless of what safety measures were in place.<p>I&#x27;m imagining a friend challenging me on whether it&#x27;s safe to go out to get boba. I might point out that they don&#x27;t have you wait inside the shop or that I avoid going when there&#x27;s a long line, but the honest answer is that I really want boba and don&#x27;t typically think about the risk in this level of detail.')
