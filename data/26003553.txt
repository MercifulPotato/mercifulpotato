Item(by='vlovich123', descendants=None, kids=[26005489, 26004725], score=None, time=1612285633, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002996, text='Compressed instructions really hurt the parallelism of CPUs. See M1 for an example of what happens when you design your decoders to fetch independently of each other because the offset of their instruction is deterministic. I think a better model is for code to be compressed with an efficient encoder at cache line size (eg zstd with the dictionary defined to be your instruction set)')
