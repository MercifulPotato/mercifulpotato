Item(by='throwdbaaway', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612308384, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25991484, text='In your example, the WHERE clause of the query and the partial index didn&#x27;t match logically, i.e. the query may return rows that are not indexed. There&#x27;s nothing that postgres can do, and I wouldn&#x27;t classify the behavior as peculiar.<p>On the other hand, with sqlite, the WHERE clause of the query and the partial index must match *literally*. So let&#x27;s say you have a partial index with WHERE severity_id != 0, and a query with WHERE severity_id = 1. All the rows with severity_id = 1 are already indexed, but the engine is still not able to make use of the partial index. This one bit us hard.')
