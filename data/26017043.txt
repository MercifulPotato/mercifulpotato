For medical reasons you should stop often.  (experts don&#x27;t seem to agree on exactly what the right amount is, but for discussion lets call it 5 minutes out of every hour). Note that the stop is to move around, if you sit on a toilet for that time it doesn&#x27;t count.<p>Many people will stop only for fuel every 3 hours, and try to get everything they need to do (restroom, buy snacks, eat...) in the amount of time it takes the pump to fill the tank.  This isn&#x27;t a good idea, but it is what people in the real world do.')
