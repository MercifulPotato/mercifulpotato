Item(by='timr', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612392028, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017769, text='The technology is pretty standard. I can&#x27;t imagine that there&#x27;s any tech-driven bottleneck to development, but rather, issues surrounding licensing, IP transfer, liability, etc (in other words: legal concerns).<p>Once those are resolved, there&#x27;s still a fixed amount of time that it takes to convert existing production facilities to some new purpose that you can&#x27;t really eliminate (the old saying about 9 women having a baby in 1 month applies here...)<p>Basically, it&#x27;s the sort of thing HN readers don&#x27;t like to hear: the complexities of the real world create obstacles that are difficult to surmount with technology alone. If I had to guess, even if you got approval to have N partners manufacture the vaccine <i>today</i>, it would still take a few months to ramp those partners up to capacity.')
