The conceptual gulf between their positions was too wide to allow any meeting of minds; Léon Rosenfeld, one of Bohr&#x27;s devotees, talking about Everett&#x27;s visit, described Everett as being &quot;undescribably [sic] stupid and could not understand the simplest things in quantum mechanics&quot;. “\nSource and original source here: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;Many-worlds_interpretation" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;Many-worlds_interpretation</a><p>Everett spent 4 years doing graduate studies, taking his first physics courses then and coming up with his many-worlds dissertation within a single year (137 pages ’typed’ (according to current historical records) by his later wife Nancy Gordon).
