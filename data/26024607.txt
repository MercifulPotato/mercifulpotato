But this reads as awfully sad to me. I hope you&#x27;re doing OK.<p>I had a a pretty long period of time where I consigned myself to rubber-banding between relentless, meaningless, profitable, and phoned-in productivity vs total solipsism under the guise of &quot;self-care&quot;. I told myself things like this comment back then. I thought if I could savor and find meaning in the little things, then I could learn to accept the fact that I had sacrificed most of my agency as a human being in the quest for career progress and the appearance of success. It was really, really not healthy in the long-run, and I don&#x27;t ever want to be stuck in my head like that again.<p>I&#x27;m fairly certain that I really am what I do and what I&#x27;ve done - and that&#x27;s OK. I don&#x27;t think I could tolerate existence otherwise. I still waste plenty of time (and recommend choosing to do so ), but I&#x27;m now more conscientious about whether the time I waste is manifesting into something physically observable at the very least. It&#x27;s easier to keep the habit alive than it is to build it back once you&#x27;ve lost it.')
