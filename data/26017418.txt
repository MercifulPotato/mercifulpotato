Item(by='zanecodes', descendants=None, kids=[26017563], score=None, time=1612377876, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26016532, text='Maybe what app stores should do is give apps a 3-star &quot;implicit&quot; review by default, when the user downloads and installs the app. This could possibly also be deleted when the user uninstalls the app.\nThis way, users who don&#x27;t feel strongly enough about the app either way to give an &quot;explicit&quot; review are still represented in the app&#x27;s overall rating, and the impact of early good&#x2F;bad reviews is diminished.<p>On the other hand, this would probably cause app ratings to tend much more strongly towards the median, which could make them less effective signals of quality.')
