Item(by='dsr_', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612301867, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26006418, text='Replacing the surrounds on a driver is a thing that an amateur can do with the aid of a YouTube video, on the order of replacing a dishwasher but not as heavy or dangerous. It involves glue and being careful.<p>Replacing ear cushions is even easier than that: you order replacements, pull off the old ones and tug the new ones into position. It takes slightly longer than changing the batteries in a remote control, but much less time.<p>I have a 60W stereo amplifier from the 1970s that works and sounds great. I have an FM tuner from the 1960s of excellent quality.<p>I no longer have cassette tape decks, but I have a reel-to-reel machine from 1962.<p>There are speakers from the 1960s and 70s that are worth owning and using; there are lots from the 80s and 90s that are not.')
