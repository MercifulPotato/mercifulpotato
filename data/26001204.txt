Item(by='mellosouls', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612275643, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999813, text='There are libraries and libraries. I think they are a wonderful institution and there is something romantic about the idea of them, and being in them; naturally that isn&#x27;t always the reality.<p>Nosing into a book in a quiet corner of a grand city building is not quite the same as rifling through the daily papers in a brightly lit communal area of a dilapidated town centre building with the local bored&#x2F;lonely&#x2F;bookish. Each have their different pleasures and vibe.<p>Then there are the researchers, official or otherwise - e.g. see the teeming hordes in the British Library in London bagging their daily laptop spot.<p>As more and more gets digitised (which modern libraries are fundamentally core to and involved with) and the physical environments (formerly the intellect-grooming dens of the working-class) are seen as an irrelevance to be defunded - or merged with communal zumba classes and the like - with librarians replaced by floorwalkers and customer service we may see them decline, but it will be a sad day when there are none left.')
