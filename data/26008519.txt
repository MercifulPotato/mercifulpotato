There are alternatives that don&#x27;t involve interest. You see it all the time in the auto industry where they have 0% loans. Do the same with houses.</i><p>If your perspective is that artificial bubbles in asset prices will be reduced by lowering the cost of borrowing (i.e. no interest) then you&#x27;re going to have to explain that, because it&#x27;s the opposite of what normally happens.<p>EDIT: Shariah-compliant mortgages are generally constructed so there&#x27;s no interest, but the cashflows between the parties often look very similar. e.g. a Musharakah loan where you only initially own the home in the proportion of your downpayment but gradually buy more of it over time by paying the bank off over time, as well an element of rent.
