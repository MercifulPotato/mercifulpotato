Item(by='hunter2_', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612406429, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26016482, text='I think many people creating social content mainly just want to choose between two classifications:<p>Option 1 is for content that won&#x27;t offend anyone or tarnish the author&#x27;s reputation. The audience can include all contacts including coworkers, family, etc. and the author will often use their real name. Example: Facebook.<p>Option 2 is for risqué content. It could cause issues with employment and other real-life relations. The audience must be carefully controlled, or if not, a pseudonym (that only a carefully controlled audience, if anyone at all, can map to a real name) must be used. Example: Reddit; private group chats.<p>This seems to cover most scenarios without the overhead&#x2F;stress of trying to shoehorn all of these boundaries in a single platform&#x2F;app.')
