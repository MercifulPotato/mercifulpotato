(i&#x27;m glad i bought it before &quot;smart&quot; teevees that phone home to the mothership became the norm.) my trust in amazon was much higher in those days. really doubt i would ever do that again. for any moderately expensive piece of high tech gear, i&#x27;d try to buy it directly from the manufacturer, or from a reseller that specializes in that particular type of thing.<p>if you limit your amazon purchases to medium-expensive items that are not easily cloned in china, you&#x27;ll probably be fine. it is kind of a shame that you have to be so cautious, though.<p>okay, actually, i lied: i wouldn&#x27;t buy anything at all from amazon these days. they are looking far too much like a monopoly for my tastes, and i do not approve of the way they treat their minimum-wage workers. it has been a struggle, but i recently abandoned my amazon account, created in 1998, and have moved 100 percent of my substantial amount of e-commerce to other sites: chewy.com, target.com, crutchfield.com, b&amp;h photo and video, newegg, ebay, and so on. almost all of them have a substantially worse experience than amazon did, but i am willing to suffer a bit of inconvenience for this particular bit of principle.')
