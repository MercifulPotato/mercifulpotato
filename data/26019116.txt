Item(by='rkagerer', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612386472, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018914, text='That&#x27;s a great question!  I used to worry once miners are no longer enticed with newly minted coin their activity would trail off - particularly when transaction fees were close to $0.  But fees have gone up since then without curtailing use, meanwhile a succession of halvings has significantly reduced the rate of coin issuance.  And mining is still going strong.<p>Presently fees make up 13.5% of total block rewards.  Here&#x27;s a graph of how that&#x27;s varied over time: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;bitinfocharts.com&#x2F;comparison&#x2F;bitcoin-fee_to_reward.html" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;bitinfocharts.com&#x2F;comparison&#x2F;bitcoin-fee_to_reward.h...</a><p>It will shift toward 100% as we approach the 21M mark.  As long as people still want to transact in bitcoin, I think mining will continue to be viable.  Of course there will always be competition from newer, more efficient protocols with lower fees.')
