Item(by='totalZero', descendants=None, kids=[26010354], score=None, time=1612312525, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008845, text='&gt; Short squeezes are not good for the market, period<p>This is an opinion. To briefly articulate some arguments that take the other side:<p>Short squeezes are a disincentive for hedge funds to take undisclosed bearish positions in otherwise healthy companies, and for options dealers to sell cheap call options on those companies. They also increase equity value for shareholders. A squeeze can reduce the debt load for a company by incentivizing bondholders to convert debt into equity, and the profit potential thereof may help a distressed company issue convertible bonds to raise funds.')
