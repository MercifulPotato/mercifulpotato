Item(by='lkbm', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612279090, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001509, text='It&#x27;s pretty polarized in the US right now, so people will naturally lump people into the &quot;pro-mask&quot; camp and the &quot;anti-mask&quot; camp, and the latter is closely tied to a lot of anti-science views. It&#x27;s an unfortunate heuristic we use a lot. You&#x27;ll get it less if you provide more nuance and context, or signal that you do take things seriously. &quot;We need to start using N95 and surgical masks because cloth masks don&#x27;t work.&quot; won&#x27;t get you called anti-science. (Maybe impractical, though surgical masks pretty widely available.)<p>To respond to your specific statement, part of the problem is that &quot;don&#x27;t work&quot; is too blunt and not quite true. &quot;Don&#x27;t protect the wearer&quot; might be true. &quot;Barely protect the wearer&quot; would be. &quot;Don&#x27;t provide strong protection for others&quot; also true. But even a poorly fitted cloth mask is almost certainly better than nothing. (Of course, I&#x27;d also believe that they likely provide a false sense of security, leading to risk-taking that more than offsets their benefits.)<p>This ties in with one of my biggest frustrations of the whole pandemic, at least individual behavior: so many people&#x27;s risk analysis is just sorting things into &quot;safe&quot; and &quot;unsafe&quot;, rather than thinking in degrees. &quot;Cloth masks don&#x27;t work&quot; is unhelpfuly binary and non-specific. (Though including &quot;poorly fitting&quot; makes it better -- &quot;ensure proper fit&quot; is actionable advice.)<p>And while efficacy of &quot;cloth mask&quot; vs. N95 is good to know, &quot;cloth mask&quot; is a super-broad category. I&#x27;ll occasionally see people specify &quot;single layer cloth masks&quot;, but the only single-layer cloth masks are gaiters, and even those are frequently folded over double.<p>How good is my 2- or 3-layer cloth mask? What materials should I use? (To be fair, I have seen some people answer this question.) Is it relevant that I can barely feel my breath through it or around the edges even if I blow hard? How much of a difference does fit make?<p>Ideally we&#x27;d all have and use good quality N95s, but we shouldn&#x27;t make perfect the enemy of the good. We need information that will help us do better, rather than a binary &quot;N95 good; everything else bad.&quot;')
