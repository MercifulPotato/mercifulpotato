Item(by='gens', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612377975, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014879, text='As others have said, the energy that a supercapacitor can store is practically nothing compared to lipo. And that is by weight, when supercapacitors are less dense then lipo.<p>If you want more power you can just put more batteries in parallel. Or use batteries with lower resistance (the flat ones).<p>As for gathering bursts of energy; Again the problem is energy capacity. The (potential) energy released by braking really hard is really high (per time).<p>You could use a flywheel battery for that. They can take it. They are even used in roller coasters just for that purpose (granted they use big flywheels).<p>Considering people usually break &quot;softly&quot;, it is just not worth it.')
