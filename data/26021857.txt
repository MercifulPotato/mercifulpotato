Item(by='Xixi', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612405665, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26021193, text='Direct democracy = popular vote, representative democracy = elected representatives.<p>China, Russia, USA, France and Switzerland are all republics: two are dictatorships, two are representative democracies, and one is a direct democracy. Canada, UK, Japan and Belgium are all constitutional monarchies, and all of them are considered democratic. Saudi Arabia and Qatar are monarchies, but certainly not democratic.<p>Democracy is a scale, probably better used as an adjective: the more democratic, the more power is wielded by the people.')
