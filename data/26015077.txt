Item(by='PragmaticPulp', descendants=None, kids=[26016627, 26016662], score=None, time=1612368967, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014959, text='Good points. When we tech people spend all day in terminals using software like git, it’s easy to forget that the average person doesn’t have the time nor desire to learn difficult software. They just want to get the job done and get on with their day. Ideology isn’t a factor at all.<p>I think the free software community would get much farther if the acknowledged the realities of school districts with limited IT staffing. If you have a team of 2-4 IT people to manage hundreds or thousands of kids, you don’t want any software solution that doesn’t come with some form of tech support or at least some readily available help documentation or tutorials.<p>The biggest thing FSF could do is make a push for making the software as easy to use, troubleshoot, and understand as possible. Once it becomes less work (not necessarily less cost) to use free software, the change will follow organically.<p>Trying to petition schools to use free software on ideological grounds is a step in the wrong direction. Tie your movement to real-world incentives, not abstract ideology.')
