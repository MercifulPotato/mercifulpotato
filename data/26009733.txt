Item(by='jart', descendants=None, kids=[26014580], score=None, time=1612317723, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001128, text='I distribute binaries. My binaries work on six different operating systems. In order to do that I had to roll my own C library. I&#x27;m happy I did that since it&#x27;s so much better than being required to use six different ones.<p>I&#x27;m not opposed to vDSO but I disagree with how Linux maps it into memory by default. Linux should not be putting anything into the address space that the executable does not specify. MMUs are designed to give each process its own address space. Operating systems that violate that assumption are leaky abstractions imposing requirements where they shouldn&#x27;t.<p>The main thing dynamic shared objects accomplish is giving upstream dependencies leverage over your software.  They have a long history of being mandated by legal requirements such as LGPL and Microsoft EULAs. It&#x27;s nice to have the freedom to not link the things.')
