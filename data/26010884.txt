Item(by='prepend', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612330051, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000940, text='&gt; Trying to boil down a very diverse global population, which may or may not even be paid, into a single $&#x2F;day seems a bit ridiculous to me personally.<p>This is not the method described in the world bank web site. I don’t think the intent is to boil down to a single measure.<p>Tracking data globally is really challenging, so a consistent and meaningful measure is required to even have a hope of a perspective across countries. It’s useful in eradicating poverty to both measure progress or failure as well as to prioritize investment for areas of greatest need.<p>This also isn’t the only measure of poverty as there’s many others and there’s quite a bit of literature in global health on other measures as well.<p>That being said, I think there is room for improvement both in developing more useful metrics as well as improving accuracy of measures.')
