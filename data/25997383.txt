Item(by='pdonis', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612239537, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25990518, text='I don&#x27;t think this means you shouldn&#x27;t say no. It just means that you need to understand how to decide <i>what</i> to say no to. If your boss is the one who decides your performance, and they would rather you dropped everything to help others than do what you&#x27;re &quot;supposed&quot; to be doing, then you need to say no to what you&#x27;re &quot;supposed&quot; to be doing.<p>There is a bigger picture version of &quot;saying no&quot; in play here as well, though: is a company where your boss doesn&#x27;t even seem to care about what you&#x27;re &quot;supposed&quot; to be doing one that you want to work at? Will it make the best use of your talents?')
