Item(by='p1esk', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612306219, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999950, text='Regarding Optane and &quot;consumer&quot; usage: If you&#x27;re talking about a typical PC usage, like browsing, using MS Office, and playing games, then sure, there&#x27;s absolutely no perceptible difference between Optane and any other SSD, even a SATA SSD. But that&#x27;s not what people buy Optane for, so it doesn&#x27;t make much sense testing such an expensive drive on regular &quot;consumer&quot; PC tasks.<p>I train deep learning models, so I read random images from disk to feed data loader pipelines, and I also write back stats, logs, and model checkpoints. I frequently use 8 GPUs, each running a separate experiment, with its own data pipeline. This means every GPU is reading 1.2M jpg images in a random manner, every 30 minutes. I haven&#x27;t actually tested if the disk reads are a bottleneck, but I&#x27;m guessing random reads of 20M images per hour would stress any drive, so I&#x27;d rather not risk it and get the best money can buy.')
