You might get unlucky by holding cash during a market rally, then buy back for a dump.<p>An investor who sells when they think the market is going to go downhill, with the intention to buy back later is not acting as an investor but as a <i>trader</i>.<p>That&#x27;s why hedging with options is less risky (and less profitable in the best case).<p>edit: My use of word &#x27;unlimited&#x27; applies if you want to keep the same stake at the companies. In money terms you cannot lose more than the value of your holdings.')
