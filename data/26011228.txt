Item(by='mdtusz', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612334710, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008542, text='The way I always see it is that `null` is for known unknowns, and `undefined` is for unknown unknowns.<p>Explicitly defining something as `undefined` is paradoxical and IMO should never be done. I&#x27;ve tried to explain this concept to some co-workers without much lasting success, but it&#x27;s a pretty common concept to other languages.<p>Undefined should basically never be used as a value directly.')
