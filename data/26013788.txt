Even with a margin account, your purchasing power is 4:1, at best! Now - if you have _enough_ money, you can write uncovered(aka &#x27;naked&#x27;) option contracts and open the possibility of unlimited losses at least, but that won&#x27;t afford you any more leverage.<p>IMHO...that &quot;fastest&quot; method to lose money(in the markets) is the commodity futures market. If commodity futures don&#x27;t scratch your itch for losing(or gaining) money very quickly, you can also trade forex with similar leverage.
