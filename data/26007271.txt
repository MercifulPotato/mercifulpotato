Item(by='arcticbull', descendants=None, kids=[26007329, 26008005, 26007476, 26007358, 26007855, 26007403, 26007688, 26007354], score=None, time=1612302424, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007194, text='What do you mean by &quot;get this big&quot;?<p>Governments aren&#x27;t defined by a market cap, but rather by the services they provide to their citizens.<p>Consider that a private company&#x27;s only goal is to enrich its shareholders, and they have no responsibility to the people or places around them. They are only accountable to their shareholders and the government.<p>A governments responsibility is solely to its citizens who also control it by vote.<p>The bigger your biggest companies are, the more powerful your government must become to keep them in line - to ensure they don&#x27;t break the rules. If you want a small government that is able to retain order and fairness, you must also limit the size of the companies within its purview.')
