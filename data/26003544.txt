Item(by='DCKing', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612285578, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26003192, text='I would say that&#x27;s a fair summary.<p>On Linux, there&#x27;s X11 based desktops and &quot;post-X11&quot; desktops. &quot;Post-X11&quot; would involve implementations of Wayland and Freedesktop Portals [1], using libinput [2] - and some other things I&#x27;m forgetting. All these things don&#x27;t depend on Wayland and run fine on X11 too (heck you can even run a Wayland compositor in an X11 window), but most of this stuff is pretty new, implementations have lots of QA issues, and application adoption is also not fantastic. On X11 you can just talk to the X11 socket directly for what you want as a workaround, but on post-X11 you <i>have to</i> adopt a new means of doing it. And that hurts.<p>[1]: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;flatpak.github.io&#x2F;xdg-desktop-portal&#x2F;portal-docs.html" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;flatpak.github.io&#x2F;xdg-desktop-portal&#x2F;portal-docs.htm...</a><p>[2]: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.freedesktop.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;Software&#x2F;libinput&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.freedesktop.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;Software&#x2F;libinput&#x2F;</a>')
