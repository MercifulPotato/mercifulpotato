Item(by='johnvaluk', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612309599, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008130, text='I also have my dotfiles in portable repositories that must work on a variety of unices (so I don&#x27;t use the popular-but-nix-specific home-manager package). My custom ISO has no user data, it just creates my immutable account with my public SSH key (in case I need to remote into the machine immediately) and has a global alias named &quot;bootstrap&quot; that fetches and installs my dotfiles. After that, I can pull any updates made on other machines, log in and out of the DM, etc. until I turn off the computer. I&#x27;ve gone weeks on the same session in RAM (waking from sleep works fine on most machines).')
