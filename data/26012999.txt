Item(by='simonh', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612355182, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012056, text='There was a lot of Norse cultural influence in the North of England, but of course they had a lot of linguistic crossover with the Anglo-Saxons. In some recent historical TV shows the cultural differences between Anglo-Saxons and the Danes are exaggerated for narrative reasons, but they could actually understand each other&#x27;s languages reasonably well.<p>The influence of Anglo-Saxon culture is the main reason why English culture isn&#x27;t the same as French culture, so I don&#x27;t think you can really say it&#x27;s defunct. The influence of French is stronger in southern &#x27;BBC&#x27; English, but England has a lot of regional dialects that still retain a lot of quite ancient linguistic influences.')
