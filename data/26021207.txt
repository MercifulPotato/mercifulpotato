Item(by='abcc8', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612399462, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26019602, text='None of the techniques you describe are reliant on WGS. I wholeheartedly agree that sequcning has revolutionized medicine, but WGS isn&#x27;t there yet.<p>NIPT uses low-coverage sequencing to identify aneuploidies for chromosomes 13,18,21 and some larger microdeletion syndromes - this is not WGS.<p>Cell free cancer screening is panel based and assays specific, known driver mutations.<p>Rare disease diagnostics can be WGS based (and some of the rapid 48h WGS studies of NICU babies are compelling from a technical standpoint) but most diagnoses identified via WGS can also be found via WES + chromosomal microarray.<p>Targeted cancer therapeutic target identification is panel based for most patients, as WGS doesn&#x27;t identify too many targets for FDA-approved therapies that a panel + IHC + FISH + fusion testing won&#x27;t.')
