It&#x27;s the &#x27;risk-free bond&#x27; term that I dropped from my explanation of the put call parity.<p>To replicate the stock, you&#x27;d buy the call and sell the put.  The risk exactly balances out in the sense that a total portfolio of 1 stock short, 1 call long and 1 put short would have zero risk and behave like a risk-free bond.<p>(If the risk premia of the long call and the short put would not exactly balance, you could make money with very simple arbitrage trades.)')
