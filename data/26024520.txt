Item(by='dagw', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612438691, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26023827, text='<i>Can we talk about why service is astronomically better in the US</i><p>I&#x27;ve been reflecting on my restaurant experiences in the US and Europe and I&#x27;m not sure, is it really &quot;astronomically better&quot;? On balance I&#x27;ve probably had people screw up my order and tab at least as often in the US as in Europe. Waitstaff in the US don&#x27;t seem more knowledgeable about the menu or the wine list, in fact on the whole they tend to be less knowledgeable (probably due to high turnover and restaurants not investing as much in staff). Kitchen &#x27;failures&#x27; and food not arriving on time isn&#x27;t less common in the US and so on.<p>Sure US waitstaff will be a lot chattier, smile a lot more, ask me more about my day and my meal many more times and say &quot;I am SO sorry&quot; a lot more when things go wrong.  But on the whole they don&#x27;t seem to handle the mechanics of making sure I have a smooth and stress free dining experience any better.<p>One &#x27;plus&#x27; I will give to the US which might account for some of it is that due to wait staff being basically free there are a lot more of them pr. customer which sometimes speeds up service.')
