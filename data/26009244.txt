Item(by='sudosteph', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612313372, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004552, text='I picked up a keyone for my personal device after my Nexus 5 bit the dust (it lasted over 5 years), and I&#x27;m really happy with it. I had used a few newer android flagship devices as work phones (pixel 3 most recently), but I really hated the huge screen size on everything. I was getting frequent hand cramps on the latest pixel, and one handed usage, even for light browsing was impossible for me (fwiw, I&#x27;m a 5&#x27;5&quot; woman with hands to match). The smaller screen height of the KeyOne fits my needs perfectly, and having a physical keyboard again is just icing on the cake. Related, this is the first phone where I haven&#x27;t cracked the screen within the first year (without a case, no less!). I&#x27;ve dropped it a ton on hard surfaces, but it seems that the keyboard cushions the fall, so that was an unexpected benefit.')
