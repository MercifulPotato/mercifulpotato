Item(by='arsfeld', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612290014, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004118, text='I used both and even though their purpose is very similar (have an &quot;immutable&quot; system tree that you create and switch into it), the day to day is very different.<p>Silverblue is still pretty much imperative, you install&#x2F;remove RPM packages and that&#x27;s it, you use Flatpak for everything else. NixOS you have to describe your entire system in a programming language. NixOS gives you so much more freedom to do what you want, but you have to work for it, learn a language, learn it&#x27;s constructs, etc.<p>I enjoy both though, feels like the right direction to go, you just need to choose how you want to interact with your OS.')
