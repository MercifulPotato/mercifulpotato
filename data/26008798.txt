Item(by='astrange', descendants=None, kids=[26009595], score=None, time=1612310170, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008197, text='A frequency graph doesn&#x27;t describe headphones that well because there&#x27;s many other objective variables. I think impedance, sound isolation and THD are more important since you can&#x27;t calibrate those out even if you wanted to.<p>Even a good objective review like rtings is just another review, since headphone performance depends on your player, the shape of your head and ears, and the noise level of your environment.
