Item(by='eat_veggies', descendants=None, kids=[26008630], score=None, time=1612308109, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26006229, text='You&#x27;d only do this once, at the point data is going into your app via e.g. a network request. Data from the network is an untyped blob, and all your TypeScript efforts fall apart if that blob doesn&#x27;t match your expectations (which are encoded in your types).<p>In most cases it&#x27;s worth it to trade the small performance penalty for correctness, especially since the cost of fetching data is already dominated by the network and backend.')
