Item(by='dane-pgp', descendants=None, kids=[26020398], score=None, time=1612389243, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017822, text='To increase user engagement, what about allowing users to carry out threaded discussions of their own in a part of the UI that is distinct from the main conversation?<p>Perhaps users should have to opt-in to seeing comments from the &quot;crowd&quot;, and there should be a minimum amount of time the user has spent on the site (and discussions read) before they can comment.  Also they&#x27;d have to receive a minimum number of upvotes for their comments before they were allowed to vote, etc. which would gamify it a bit.<p>Rather than fighting the echo chambers, you could use them to your advantage, by requiring users to pick a side they support in each topic and splitting the discussions into two camps.  That way expert A could see popular comments in support of their position, and expert B could see comments which support them instead.  They could even opt to see what the other camp was discussing, to make sure they weren&#x27;t misapprehending the other side&#x27;s position.<p>My other request is that you make the site work without JavaScript.  People don&#x27;t want your annoying pop-ups, and it should be possible to present text in a browser without running scripts.  (Also it might be nice to have meaningful URLs, like Reddit has).')
