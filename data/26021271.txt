Item(by='llamaz', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612400010, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26020765, text='How do you know truth is objective? Without a doubt it&#x27;s true in hard science, e.g. chemistry and biology.<p>But by the time you get to social science, economics, politics, and ethics I think we&#x27;re closer to the dark ages than we like to admit. In the dark ages religion was taken as objective truth, but in the present that doesn&#x27;t work because people think religion exists to &quot;trick&quot; them or gain political power.<p>It&#x27;s possible that we extend the authority that science has in the fundamental sciences to areas where it is not as effective without justification. The free marketeers always touted their view of objective science as dispassionate, objective science. Similarly, everything from the marshmellow test to the left-vs-right brain (creative vs logical) has been debunked, or at the very least been called into serious question due to a reproducibility crises in psychology and similar disciplines.<p>We really live in a HP lovecraft-esque world, where we understand almost nothing, and are controlled by powers beyond human understanding and control. This idea is something that naturally repels the human mind, and those who profess such a view are treated as being deficient if not mentally ill (external locus of control, anxious&#x2F;depressive).<p>The reason Nietzsche is so revered but Schopenhauer is not, is that Nietzsche offers a hopeful &quot;way out&quot; of this crisis with his nonsense idea of the overman, while Schopenhauer is more disciplined and stops at pessimism. The latter is attacked as bourgeois, and as being a hypocrite, while the male chauvinism and other negative character traits of Nietzsche are overlooked.')
