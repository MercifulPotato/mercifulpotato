I&#x27;ve considered doing something similar due to the dearth of decent material on FPGA and hardware design on the internet though never quite got around to it...<p>For the sprites did you consider a part on dealing with multiple overlapping sprites? (I just skimmed, so apologies if you did this somewhere and I missed it!) There&#x27;s at least two approaches here and I think it&#x27;s an interesting thing to explore to discuss design trade-offs.<p>The two approaches I can see are:<p>1. Load the relevant line of each sprite into flops during the blank interval, when drawing determine which sprites produces a pixel for a given output pixel then apply ordering, taking into account transparent pixels to determine which one wins. You have a simultaneous read of all of the sprite flops each output pixel to do this<p>2. Pre-render all of the sprite pixels for a line into a memory buffer during the blanking interval. If you have multiple sprite pixels per cycle from memory and take advantage of that fact your FPGA can probably run at some multiple of the pixel clock (say 100 MHz vs the ~25 MHz pixel clock for 640x480) you do quite a lot in the blanking interval.<p>I reckon 2 wins when you&#x27;re wanting more sprites per line and higher bit-depths per sprite pixel (8-bit 256 colour sprites vs 2&#x2F;4&#x2F;8 colour sprites).<p>I&#x27;ve got a ~60% complete version of 2, maybe I should follow you lead and finish it off and write about how it works.')
