Item(by='liaukovv', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612269062, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000145, text='The problem with clocks is not how to generate them fast enough, but rather how to keep them exact and consistent<p>Phase locked loop can use reference clock(generated outside of the chip by an oscillating crystal, like in a watch) to generate many other clocks of different frequencies each of which is corrected and guided by this reference clock<p>In fpga there is no &quot;system clock&quot;, each module can use its own clock at frequency that it needs, all defined and routed by the designer')
