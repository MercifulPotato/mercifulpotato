Item(by='betwixthewires', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612424996, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014344, text='I use RSS for everything, especially on mobile. Every blog I follow, every news source, every video channel (YouTube or otherwise) I follow using RSS or Atom. My reader (feeder, a FOSS feed reader for android) is my morning paper.<p>If a site serving recurring content does not have an RSS or Atom feed, I do not subscribe. If a feed truncates content and forces me to open the article in browser I do not subscribe. If I&#x27;m trying to read what you have to say or watch your video or listen to you, I do not need to load a complex web UI to do it. I do not need an account just to follow what you want me to hear or read.')
