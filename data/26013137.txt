Item(by='dragontamer', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612356335, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012250, text='&gt; As for whether 1 dose prevents spreading: for many countries, in particular UK, the current bottleneck is hospital occupancy, and that is overwhelmingly people over 70 - something like 60% IIRC.<p>Preventing the spread of COVID19 has an exponential effect on all stats.<p>If 1-dose only reduces the symptoms (but continues the exponential spread), then 20% vaccination rate means that you have 20% fewer hospitalizations after a month.<p>-----------<p>In contrast, if 2-dose prevents the spread, then 10% vaccination means 10% fewer spread *PER GENERATION* of COVID19.<p>That&#x27;s 90% of the hospitalizations in one generation (~1-week period). After 2-weeks, you have ~81% hospitalizations. After 3-weeks, down to 73%. After 4 weeks, only 65%.<p>Controlling the spread is the #1 health priority that has exponential effects. I&#x27;d take 1&#x2F;2 the vaccination rate if we have a better chance at tackling the exponential-spread issue.')
