Item(by='senko', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612285143, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002217, text='I&#x27;ve been in both sides of the table: ran a dev agency in Central Europe, as well as hired developers from various places in the world.<p>In my experience, wages of experienced developers don&#x27;t vary so much geographically, because outsourcing is a global market. Dev&#x2F;agency in Ukraine, Germany, China or Argentina know what kind of rates they can get based on the skillset involved.<p>These rates are much lower than in superstar cities, that&#x27;s basic supply and demand and CoL calculation.<p>But companies chasing extra low rates will end up with subpar team and these are the clients and agencies that give outsourcing a bad name - be it India, Nigeria or Slovenia.<p>That&#x27;s not to say rates are flat. Specialized shops&#x2F;devs, people involved with prominent open source projects and those that invest in deeper relationships with the clients (and delivering higher&#x2F;noncomiditized value) can charge a premium, while body shops on UpWork will bid lower amounts.<p>Outsourcing, like many other things in busines can be great IF you know what you&#x27;re doing.')
