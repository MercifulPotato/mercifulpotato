Item(by='totalZero', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612399074, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018498, text='&gt; I have been seeing people on WSB claim that the stock price shown on the ticker is &quot;manipulated by the hedgies&quot; and asking when the real stock price is going to finally appear. This is pretty similar to Q people asking when it will be revealed that 45 is still the president.<p>I don&#x27;t think it&#x27;s fair to draw an equivalence between the two theories.<p>It&#x27;s pretty easy for a large market participant to impact the bid&#x2F;offer when there isn&#x27;t much liquidity, especially if the natural buyers are restricted from trading. The price moves because of supply and demand.<p>The WSB perspective is perhaps a bit uninformed, but it&#x27;s not particularly bizarre (unlike the QAnon garbage). They&#x27;re just asking &quot;when will the price reflect our pent-up demand rather than artificial supply?&quot;<p>Unfortunately, the reality is that prices get set during these moments of absolute craziness. That&#x27;s why you sometimes see hedge funds reload after hours after an earnings miss on one of their long positions. They want to absorb the supply so the stock doesn&#x27;t crash lower.<p>Look at GME today. The price moved a couple of percent even though nobody has the slightest reason to believe that GME stock is worth this particular price. Some say it should be higher due to flows around positioning, and others say it should be lower because of fundamentals.')
