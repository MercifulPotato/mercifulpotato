A sports team practices together, does your team? A sports team works as a unit, does your team?<p>This is why I like XP. Another architecture. Sometimes a great dev is just the one who can take the unglamorous tickets and get them done at a sustainable pace. Or someone who is good at devops, teaching, or morale building. Sometimes just communication.<p>Everyone has different strengths. No one would ever say &quot;who&#x27;s the best (American) football player?&quot; because you&#x27;d have to ask &quot;who&#x27;s the best kicker, tight-end, defensive lineman&quot;. They are all different roles.<p>To think that football would have the level of awareness that it cannot be measured as a single dimension makes it sad and laughable that people reduce programming skill down to one most of the time.')
