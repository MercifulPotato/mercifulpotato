Item(by='Ohwie6oaHem', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612294587, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001972, text='The main flaw in the emerging hardware ecosystem is the lack of reciprocal&#x2F;cooperative licensing.<p>The RISC-V ISA and some of the open cores allow (or even encourage) being bundled with proprietary hardware.<p>Even locked-down, user-hostile hardware with patents and cryptographic protections is allowed.<p>If you expect RISC-V to be the &quot;Linux of hardware&quot;, think again. Your next phone will not be &quot;free&quot; as in &quot;free-from-backdoors&quot; or &quot;free-from-patents&quot;.<p>Yet, compared to the status quo, it&#x27;s a step in the right direction.')
