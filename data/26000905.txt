Item(by='sandworm101', descendants=None, kids=[26001301], score=None, time=1612273816, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000768, text='The material it is written on is from 1400.  That is very different than saying was written in 1400.  It is common for hoaxers to use old materials.  It could easily be from 1500, only written on a bit of 1400 pig found or cut from another work.  Even a test of the ink could be inconclusive as the ink too could incorporate older material (charcoal from an older building).')
