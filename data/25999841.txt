Item(by='bootstrapping', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612266135, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999080, text='This is a furphy.  None of the comments here containing the word &quot;calm&quot; have, on review, made that claim.  They are all personal anecdotal observations.  Indeed there are also dissimilar observations.  This is consistent with my experience of discussion within ADHD-specific forums, at least in part because <i>people aren&#x27;t generally so stupid</i> as to generalise their personal experience to a universal ambit claim.<p>Neither is such a statement made on the product labels.<p>I <i>have</i> seen such absolute statements along such lines made in the popular press and their online equivalent, but I&#x27;d rather leave toilet paper out of the discussion.<p>So by all means, do not believe the statement is true, but understand that there is, in fact, no broad acceptance or community belief of such an absolute statement being true, and furthermore, rather crucially, that what you may believe has <i>no bearing whatsoever</i> on the veracity of individual experiences.<p>Consequently, even the anecdotal reports have continuing clinical relevance, because:<p>* Medicine is the field of treating a patient, and if the result is a better adjusted, rested, and socially engaged individual, then any such observed effect for the individual patient calls for psychiatric consideration.  What such effects are not, is <i>diagnostic</i>, or the basis for a product claim; and,<p>* Science is the field of explaining phenomena, and the reports are not adequately explained by the research, in large part because the wide gamut of behaviours, symptoms, mechanisms, measures, and outcomes collectively labelled &quot;ADHD&quot; are themselves a very poorly explained and haphazardly classified selection of neurological variations from the population majority.  This being, of course, a very common circumstance in the young field of psychiatry.<p>All of which means there is no such paper, and besides, no-one is even writing one.')
