Item(by='inglor_cz', descendants=None, kids=[26021661], score=None, time=1612386019, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018491, text='CFR isn&#x27;t the main problem about Covid, the 5 per cent of cases that end up in hospital is - and for how long they stay there.<p>People with bad cases of Covid routinely spend weeks in ICU before they either get better or die. This is an enormous workload for doctors and nurses.<p>Also, we do not know for sure how long haulers will fare. I myself had heart arrythmia after an otherwise very mild case of Covid. Fortunately, a simple beta blocker helped me and there are no traces of anything worse on my heart, but the doctors that took care of me told me that they have a surge of youngish patients with myocarditis and similar problems. Some of those are actually sportsmen.')
