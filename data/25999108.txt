Lets you pick color schemes from a subset of the image&#x27;s colors (specifically, those which form a convex hull, when visualizing the colors in RGB color space).<p><a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;image2palette.app" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;image2palette.app</a><p>(Note: There are still some issues with showing images on iOS browsers)<p>I mainly made this to satisfy my own curiosity. I had the idea of forming a convex polytope based on an image&#x27;s colors, and I thought it would be cool to be able to explore that in a responsive way through a graphical UI. Also it was a great way to learn Svelte and ThreeJS, which in many ways work rather well together! If anyone is curious to see the source code, I&#x27;m happy to link it too.')
