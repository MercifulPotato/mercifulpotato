Item(by='btown', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612371684, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26010290, text='Self-replying to add that it&#x27;s important to know when <i>not</i> to use a confirmation dialog (for instance, in a &quot;critical path&quot; of rote data entry), and that your customers may organically show you that things are critical-path that you may not have thought about. In that case, though, having a good abstraction for confirmation dialogs is even more critical, because it&#x27;s easy to remove (or A&#x2F;B test) without feeling like you&#x27;re throwing out significant amounts of effort; it becomes a zero-point story, so to speak. When you&#x27;re writing less code, there&#x27;s less code to remove when that becomes necessary, and that&#x27;s why abstractions are such a big part of our roles!')
