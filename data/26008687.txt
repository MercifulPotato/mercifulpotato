Item(by='satellite2', descendants=None, kids=[26008814], score=None, time=1612309391, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008291, text='Swift is only a communication protocol it doesn&#x27;t solve the clearing problem.<p>The clearing problem is really at its core about avoiding to move cash and how to transfer debt between two entities that don&#x27;t know (and trust) each other, as far fetched as it sounds.<p>When you transfer $1 to a friend of yours that is at another bank, your bank owes you one less dollar and your friend&#x27;s bank owe him one more. But aginst what? The promise by your bank to pay it with a physical dollar (or some other better debt) in the future. But if you are with a small bank, your friend&#x27;s bank might not trust it. So the they use a bigger bank that both trust that act as an intermediary and that lends for a very small rate as it is a 100% collateralized debt. This is the definition of clearing.')
