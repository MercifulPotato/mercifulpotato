Item(by='ineedasername', descendants=None, kids=[25997761], score=None, time=1612238190, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25997252, text='Kind of, but Geforce Now made the mistake of trying to create something that looked kind of like a storefront that appeared to auto populate your game library from Steam, which pissed off publishers who thought GeForce was basically making money off of their games without giving them a cut.<p>It&#x27;s probably more of a semantic difference, but saying &quot;Here&#x27;s a VM, do what you want with it&quot; is different than saying &quot;Create a game library with us and we&#x27;ll stream publisher X&#x27;s games to you. We&#x27;ll even put them in your GeForce library if you own it somewhere else!&quot;<p>So publishers got mad and many of the biggest pulled the most popular games. Had GeForce just said &quot;Here&#x27;s a gaming optimized VM&quot; and let users do whatever they want, it probably wouldn&#x27;t have been a problem. In fact, an very early version of GeForce now did exactly that, complete with a windows desktop.')
