Item(by='muzani', descendants=None, kids=[26001349], score=None, time=1612235241, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25994417, text='I&#x27;m 35 this year, not exactly old, but slower.<p>At this stage, I find that my learning skills have outpaced my slower mental agility. It&#x27;s similar to how people start doing their triathlons in their 40s. I can speed read and have better comprehension. I sleep, cook, and eat better (having money helps vs being a broke 23 year old). I can listen to dense, technical videos on 1.25x speed or less professional videos with stuttering on 2x-3x speed and write down clean notes in real time.<p>Bill Gates allegedly reads 150 pages&#x2F;hour in his 60s. So that&#x27;s my target. Much of it is skills, not attributes, and it&#x27;s something you have to actively improve.<p>You have to rekindle the flame constantly. Passion is easier to feed than discipline. &quot;Sucking it up&quot; is a good way to burn out. Think of it sort of like lighting up your marriage - you don&#x27;t need marriage coaches or toys. You don&#x27;t need to learn tolerance. You need to see things in the way you once did, and sometimes enjoy the little flaws.<p>A lot of the best people also have some methodology to learn YetAnotherProduct. Learn to deal with information overflow - find a way to rapidly filter out what&#x27;s good, what&#x27;s bad, what has potential. Identify which recommendation sources are good and bad. It&#x27;s okay to evaluate on gutfeel, but take a scientific approach to it, and note why you feel that way, then revise if you were correct.')
