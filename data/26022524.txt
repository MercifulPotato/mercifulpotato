Businesses were not nationalized and they weren&#x27;t staffed by anyone in need of work.<p>The War Productions board simply directed companies on what to produce.  They could run their business as they see fit with some controls like wage caps, raw material quota, production quotas.<p>And it works better for war - switching from making planes to airplanes versus having companies switch from making microchips to making vaccines.')
