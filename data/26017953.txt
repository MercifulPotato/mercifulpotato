Item(by='dcolkitt', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612380263, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017671, text='I think you see that pattern in a lot of subcultures that glorify destructive and reckless behavior. Look at hard drug subreddits, like &#x2F;r&#x2F;stims, or binge drinking fraternity culture, or that Facebook group where everyone points loaded guns at their crotch.<p>Participants will willingly acknowledge that the behavior is destructive or reckless. But they do so in a way that glorifies it. &quot;Woah, I totally blacked out again last night. So crazy. <i>High Five</i>&quot;. When the most self-destructive member receives the most attention that acts as a pretty powerful incentive.<p>Humans are social animals. And the way most of us avoid bad decisions day-to-day is to look at our peer group and think &quot;would this be something that a normal, respectable person would do&quot;. When you create a subculture filled with self-destructive people, it normalizes and encourages self-destructive behavior, even when people rationally know they shouldn&#x27;t be engaging in it.')
