Item(by='WalterBright', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612259340, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25998999, text='Curtis started out making airplanes by copying the Wright designs, including putting a stabilizer in a fixed position in front. Amateurs would buy his airplanes and race them. One racer crashed into a fence and damaged the forward stabilizer. In a hurry to rejoin the race, he simply sawed it off and took off. He found the airplane flew faster and better without it.<p>He transmitted this to Curtis after the race, who promptly pulled the stabilizer off all his airplanes that were in production.<p>And thus, the forward stabilizer disappeared.')
