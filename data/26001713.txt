I only mean the specification means very little in north america and of all the KN95 masks I have tried they have too high of a pressure drop across the actual filter to do filtering. When I use medical tape to seal them to my face they puff out and suck in dramatically. A low pressure drop N95 mask, when sealed with medical tape will barely move. You can even feel drafts from air conditioning through it.<p>What I am saying is that KN95 types in the USA are of such variably quality they are not a good choice. Use local standards and brands.')
