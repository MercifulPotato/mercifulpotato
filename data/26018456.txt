Item(by='Sohcahtoa82', descendants=None, kids=[26023038, 26020495], score=None, time=1612382754, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017164, text='Tesla&#x27;s supercharging network is a very under-advertised feature of a Tesla.  So many people think that a road trip in a Tesla means frequent 1+ hour stops to charge, when in reality its closer to a 15-30 minute charge every ~2-3 hours.  Supercharging stations are typically spaced close enough on the US Interstate system that you&#x27;ll usually drive to around 20% battery and then charge to 80%, a range which optimizes charging speed.<p>Sure, for some people, this is still not acceptable.  I won&#x27;t deny that an ICE is still going to be faster and more convenient.')
