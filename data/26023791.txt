Ask for a &quot;chargeback.&quot; Use that word. Don&#x27;t just dispute the charge, then resolution will be under the control of the scammer. Say, &quot;I want a chargeback.&quot; Under Visa Int&#x27;l&#x2F;MC rules, the bank has to initiate the chargeback and take the money back from the holder of a merchant account who has charged your sister&#x27;s credit card. Remember the magic word,&quot;chargeback.&quot; Note also that too many chargebacks will cause the termination of a merchant&#x27;s account.')
