Item(by='brofallon', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612372812, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26015071, text='This is a common misconception - &quot;averaging out&quot; errors only works if the errors are pretty rare at any given site. This is true for some types of errors &amp; sequencing technologies, but not universally true. Some types of DNA sequences (most notably homopolymers and other simple repeats) are very difficult to sequence correctly, and X% of the reads there will be incorrect. If X&gt;20% of so, then it may look like real germline variation no matter how many reads are sequenced')
