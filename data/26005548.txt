Item(by='jnwatson', descendants=None, kids=[26005649], score=None, time=1612294798, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005240, text='I used to work for a company that used to classify software as malware.  The original definition is much closer to what is broadly accepted (at least by antivirus vendors).  Much of what is classified as malware doesn&#x27;t exploit anything other than the user&#x27;s expectations.<p>The definition of malware is social construct, not a technical one, otherwise Powershell.exe would light up like a Christmas tree.<p>The only reasonable way to talk about malware is with respect to users&#x27; wishes and desires.<p>I&#x27;ll note that AV vendors invented a &quot;less bad&quot; label, PUP, for less-destructive malware.  That doesn&#x27;t make it not malware though.')
