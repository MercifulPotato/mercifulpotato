Item(by='Lichtso', descendants=None, kids=[26022980, 26020411], score=None, time=1612386638, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017086, text='Rendering (forward or backward) of algebraic curves (like bezier and nurbs) never gets mentioned in any such collections, despite being essential to any 2D or GUI framework. And if they are, then only as a short: Just subdivide &#x2F; tessellate it and then put it through the polygon pipeline! However there are far better ways to handle them, which even people in CG seem to be oblivious of.<p>And of course not to forget all the other approaches to modeling geometry like implicit surfaces, meta-balls, signed distance fields, constructive solid geometry, point clouds, splats, voxels, etc ...')
