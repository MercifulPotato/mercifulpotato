Item(by='vmception', descendants=None, kids=[26016837, 26016443, 26016159], score=None, time=1612372280, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26015528, text='All three cases by this author are only controversial because we have ignored a greater point: colleges don’t exist to get you a job. They exist for a class of society, a finishing school where they all meet each other and collaborate on the obscure arts that weave the fabric of our reality.<p>It has been this way for a millennium.<p>The workforce started using them en masse 60 years ago and we’ve been trying to patch this concept ever since. It is great that we have large populations educated in advanced multidisciplinary concepts. There is <i>also</i> no way for the university concept to not exacerbate inequality.<p>This still leaves the need for a way to screen for competent people, something that shows discipline and life stability to commit to something. I think the apprentice concept and trade school does that. Germany has this fairly institutionalized (and also free university). I hope larger economic unions are able to reconcile this.')
