Item(by='graeme', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612267203, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999908, text='I think limited missile defence is sensible for dealing with accidental firings or countries with tiny stockpiles like North Korea.<p>But it isn’t 2x. Try 10-20x. Modern MIRV’s can have ten warheads, and it is also <i>substantially</i> more difficult to target a moving missile than to hit a launch silo. Tests so far have not been anywhere near the accuracy needed to confidently guarantee you could shoot down a first strength. You need 100% performance, they’re getting less than 50%, for ideal conditions.<p>The economics just don’t work with a 20x cost gap.
