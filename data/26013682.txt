I know, Lua sucks very much, but nonetheless the power that LuaTeX (and&#x2F;or ConTeXt) offers is probably currently unmatched.<p>(And don&#x27;t forget that Lua has a C API, so it is possible to drive the TeX engine with C++ or Go or Rust or whatever.)')
