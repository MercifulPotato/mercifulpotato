Item(by='another-dave', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612342608, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26010608, text='Context matters too though — \nAs a general rule &quot;i&quot; is a terrible name for a variable but in a simple unnested loop it&#x27;s expected.<p>All these shorthands (e.g. K,V = Key,Value) have to be learned so should be used sparingly but in some places I think having shorthands for things like generics &amp; indices allow boilerplate code to &quot;get out of the way&quot; of the business logic.<p>You expend less mental energy trying to keep track of &quot;i&quot; (as you know it&#x27;s a simple loop counter) than &quot;index&quot;. And reducing cognitive load is the point of having descriptive names in the first place.')
