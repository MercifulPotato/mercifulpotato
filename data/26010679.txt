Item(by='ashtonkem', descendants=None, kids=[26011592], score=None, time=1612327374, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008153, text='Getting rid of commission was actually probably a bad idea. It creates an expectation that a very complicated service should be free, and puts a lot of pressure on now free brokerages to find a new revenue source. There&#x27;s a reason why RH is accused of helping Citadel front run their own customers.<p>This seems to me a lot like when VC backed startups artificially suppress prices below break even to push out competitors, with the intent to raise prices even more later. It&#x27;s good for the consumer while they can get VC subsidized goods and services, then very bad for the consumer once the VC funds dry up.')
