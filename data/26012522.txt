Item(by='Arnt', descendants=None, kids=[26012609, 26012607], score=None, time=1612349782, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012350, text='The article mentions a server in Japan that has a million users. Assume they each follow 15 users, half on their own instance, half elsewhere, and that off-instance followees are followed by an average of three users. If it used RSS, that server would issue 2.5m HTTP GET requests every few minutes in order to update the feeds, <i>even the feeds where nothing has changed</i>. Of course it could&#x2F;would use per-feed adaptive fetch frequency and use HTTP if-modified-since, but all that&#x27;s only damage limitation.<p>This happens because RSS network activity is mostly O(users × time), not O(activity).')
