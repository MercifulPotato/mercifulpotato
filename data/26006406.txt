Item(by='kelnos', descendants=None, kids=[26007944], score=None, time=1612298850, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005917, text='&gt; <i>He’s calling for real-time settlement which is not practical for equities.</i><p>Why not?  In most cases share ownership is just a row or two in some database.  If you can update those in real time (which you should; a legacy system that doesn&#x27;t support this can be upgraded, even if at significant cost in implementation and testing), and do instant (or near-instant) funds transfers, you should be able to settle within minutes.  Certainly same-day, at least.<p>Now, I would accept that maybe instant settlement is not <i>desirable</i>.  Maybe it&#x27;s good to be able to reverse fraudulent or illegal trades before money fully changes hands, for example.  But that&#x27;s a different thing.')
