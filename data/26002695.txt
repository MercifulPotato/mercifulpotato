Item(by='Nextgrid', descendants=None, kids=[26003599], score=None, time=1612282231, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002042, text='This is not a systemd issue per-se. Whatever process it’s trying to stop is taking its time. Systemd doesn’t explicitly know what to do and most likely falls back to a reasonable compile-time default.<p>The other comments already explained how to change the timeout per-unit, but I wonder how would you want a “perfect” init system to handle this?<p>Hang forever until the process dies without any message (as far as I know this is how legacy shell-script-based inits would handle it)?<p>Kill the process immediately and risk data loss?<p>Behave as-is but with a lower default delay?')
