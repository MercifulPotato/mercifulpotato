Item(by='ahepp', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612340251, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26011607, text='How can unions lose their incentive to serve workers? It&#x27;s a collective body of the workers. When workers are owners, incentives are aligned.<p>If you&#x27;re talking about union leadership being beholden to management, that is an issue unions often have, but I don&#x27;t think it is relevant here.<p>I&#x27;m saying, create a union trust fund with 25% of the company&#x27;s stock. It pays out dividends to all union members equally (not a slush fund for union leaders).<p>Thus union members have some say in how the company is run, but also have an incentive to be efficient and competitive in the marketplace.')
