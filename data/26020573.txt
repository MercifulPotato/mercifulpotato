Item(by='dragonwriter', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612394491, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26019716, text='Trying to assess <i>actual policy disagreements</i> by putting the polar extremes at 0 and 100 and having people pick a number in between for their actual policy preference without any concrete touchpoints for what those intermediary value means doesn&#x27;t work. Your aren&#x27;t comparing Republicans and Democrats policy positions, you are  comparing Republicans and Democrats views of the relative extremism of their own policy positions and those of the other parties.<p>A valid summary is not that the <i>actual differences in policy preferences are smaller than expected</i>, but that members of each party see their own positions as less extreme and the other parties positions as more extreme than members of the other party do.<p>Which is very much not news, and doesn&#x27;t actually indicate anyone is overestimating real policy divides as the authors claim. Their methodology could not, even remotely, support the basic claims they are making.')
