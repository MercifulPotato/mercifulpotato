Item(by='vegetablepotpie', descendants=None, kids=[25998072], score=None, time=1612242021, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25997299, text='Not OP, but we are a far cry from a techno utopia. We really had two opposing world views, one where technology would help us achieve a better life, another where it would contribute to us falling off a precipice. Neither won. On the plus side we have a world where global poverty is falling and more people are developing middle class life styles, we have amazing technology like gene editing, incredible computational ability, and entertainment options. However, this same technology has pushed us dangerous close to climate disaster, has us in the middle of a mass extinction, and has enriched a very small number of people.<p>You could evaluate this bet on the technical measures, and that was probably prudent, but at the full picture, the situation looks very mixed.')
