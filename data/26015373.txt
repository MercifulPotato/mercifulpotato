Item(by='bhupy', descendants=None, kids=[26017009, 26016076], score=None, time=1612370163, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26015250, text='Warehouse and distribution workers that are paid $15&#x2F;hr at a minimum, enjoy the same group health insurance benefits as their software engineering and product management coworkers, and enjoy the same 401k matching as their desk-bound co-workers — benefits that are unavailable to almost any other entry-level job that requires no college education.<p>And while it&#x27;s definitely difficult that they have to put themselves at serious risk for Covid, that&#x27;s true for any essential worker too. Ultimately most people agree that this is a worthwhile tradeoff to ensure that people continue to get access to food, supplies, COVID tests (in the case of frontline nurses), etc.<p>I&#x27;ll re-iterate, there&#x27;s no obvious correct answer, and it&#x27;s difficult to agree on the best way to litigate these issues. One thing I can say for sure is that blithe one-line HN comments is the worst possible way to litigate them, much less understand them.')
