Item(by='sighingnow', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612269276, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000243, text='GraphScope is a unified distributed graph computing platform that provides a one-stop environment for performing diverse graph operations on a cluster of computers through a user-friendly Python interface. GraphScope makes multi-staged processing of large-scale graph data on compute clusters simple by combining several important pieces of Alibaba technology for analytics, interactive, and graph neural networks (GNN) computation, respectively, and the vineyard store that offers efficient in-memory data transfers.<p>We just released the version 0.2.0. And along with the release, we launched a public JupyterLab service where you can have a try in your browser: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;try.graphscope.app" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;try.graphscope.app</a><p>Github: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;alibaba&#x2F;graphscope" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;alibaba&#x2F;graphscope</a>.
