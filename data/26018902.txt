Item(by='Jtsummers', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612385332, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018226, text='Item 5 in TFA is one that I found related to performance most. Using filters to toss out bad input means you&#x27;re potentially generating thousands of inputs for dozens of tests. Aggregate this across all your tests and it&#x27;s an incredible waste of time. Constructing proper inputs <i>directly</i> can get you closer to the minimum time required to execute the tests and help achieve that desired performance threshold. (NB: Not used professionally, only got one office to even entertain them but they wouldn&#x27;t take it and run with it even after I showed them a dozen errors I&#x27;d found in an hour of using them. Consequently my experience is still limited.)')
