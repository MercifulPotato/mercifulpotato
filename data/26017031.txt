Which are perhaps some of the most notoriously hard to please people in the world (I say this with some amount of love). And they take tremendous pride in their hardware (see: pc building subreddits, pcmasterrace, etc) and companies (nvidia, alienware, evga, msi, asus, etc) take great pains to market to them because they can sell to them at great markups just for making build quality a bit better than the usual dreck you find at Best Buy and slap on a bunch of alien heads and LEDs (again, I say this with some amount of love).<p>So I think the existing market is going to be really hard to crack for a lot harder-to-unseat reasons than just &quot;it&#x27;s a better experience&quot; (which they can&#x27;t provide anyway!). These people aren&#x27;t cost-sensitive (generally) and will generally have pretty decent, dedicated hardware.<p>So I can only conclude that they&#x27;re hoping to create a market of people who don&#x27;t currently play AAA games due to cost or space constraints.<p>Which is consoles.<p>So yeah, I really don&#x27;t know where they&#x27;re going with this.')
