Any display protocol can have multiple client&#x2F;server implementations. It just so happens that most of the free desktop standardized around the X.org implementation for X11, but even for X11 there were multiple client and server implementations.<p>The second thing is that software duplication efforts have a well known, tried and tested solution: implement the duplicated effort in shared implementations or libraries [1] !<p>The thing I find hard to wrap my head around is that this complaint is given in a community that usually champions protocol-over-implementations and diversity of implementations. The people actually making a difference on the Linux desktop are damned if they do and they&#x27;re damned if they don&#x27;t.<p>[1]: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;swaywm&#x2F;wlroots" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;swaywm&#x2F;wlroots</a>')
