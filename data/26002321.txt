Item(by='DCKing', descendants=None, kids=[26002467, 26002512, 26002423, 26002452, 26002344, 26002669, 26002519, 26002834, 26002946, 26002992, 26003745], score=None, time=1612280754, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002129, text='I&#x27;m beating a dead horse at this point, but there&#x27;s no such thing as &quot;switching to Wayland&quot;.<p>What most people mean when they &quot;switch to Wayland&quot; is that they&#x27;re switching from Gnome on X11 to a &quot;pure Gnome&quot; stack that uses Wayland and a bunch of other Freedesktop standards for interopability. There is absolutely no code from the &quot;Wayland project&quot; running there, because the Wayland implementation is Gnome&#x27;s own (it&#x27;s called Mutter). Similarly, KDE&#x27;s implementation is in KWin itself. The source article is from an author of yet another window manager (Sway) implementing Wayland.<p>What you&#x27;re observing is not &quot;Wayland not being there yet&quot;, it&#x27;s <i>Gnome not being there yet</i>. All of these deficiencies have nothing to do with Wayland itself: they&#x27;re general QA issues that plague an underfunded Linux desktop that&#x27;s making a slow technology transition.')
