Item(by='ThePhysicist', descendants=None, kids=[26018647], score=None, time=1612379418, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26016276, text='As someone who runs a small (bootstrapped) startup in Berlin I&#x27;d recommend you to avoid the smaller startups, as you will often be paid under par and might have bad experiences like the ones you described. I&#x27;d go for larger and well-funded ones. Also, if it&#x27;s not a must to work at a startup you could have a look at all the digital labs &#x2F; hubs etc. that established companies like BMW, Daimler, VW, BASF etc. are opening in Berlin. They offer somewhat of a startup experience but with big funding and the standing of a large corporate behind the scenes, so you might be able to get a better deal there. I worked for such a spinoff before founding a company again and I really enjoyed it, great pay, nice work-life balance and friendly and savvy colleagues. Just my 2c.')
