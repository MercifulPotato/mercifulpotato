It makes it impossible guess what they do. It makes it easy to overload the service with things that don’t belong.... if the service was named “OrderService”, if somebody wanted to add a “spell check” endpoint people would start asking questions if that service was the right place. But since the service was named “CaptainCrunch”, who knows if spell check belongs...<p>Same goes for team names too... clever team names are obfuscation.')
