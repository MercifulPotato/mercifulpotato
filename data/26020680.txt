Item(by='lhorie', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612395217, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26020533, text='Well, I don&#x27;t think the feedback of people that don&#x27;t use (or, more specifically, who don&#x27;t feel the pain of not having) generics is all that relevant. In my mind, the trade-off is between potential runtime performance improvements via monomorphization vs the compile time cost of figuring out how to emit said code.<p>I also think there&#x27;s an important consideration in terms of community and ecosystem. There are plenty of examples elsewhere where splitting on some core aspect of a language had some long lasting damaging effects (python 2 vs 3, perl vs raku, D v1 phobos vs tango, etc). I don&#x27;t necessarily anticipate that this would be the case here, but it&#x27;d be unfortunate if the community split into different go flavors based on similar-but-incompatible ways of doing similar types of tasks.')
