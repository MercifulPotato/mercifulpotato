Somewhere, I might still have an advertising card the O&#x27;Reilly folks were handing out about it.  You can see more here (and yes, bizarrely, wood-cut-look icons were a big design fashion trend for a period in the early &#x27;90s.  Sun&#x27;s TOPS used them extensively, too...) <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.oreilly.com&#x2F;gnn&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.oreilly.com&#x2F;gnn&#x2F;</a><p>That said, there were several earlier browsers that could opened image tags in another window or app - Viola, Cello, etc.  (Even Lynx, which still exists for some bizarre reason...)<p>As hard as it will be for the younger crowd to believe, at that time every new web site on the Internet got posted to the NCSA &quot;What&#x27;s New&quot; page, so people would know what all&#x27;s out there!')
