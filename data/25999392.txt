Item(by='steerablesafe', descendants=None, kids=[26001347], score=None, time=1612262525, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999071, text='I don&#x27;t get why everyone gets so defensive for Wayland. It&#x27;s a fact that a lot of things still do break for users if they switch, without existing alternatives or workarounds.<p>Yes, Xorg is now legacy, outdated, insecure, and does too much. But what wayland did was to replaced one functionality of Xorg, without offering alternatives to other functionalities. Which is good, I guess, it has a clear and focused responsibility.<p>But now compositors are responsible for other functionalities and they seem to be doing things in their own way. Which is also good, I guess, they explore the design space and then they can gravitate to a standardized way of doing things later.<p>But it&#x27;s more than a decade now, many things seem to be stuck in the &quot;exploring design space&quot; phase, so it&#x27;s understandable if some users are impatient. Yes, it&#x27;s free software without warranties, it doesn&#x27;t mean immunity from criticism though.<p>I also get performance regressions in wine games on xwayland, didn&#x27;t try wine-wayland yet though. But many games I play will never move from X.')
