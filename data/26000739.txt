Item(by='swiley', descendants=None, kids=[26000945], score=None, time=1612272769, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000076, text='One more nice thing about X that people sometimes miss is that you can turn off compositing (this is the default really.) Lots of people say Wayland feels &quot;sluggish&quot; and this is likely a major contributor to that. Especially if you don&#x27;t have hardware acceleration (and there are a huge number of reasons why that might not be working.)<p>Wayland solves a lot of problems by deciding to just not handle things but we have to recognize that taking that position sacrifices things.')
