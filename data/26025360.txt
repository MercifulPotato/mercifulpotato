Item(by='macawfish', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612447334, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26024855, text='For most eukaryotic organisms DNA it might not be &quot;random access memory&quot; but it contains a record of genes that somehow managed to get through the selective pressures they were subjected to over countless generations. I&#x27;d say that this information is an imprint of history, and is memory.<p>My hunch is that you&#x27;re thinking of memory as being equivalent to RAM in a computer. So I&#x27;ll remind you that read only memory is still memory.<p>Some microorganisms, like viruses, do directly modify DNA. That&#x27;s where CRISPR came from.<p>I&#x27;d encourage you to check out the full range of definitions of the word &quot;memory&quot;.')
