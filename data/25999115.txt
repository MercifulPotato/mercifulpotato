In some case, for hours at a time.<p>We were also shocked at how often airlines will change the T&amp;C&#x27;s, often to the detriment of the customer. Especially in regards to seating plans (separating friends and family), and baggage allowance.<p>I wanted to do something to help customers challenge airlines on such issues and, where possible, discuss solutions amongst themselves.<p>Techwise, while simple (Elixir, Vue, CouchDB), I think it works well.
