Item(by='mullr', descendants=None, kids=[26023336], score=None, time=1612394848, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26019950, text='To me, Cucumber is a way to write executable specifications that can also be read by non-developers. This can be tremendously powerful if done judiciously, or tremendously pointless if not. This may be because you do not in fact want or need executable specification, or perhaps because there&#x27;s nobody interested in reading it.<p>I believe that Cucumber is at its best in situations where it&#x27;s clear to all parties that a specification is valuable. In that case, making the specification executable is very clearly a massively useful way to spend your time.')
