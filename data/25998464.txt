Item(by='echelon', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612253957, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25992735, text='I remember pirating anime back in the 200[2-7] era and having to hunt for codecs to play anything. (Pirating was the only way to get anime back then.)<p>Windows movie player sucked and wouldn&#x27;t work with hardly any of the series being distributed over KaZaA, eMule, BitTorrent, etc. You&#x27;d need DivX, or QuickTime, more frequently some obscure and sketchy codec that came with its own `.exe` installer.<p>When VideoLAN came on the scene, it played <i>everything</i>. It also supported proper subtitles, so anime sub groups stopped baking them directly into videos. (The switch to MKV was huge for fansubs.)<p>VideoLAN was a huge part of my youth.<p>Many kudos!')
