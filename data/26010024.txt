Item(by='theamk', descendants=None, kids=[26011407], score=None, time=1612320671, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008018, text='This seems like lots of useful code, but at the same time it looks like it is designed in the &quot;all or nothing&quot; fashion, and I think it&#x27;ll severely hurt your adoption.<p>The hubstore has its own packaging format, as opposed to standard approach, such as a .whl file with entry_points of setup.py. As a result, you cannot use any of the pre-existing python apps. And if someone makes hubstore-compatible app, it is not going to be usable with regular python tools by default.<p>The Pyrustic is kinda the same -- it has some cool components, but it also requires a lot of buy-in. I could use better TkInter widgets, but I am not even going to consider it if it requires some sort of interactive, <i>non-scriptable</i> wrapper shell which completely destroys all my existing workflows.')
