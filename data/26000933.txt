Item(by='tootie', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612273996, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000763, text='There&#x27;s not really any proof that hygiene hypothesis leads to better outcomes. Yes, the immune system generally only strengthens when it&#x27;s exposed to pathogens, but being exposed to lots of pathogens also damages your body. And controlled exposure to pathogens (ie, vaccines) can trigger an immune response without injury. So, we&#x27;re likely trading weaker immune systems for millions fewer deaths. Look at the mortality rates due to infectious disease in countries with poor sanitation.')
