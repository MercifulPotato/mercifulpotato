Item(by='mr-wendel', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612304259, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007505, text='I like it too, but I think it misses something crucial: how often is the team <i>unblocking</i> others? This, naturally, is at odds with minimizing interruptions.<p>On one extreme there is no shield around engineers and they experience constant whiplash with &quot;code oracling&quot;, shifting priorities, obscure trivialities, and other things that can drive people insane and can prevent meaningful work from being completed.<p>On the other hand, sticking perfectly to &quot;we&#x27;re committed to this sprint and unless its on fire you wait in line&quot; and much of your business becomes unnecessarily rigid and painful. That, IMHO, is much worse.<p>Finding a balance is crucial, and to me thats just as interesting of a datapoint.')
