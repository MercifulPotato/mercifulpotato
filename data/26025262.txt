We were working on a system that would transfer data between a bank and the tax office. This system would send files from the bank to the tax office, when someone paid &quot;taxes due&quot; and fetch files from the tax office to the bank when the tax office saw that taxes had been over-paid.<p>All it did was &quot;shuffle files back and forth&quot; (with some data conversion between different IBM formats thrown in, for good measure).<p>But, I still recall the project 20+ years later, with some amount of pride. Partially because it was delivered on-spec, on time (well, it was delivered on Dec 23th and the deadline was Dec 31st, but as there&#x27;d be no one at the customer between xmas and new year...) and just a fraction under budget, with an exceedingly happy customer...')
