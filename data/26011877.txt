There&#x27;s nothing like being in the field with real users.<p>Ideally, you don&#x27;t just talk to them.  You watch them work.  Observe, and gather clues about what will serve their needs.  And when people tell you what they think they want, don&#x27;t assume that&#x27;s the product spec; treat that also as an opportunity to listen to clues that will help you understand their needs.<p>The best products are designed around how people work, not necessarily what people <i>say</i> about how they work.')
