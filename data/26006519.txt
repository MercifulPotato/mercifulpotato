Item(by='brucehoult', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612299354, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26003257, text='ARM64 and RISCV64 are so similar ISAs that whatever implementation you can do with one you can do with the other, given the same level of engineering skill and effort.<p>Apple probably spent billions to make M1. If someone made the same investment with RISC-V they could get the same results -- the main company currently pushing higher performance RISC-V (SiFive) has total funding of under $200m so they clearly don&#x27;t have billions to spend. They&#x27;ve got an announced CPU (tested in FPGA) with ARM A72 level performance. That&#x27;s only about five years of very incremental performance improvements behind (non-Apple) ARM.')
