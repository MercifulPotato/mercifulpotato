Item(by='eigenrick', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612286820, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001179, text='As a Wayland (and Sway) user;  Overall, I have loved the Wayland experience.  I am very grateful to all of the devs who&#x27;ve worked to make this happen.<p>That said, I feel that Wayland devs (and users) are just now (re)learning the ecosystem catch-22 problem.  The ecosystem hasn&#x27;t caught up with Wayland&#x27;s new architecture because the layers don&#x27;t line up the same way.  This means entirely new layers (or adapters) need to be created all the way up the stack.<p>Also, this new design and these interfaces are almost completely undocumented, so it makes it very hard for a non-waylander to step in and contribute. I made a couple attempts to fix bugs, but after wading through Github issues for 4 hours, and getting no help on IRC, I gave up.<p>Unfortunately the ecosystem is responsible for building the rest of the stack, but it&#x27;s hard, because there isn&#x27;t much in the way of documentation or examples in the ecosystem. That&#x27;s the catch. The ecosystem is expanding very slowly because there isn&#x27;t much of an ecosystem.')
