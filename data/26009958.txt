Item(by='snystrom', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612320114, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007795, text='A strong argument against this is the potential to apply selective pressure for virus variants that escape antibody binding. After the first vaccine dose, antibody titer in the blood is lower than it would be after 2 doses. Some of the new variants are better at escaping antibody binding, but with high enough antibody titer in the blood, they can still be cleared. Using only 1 dose risks not having enough antibody to eliminate an antibody-escape mutant.')
