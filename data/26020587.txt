My point is more along the lines that what stops debates in real life isn&#x27;t edginess, extreme views in and of themselves, or the lack of arguments. After all, many people enjoy the feeling of camaraderie that comes from sharing purportedly taboo topics with people they don&#x27;t know all that well yet. It&#x27;s just that what is actually abhorrent as measured by people&#x27;s actual rejection of a discussion has more to do with calling into question the status or material wealth of their own class. For example, looking at a typical high-salary liberal in the tech sector, they generally hold a mix of standard pro-equity beliefs, some more discreet randian&#x2F;ancap beliefs about separating themselves from what they see as lower classes and the meddling government, and a strong taboo surrounding an actual decrease in their social or effective capital.<p>Of course, this is all an intuitive reasoning based on anecdotal experience, so we might just be butting anecdotal realities at each other.')
