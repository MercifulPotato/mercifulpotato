Item(by='ineedasername', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612312823, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008862, text='No, most families at Princeton are not typical working class families. The median family income of a student&#x27;s family is about $180,000.<p>Yes, if you&#x27;re accepted, you really don&#x27;t have to worry about the price tag because of their financial aid. But poorer families are more likely to go to lower quality schools that may not even offer AP courses and do very little test prep. Families are also unable to afford top-tier test prep services either. On average, if you come from a poor family you&#x27;ve probably had to work harder, or be smarter, to get into Princeton.<p>I&#x27;m not saying this is a problem for Princeton to solve. I&#x27;m just saying that it&#x27;s really not an institution highly accessible to middle&#x2F;lower class. Just take for example the fact that they favor children of past alumni heavily: something like 30% of the class comes from &quot;legacy&quot; students whose parents(s) attended as well.')
