The Church further argues that God cannot be in error, but our understanding of God is often in error.<p>As a Catholic, the arguments of the church are summarized in the Catechism which is available on the vatican.va website. This is a direct rejection of utilitarianism, the argument that the ends justify the means, and it is why the Church argues against eugenics, abortion, and capital punishment.<p>As for historical arguments, the Catholic narrative is like this.  God intervened directly throughout mankind&#x27;s history and has slowly revealed himself.  First, to Abraham, then to Moses, and lastly through his divine Son.  The world of ancient Europe during Jesus&#x27; time was dominated or some might argue enslaved by Rome.
