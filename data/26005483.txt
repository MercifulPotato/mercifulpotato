Item(by='fractionalhare', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612294499, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005215, text='Banks are prone to bailouts, hedge funds are not (in the traditional sense of taxpayer money, not private investment).<p>Many banks control trillions of dollars. Hedge funds almost always have less than $50B, usually by one or two orders of magnitude. If a bank fails, it&#x27;s actually a problem for the government. If a hedge fund fails, it&#x27;s barely a blip. If the limited partner was smart, it will also only cause them a single digit percentage decrease in their portfolio.<p>Hedge funds can amplify a systemic risk, but they would not really be targets for a bailout, the banks would be.')
