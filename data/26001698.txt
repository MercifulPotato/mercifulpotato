Item(by='soylentcola', descendants=None, kids=[26001949], score=None, time=1612278142, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001391, text='I&#x27;m sure I&#x27;ve read this elsewhere and didn&#x27;t come up with the distinction on my own, but my thoughts on this are:<p>Theism and atheism refer to a belief or lack of belief in the existence of god(s).<p>Gnosticism and agnosticism refer to a (claim of) knowledge of the existence of god(s).<p>I&#x27;d consider myself an atheist because I don&#x27;t personally believe there are gods, but I&#x27;m also agnostic regarding the possibility.<p>I don&#x27;t have enough info to rule out the possibility of a deity or deities, but I don&#x27;t have any reason to believe there are any (or at the very least, not any that have a direct and personal interaction with humanity).')
