Item(by='rtkwe', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612378972, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017423, text='The compression is a good thing, it drives the power to the turbine in the first place and also acts like a spring so it&#x27;ll smooth out the energy impulse from the waves.<p>One of the big problems with other wave energy systems is the stress from waves is pretty peaky so the generators have to spin up and down a lot and then also deal with storms. In this the air acts like a cushion to the peaks of energy from waves and the whole power generation side can be bypassed in a storm even by just opening another air path.')
