Too much air and there isn&#x27;t enough fuel to sustain combustion, too much fuel and there isn&#x27;t enough air.  There are plenty of people who have demonstrated dropping a match into a gas can and the match goes out with only a slight poof of a flame. Full gas can can sometimes survive the building they are in burning down (if the can is sealed - doesn&#x27;t melt - there is never enough air inside to light the fuel)<p>That isn&#x27;t to say gasoline isn&#x27;t dangerous. However it isn&#x27;t quite as dangerous as you seem to think.')
