Item(by='thaumasiotes', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612446167, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26025037, text='&gt; It may not try to reduce dependencies.<p>This one&#x27;s pretty straightforward; natural selection has two very common approaches to dependencies:<p>1. If the presence of something is not a reliable feature of the environment, dependence on that something will be ruthlessly weeded out.<p>2. If the presence of something is a reliable feature of the environment, dependence on that something will be created and grow to permeate most existing systems.<p>The first effect has a lot of popular awareness; the second doesn&#x27;t. They&#x27;re both important.')
