Item(by='fabrice_d', descendants=None, kids=[26022390], score=None, time=1612395168, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26020191, text='Building a new gecko-based browser is not the same thing as &quot;embedding gecko&quot;, at least on desktop platforms.<p>Creating a new gecko-based product is not very hard, even if you will hit some cases of the platform depending on firefox that you&#x27;ll have to workaround or borrow.<p>Creating a full new blink-based product is as difficult actually. Most chromium derivatives take the simpler road of forking chromium, not building a new blink-based browser (eg. Brave maintains a pile of patches).<p>I don&#x27;t understand why you would expect a &quot;firefoxium&quot;: there is no such thing because there is no proprietary version of the open source Firefox. If you fork Firefox, you&#x27;re in the same boat as people forking chromium in terms of complexity.')
