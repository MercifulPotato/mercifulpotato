Item(by='pdonis', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612331102, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26010606, text='<i>&gt; I am debating against someone who claims free will is what will stop child trafficking.</i><p>I have made no such claim. I have said that, on net, a society where people&#x27;s right to make free choices is respected will have less suffering and more good things than a society where it isn&#x27;t. But that doesn&#x27;t mean no bad things will ever happen in the former type of society. Nor is respecting others&#x27; right to make free choices the same as &quot;free will&quot; by itself.<p>If you can&#x27;t see why respecting other people&#x27;s right to make free choices is inconsistent with child trafficking, isn&#x27;t it obvious? Child traffickers don&#x27;t respect the right to make free choices of the children they traffic in. So getting more people to respect other people&#x27;s right to make free choices would obviously reduce the prevalence of child trafficking.')
