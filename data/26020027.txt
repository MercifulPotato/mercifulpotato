I’ve got three bookmarks and a few notes here and everything else is basically lost.”<p>Myself and developers I&#x27;ve worked with suffer from this. Information is easily searchable so we never bother to memorize the function signature for things like substring(). Is it the (start index, end index) or the (start index, length)? This repeated googling of the same question (sometimes on a daily basis) is draining.<p>I have a belief that this phenomenon is one reason HN bashes on tech interviews. Developers find that they actually have very little retained knowledge when placed in front of a whiteboard. They are dependent on Google and SO.<p>I don&#x27;t think taking notes in this NEW browser will alleviate this problem. Instead of Google you now depend on notes. Personally I recommend Mastery Learning[1]. It involves more effort but produces much more confident developers.<p>When confronted with a new technology suggestion, don&#x27;t forget to ask &quot;What problem does this solve?&quot; Maybe there is a low-tech solution that&#x27;s better suited.<p>[1] <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;Mastery_learning" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;Mastery_learning</a>')
