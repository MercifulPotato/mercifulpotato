Item(by='Spivak', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612284019, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002467, text='Wayland isn&#x27;t bad design, at least not in the way you&#x27;re describing it. I feel like there&#x27;s some confusion about what Wayland actually is. It&#x27;s a document that describes how surfaces (&quot;windows&quot;) should talk to a compositor (&quot;display sever&quot;). That&#x27;s it. In a parallel universe Kwin and Mutter would be X11 display servers and we would have all the same issues. Nothing is stopping the existence of a single extensible compositor to rule them all and be the base on which every DE&#x2F;WM is based but it hasn&#x27;t happened yet.<p>When people talk about &quot;pushing stuff to the DE&quot; what they mean is the Wayland project aggressively scoping the Wayland protocol to drawing surfaces and handling input events. They&#x27;re not trying to become a generic desktop message bus for everything an application might want to do -- that&#x27;s literally what dbus is.')
