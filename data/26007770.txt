Efficiencies of scale mean there&#x27;s a natural pressure towards consolidation. The terminal state is always one blob that owns everything.<p>Once they do, they can smash any new upstarts, by undercutting prices, or by just buying them too.<p>&gt; A corporation generally doesn&#x27;t shoot dissatisfied customers.<p>Sure does shoot dissatisfied union leaders though [1] and enslave children to farm cocoa [2], and take down a democratically elected government kickstarting a 36 year long civil war [3].
