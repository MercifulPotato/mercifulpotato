The same applies to Huawei. The fact that some countries have good or bad infrastructure is not related to what equipment they use (at this point in time).<p>The end result being that quality of telco infrastructure is not an argument for or against any particular maker of equipment, because those two things are unrelated.<p>Any to underline why they are unrelated - simply because ISPs are profit-driven businesses and not tech playgrounds, so they will not invest in the latest and greatest equipment that XYZ&#x2F;Huawei has to offer, instead they will milk the existing equipment and infrastructure dry and then switch to the cheapest new next-gen-access &quot;thing&quot; that exists for the next 20+ years. This does not mean that XYZ&#x2F;Huawei doesn&#x27;t have anything better to offer, it just means that the telcos do not want it.')
