It&#x27;s probably not worth assigning an investigator.<p>If you&#x27;re HUGE and scamming billions, the ROI is there <i>but</i> you also have the capabilities to defend yourself, obfuscate things, etc so it&#x27;s unlikely they can destroy you. Therefore, it&#x27;s not one investigator but a team with forensic accountants, potentially informants, etc.<p>For everyone else, the danger is in the middle. Where you have enough that you can lose a life-destroying amount <i>but</i> you don&#x27;t have the means to protect yourself. That is a great target for investigators.<p>In short, as you gain more, be careful and have a good accountant who follows the rules to the best of their ability.. until you can make the rules.<p>Oh crap.')
