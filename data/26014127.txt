Item(by='PragmaticPulp', descendants=None, kids=[26021511, 26014178], score=None, time=1612364326, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26013751, text='Ironically, tying a cryptocurrency to a real-world use case like paying for storage is the fastest way to throw cold water on price speculation.<p>Once people can price a coin in terms of real-world assets like storage, it becomes easy to see when the coin is overvalued.<p>IMO, this is why Bitcoiners aren’t actually interested in making Bitcoin easy to use for payments. They only want two use cases: Buying and holding. Selling or spending will only drive the price down.')
