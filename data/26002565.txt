Item(by='ericbarrett', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612281746, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001434, text='Protip for the US: If you ever need an attorney, and don’t know one or have a good reference, contact your local bar office. They will ask basic details of your case and then refer you to a local practice who can handle it for a tiny retainer ($50 when I did it).<p>Also, lawyers are like any other contractor. You can get bids and don’t have to stick with one who isn’t listening, assuming you’re not already mid-trial.')
