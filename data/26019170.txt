CPU compute power is no longer growing as much (at least for individual cores).<p>But one thing that&#x27;s still doubling on a regular basis is memory capacity of all kinds (RAM, SSD, etc) and bandwidth of all kinds (PCIe lanes, networking, etc). This divide is getting large and will only continue to increase.<p>Which brings me to my main point:<p>You can&#x27;t be serializing&#x2F;deserializing data on the CPU. What you want is to have the CPU coordinate the SSD to copy chunks directly -and as is- to the NIC&#x2F;app&#x2F;etc.<p>Short of having your RAM doing compute work*, you would be leaving performance on the table.<p>----<p>* Which is starting to appear (<a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.upmem.com&#x2F;technology&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.upmem.com&#x2F;technology&#x2F;</a>), but that&#x27;s not quite there yet.')
