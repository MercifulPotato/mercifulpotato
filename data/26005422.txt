Item(by='PeterisP', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612294189, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000456, text='I would consider world peace &quot;through mutual respect, tolerance and trust amongst nations&quot; as inherently impossible for homo sapiens as we are.<p>A world peace might be plausible through a single world government that can <i>enforce</i> a world peace and resolution of diverging interests and conflicts without war, i.e. with some &quot;policing&quot; mechanism instead of relying on people not escalating through sheer goodwill. <i>That</i> is possible - world peace based on the expectation that any potential violators of that world peace would face retaliation and lose in that conflict. Si vis pacem, para bellum - that can work, if it can be established.<p>Or, alternatively, a world peace through mutual respect and tolerance may happen if at some time in future our civilization consists of substantially altered individuals - genetic alteration, chemical brainwashing, computerized circuits in brains, something that changes the way our motivation works on a fundamental level - but not for people as we are.<p>Otherwise, as long as any people and groups of people have unmet desires (not merely needs) and diverging interests, conflicts will happen, and as long as violence is a practical option for achieving goals (i.e. there&#x27;s not a threat&#x2F;expectation of successful powerful resistance to that violence) it will be considered and used if mere threat of that violence isn&#x27;t sufficient.')
