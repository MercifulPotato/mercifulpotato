Item(by='xyzzyz', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612401038, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26021324, text='Why? First doses go to highest bidders, which might as well be government, or it might wait a while until more doses are available and the bids get lower. The point here is to incentivize the vaccine manufacturers to race against one another, to produce as many doses as quickly as possible, in order to take advantage of the early high bidders.<p>Instead, the current arrangement, where the governments claim first dibs on hundreds of millions of doses, while paying relative peanuts compared to the insane amounts of value those vaccines could produce, do very little to incentivize the companies to spare no expense to accelerate the production. Once they satisfy the obligations they have with governments, remaining buyers won&#x27;t want to pay much either, so why bother?')
