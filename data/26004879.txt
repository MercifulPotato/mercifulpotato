Item(by='spiderxxxx', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612291583, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002911, text='I&#x27;ve done something similar without first learning about Markov Chains. One of my more interesting experiments was creating messed-up laws. I fed it the constitution and alice in wonderland, and it made the most surreal laws. The great thing about them is they don&#x27;t need to know about language. You could make one to create images, another to create names for cities. I made one to create &#x27;pronounceable passwords&#x27;. It took the top 1000 words of a dictionary, and then it would spit out things which could potentially be words, of any length. Of course, the pronounceability of a word like Shestatond is debatable.')
