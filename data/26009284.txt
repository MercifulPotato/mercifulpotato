Item(by='striking', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612313689, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25994511, text='It can&#x27;t be atomic if it&#x27;s done `CONCURRENTLY`. You can&#x27;t even do that in a transaction, Postgres will tell you it won&#x27;t work. By using `CONCURRENTLY`, you&#x27;re making it clear that you will handle atomicity yourself, and as a result Postgres needs to provide you with the tools to do it.<p>Want real atomicity? Don&#x27;t use `CONCURRENTLY`.')
