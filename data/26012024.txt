Item(by='Broken_Hippo', descendants=None, kids=[26012290], score=None, time=1612344715, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26011891, text='This is grossly oversimplified. You can have good sleep and have tech in the bedroom.<p>I&#x27;ve enjoyed not having to buy an alarm clock for many, many years. A phone allows me to change the noise I wake up to and set multiple alarms at random intervals. I now rarely sleep through the alarms - no more hoping that the alarm clock had a good noise and no more suddenly finding out I no longer wake to it (and yes, I sleep through them: They&#x27;d wake up others in the house. Yes, this is even with good sleep hygiene.)<p>Music comes from tech, too. This is especially helpful to cover up small noises in my house or the apartments around me: The small noises disturb me more when falling asleep than a radio&#x2F;television going on. (The same has been true of studying: A quiet library is an experience of interruption. So. Many. Noises.)<p>I&#x27;ve lived more than one place where my tech had to be in my bedroom if I wanted any sort of privacy while using it.<p>Much better to be diligent about using it. It sucks that the poster seemed to be doing this, and I understand their complaint. It seems to me that it is a software oversight, but like they said, &quot;disturb&quot; is the norm, so we probably won&#x27;t get real relief until standards change.')
