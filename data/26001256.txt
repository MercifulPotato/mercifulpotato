Item(by='jefftk', descendants=None, kids=[26003416, 26008270, 26001332, 26005822, 26001559, 26001464, 26002901, 26001675, 26002131, 26001545, 26001452, 26004239, 26006143, 26005039], score=None, time=1612275972, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000405, text='Sites are not detecting a portrait screen and deciding that they&#x27;re displaying on a phone.  Someone who switches their monitor to portrait, or resizes a window to be taller than it is wide, is still going to get the desktop view.<p>In this case the author is scaling their resolution, and so their browser is presenting itself to sites as 720px wide.  Sites see that and show a layout optimized for a screen of that width.  Those sites are acting <i>correctly</i>.<p>The author&#x27;s proposed solution is for sites to use DPI instead, but this would thwart the preferences of users who use scaling because they want the whole site to look bigger.')
