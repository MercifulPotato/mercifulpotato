Item(by='walljm', descendants=None, kids=[26009329], score=None, time=1612301999, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001696, text='I&#x27;m not sure that is true.<p>&quot;evidence of things not seen&quot; is just poetic language for &quot;faith is the thing that bridges the gap between what you are sure of and what you aren&#x27;t&quot;.<p>An assumption is an act of faith.  Its a decision to accept something that you don&#x27;t have proof of.  The dictionary lists belief as a synonym for assumption.<p>I think the mistake many people make, including Christians, is in believing that faith is something other than choosing to accept as true what you can&#x27;t prove.  There isn&#x27;t anything particularly mystical about faith conceptually.  Its just that in religious circles faith is a much discussed component.')
