But only as long as its presence doesn‘t inconvenience them in any way.<p>People hate the GDPR pop ups. Most are badly designed (probably on purpose) and make it easy to accept all and difficult to customize&#x2F;reject all. Most people are just going to click accept because it makes the box disappear faster.<p>Let‘s say we have the new Apple opt-in rules for Facebook. There‘s a pop-up some day asking users: „do you want facebook to be able to track you?“ many people will say no. But only because it‘s really easy&#x2F;frictionless and the app presumably functions as normal.<p>So primarily users want to do want users want to do. They don‘t care about tracking&#x2F;ads as long as avoiding them isn‘t completely simple.')
