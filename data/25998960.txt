Item(by='tom_mellior', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612258589, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25996888, text='What the article doesn&#x27;t acknowledge is that there are many ways to force compilation of your code. You can change the threshold for the number of executions before the JIT is called. You can run scripts as part of your deployment that exercise the paths you are interested in. So yes, it&#x27;s true that &quot;this code runs very infrequently, but I still care about its latency&quot; is not what JVMs might be optimized towards. But it&#x27;s possible to understand the issues and solve them.')
