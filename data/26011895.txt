Item(by='ygra', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612343090, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005919, text='In the limited TypeScript I&#x27;ve done (only a few months so far), every single time I thought &quot;oh well, let&#x27;s leave this at any for now&quot; or &quot;ah, crap, ts-ignore until I can think of a better way&quot; or even &quot;let&#x27;s disable strict-null-checks for now, as fixing all those cases is too much work&quot; has resulted in broken code down the line because assumptions that are not caught by the types have been invalidated. So I tend to err on more or better types for now. Some things can&#x27;t really be improved of course, but that&#x27;s where experience comes in over time in recognizing those cases.')
