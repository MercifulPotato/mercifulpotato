Item(by='DanieleProcida', descendants=None, kids=[26011789, 26009687], score=None, time=1612298168, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004719, text='Thanks for the kind words!<p>You are quite right, there are various things that aren&#x27;t included in the scheme, that ought to be included in a complete set of documentation.<p>Other examples could be: release notes (though you could include them in reference), contribution guide (though it could be part of how-to) and of course, the Introduction you mention.<p>Mostly I think that these things probably belong outside the scheme, in the same way that say an appendix or an introduction falls outside main body of text in a book.<p>An introduction (in documentation) is basically marketing: a reason for someone to keep reading.<p>For me the scheme is not so much a complete list of every kind of thing that must be written (I think it goes without saying that you need to create useful contents tables for example) as a guide to how I should be writing and a reminder why I am writing.')
