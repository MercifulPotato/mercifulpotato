Item(by='CGamesPlay', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612236354, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25992735, text='I&#x27;ve recently increased my usage of VLC when I discovered that there were no good podcast apps for macOS. Specifically, I listen to serial podcasts in batches, so there&#x27;s a very clear to-listen list and the episodes can be quite old. For some reason, even the open source RSS readers refuse to accept that someone might want to keep a long backlog of articles (podcasts are RSS feed where the article has an MP3 file, in case you didn&#x27;t know).<p>VLC natively can display the RSS feeds, but sadly doesn&#x27;t have a notion of what is &quot;already listened&quot;. So I save the episode list to a text file and have a script that pops a line off the top, saves the MP3, and I listen directly to the MP3 in VLC.<p>So, if you know how I can automatically save both the track position and track number in an m3u playlist, I&#x27;d love to hear the secret!')
