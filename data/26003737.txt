Zaraz is a little later, like &quot;i&#x27;ll do it right away&quot; - &quot;zaraz to zrobię&quot;. &quot;Już to robię&quot; - &quot;I&#x27;m doing it now&quot;. &quot;Zaraz&quot; means &quot;I&#x27;m almost finished with my current task and will do that thing next&quot;.')
