Item(by='phtrivier', descendants=None, kids=[26016888], score=None, time=1612350321, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26011968, text='My theory would be that that:<p>* &quot;disturbable by default&quot; is a carry-over from landline-only time<p>* calls where rarer in landline-only time because they (sometimes) cost _money_<p>* calls where rarer in particular times (eg late at night)  because of a social norm<p>* calls in the middle of the day where probably rarer, but also much easier to ignore because you were not at home and your phone simply run in the void<p>* calls were mostly done by human beings<p>Now, the &quot;phone calls from a human being who respects social norms or that I simply never hear&quot; have been replaced by &quot;automated notifications from bots in a piece of plastic that&#x27;s constantly in my pocket, or text messages from people that expects me to be reachable at any time.&quot;<p>So, I would argue that &quot;disturb&quot; was always the default mode, but the disturbance is now high enough that we might want to reconsider.<p>Although, the old rules like &quot;don&#x27;t call me after 11pm unless it&#x27;s an emergency&quot; would be hard to translate to &quot;don&#x27;t text me after 11pm unless it&#x27;s an emergency even though it&#x27;s currently our only way to communicate because lockdown&quot; ;)')
