DT-880 is certainly light and comfortable but it&#x27;s kind of bright and I&#x27;ve heard the other models are less neutral than that.<p>It also sounds quite detailed in the treble range, but if you listen to something like Sundara you realize the detail doesn&#x27;t actually exist and it&#x27;s more like a sharpening filter.
