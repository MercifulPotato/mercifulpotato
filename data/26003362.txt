Item(by='chrismorgan', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612284921, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000929, text='I’d express it much more strongly than you have: doing what this article suggests is catastrophically wrong. For layout purposes you <i>must not</i> look at physical resolution. Physical resolution should only be used so that you can render on scaled displays at higher quality, e.g. by choosing a higher-resolution image, or by scaling your canvas by devicePixelRatio so you can draw more precise lines.<p>The proposed solution is not just no better, but far, far worse.')
