That attracts people that have no talent&#x2F;love for the domain. People that are in it only for the money. The bigger the difference in earnings compared with the general population, the more of this you&#x27;ll get.\nI live in such a country. Offerings of &#x27;mentorship&#x27; and training (in three months or so) for good money are plenty. They make a lot of &#x27;programmers&#x27; that know almost nothing and you&#x27;ll have a good chance of getting those if you outsource blindly (not if you verify them, ask them for a university diploma on the domain and so on).')
