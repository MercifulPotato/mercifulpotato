Item(by='scarmig', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612310403, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008754, text='GCP doesn&#x27;t need to beat AWS. It doesn&#x27;t even need to increase market share, really: the cloud market as a whole is growing rapidly, and more money is more money, regardless of bragging rights. It just needs to get to profitability.<p>There&#x27;s also a strategic aspect of clipping the wings of AWS by providing a BATNA in the big deal negotiations.
