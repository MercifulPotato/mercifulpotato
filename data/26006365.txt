When I used Arch I would edit a handful of configs trying to set up something new or tweak a setting and I could not get back. I would need to take notes or just try my best (I have force-reinstalled packages countless times).<p>I&#x27;m also waiting for the parts of my new desktop to come in and I&#x27;m excited that all it takes is cloning my config repo and applying to get to exactly the same setup.
