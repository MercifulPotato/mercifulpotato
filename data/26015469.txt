Item(by='sandworm101', descendants=None, kids=[26016647], score=None, time=1612370570, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26015209, text='The speed of a chemical fire, be it from a fuel spill or chemical battery, is limited by chemistry.  Even a fully involved burning fuel tank is limited by the availability of oxygen, the math involves wind speeds and circulation of gas over a flame.  Short-circuit a normal battery and the math is all about heat and chemical bonds.   Short-circuit a capacitor and the math become quantum, involving numbers like the speed of light.')
