CSV is an OK text format, but parsing it is really slow because of string escaping. The files it produces are also pretty big since it&#x27;s text.<p>Arrow is really fast to parse (up to 1000x faster than CSV), supports data compression, enough data-types to be useful, and deals with metadata well.
