Item(by='fxtentacle', descendants=None, kids=[26006665], score=None, time=1612295086, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25993616, text='This is an advertisement piece written by a NODAR advisor about how cool their &quot;Hammerhead&quot; system is.<p>TLDR: It&#x27;s stereo cameras with wide baseline and software compensation for vibrations&#x2F;camera shake.<p>That said, I am pretty disappointed that there&#x27;s no mention of the actual problems that people working with stereo cameras in cars have, which is that optical matching usually doesn&#x27;t have a unique solution and that reflections ruin everything. The latter especially gets worse the wider you make the stereo baseline, so I&#x27;d bet you that NODAR will fail badly when there&#x27;s ice on the road.<p>That combination of false correspondences due to reflections and AI in-painting to produce the illusion of a complete dataset when it is impossible to see depth everywhere, might be quite dangerous. We laugh at Skydio drones for crashing into windows, puddles, and lakes because their AI (with 6 cameras) gets confused by reflecting things. I&#x27;d find it a lot less funny if that was my car crashing.')
