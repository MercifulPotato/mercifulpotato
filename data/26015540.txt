Item(by='crazypython', descendants=None, kids=[26015854, 26016702], score=None, time=1612370857, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26015440, text='My teachers almost universally prefer Canvas Instructure (AGPLv3 free software) to Google Classroom. It gives them far more power and freedom to build courses.<p>Granted, it has a terrible UX for students. But since it is free software, it gave me freedom to build my own interface by providing an API– unlike Google Classroom– which I used to build a better interface to view my due quizzes. I can then share this software with my fellow students, promoting the values of sharing.')
