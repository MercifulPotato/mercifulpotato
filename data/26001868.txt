Item(by='superkuh', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612278993, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001697, text='The idea is to rotate the masks, not to throw them away. You wear one then let it sit alone, without being touched, for a week or more. The next day you wear a new mask then do the same to it. A week later you can go back and wear the original mask again.<p>Throwing them away after 1 use would be silly. I personally have enough masks, and few enough indoors engagements, to let my N95 sit for a full month between re-uses.')
