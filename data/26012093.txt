Item(by='Blikkentrekker', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612345421, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26011905, text='These are not allomorphs; these are the minor exceptions where one grapheme can refer to two different morphemes. — in each of those examples, the meaning changes with a different pronunciation.<p>Allomorphs do not change meaning, and typically occur in complementary distribution. A good example would be the English words “a” and “an” being allomorphs, “a” can only occur where “an” can not, and <i>vice versā</i>, and both have the exact same meaning and function.')
