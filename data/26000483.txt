Item(by='mhsabbagh', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612271042, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999071, text='As the developer of Green Recorder [1], the second example referred to in this page, I have to say that it sounds my abandonment statement is taken out of context.<p>In order to support recording on different Wayland compositors (without having to develop tons of other middle layers by yourself), one would need to simply activate their built-in screencasting tool according the specified user settings. This took me a bit of time to figure out for GNOME, and I had to fill a bug-report to speak to one of the compositor developers to understand how to do that, as no documentation was available at all. Even so, some hidden bugs appeared (Quality wasn&#x27;t too good, audio had to be recorded separately over ffmpeg, and then both audio and video had to be merged together in one file, V9 had a bug that consumes 100% of CPU on some hardware, so we had to use V8 by default... etc).<p>In order to go further, I had to do the same thing for KWin on Wayland, Sway and other compositors out there in the market and then integrate them into my program. I had to find some workarounds for any bugs that may occur.<p>Later on, some changes for ffmpeg API broke the Xorg recording (Green Recorder used ffmpeg to record on all desktop environments on Xorg), so I had to do more testing now for multiple versions of ffmpeg and on which distro do they work and don&#x27;t work.<p>So I just gave up, as I simply didn&#x27;t find any particular reason to continue doing that since the only amount of support I received on my then-opened Patreon was $20 per month at its max.<p>But it shouldn&#x27;t be said that Wayland can not support screencasting or that it breaks screencasting. It is possible, and the previously mentioned issues are actually on the compositors&#x27; developers side, not the protocol.<p>1: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;mhsabbagh&#x2F;green-recorder" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;mhsabbagh&#x2F;green-recorder</a>')
