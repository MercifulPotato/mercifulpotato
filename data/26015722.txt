Item(by='Jtsummers', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612371526, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26015465, text='Well, the other problem with Discord is that people have to opt-in to the groupings. Circles were publisher driven. If you were in my CS Friends circle, that was just where I categorized you. It was not akin to a FB group or a Discord server. In those, you have to accept some kind of invitation into the grouping, and you&#x27;re made to communicate with everyone in the group&#x2F;server. Circles were one way, from me to the circle. For a circle member to publish to all the same people they&#x27;d need to be connected to all of them directly in G+ and create an identical circle.<p>This is the same issue as group messaging in most instant message type systems (including SMS&#x2F;MMS). It&#x27;s forcing a full connection between all participants, when really we may only want a fan-out structure.<p>I haven&#x27;t used it much (not as many people on it in my social circles) but WhatsApp&#x27;s broadcast lists are like G+ circles in this regard.')
