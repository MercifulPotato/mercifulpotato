Every problem or small improvement is a potential business opportunity or startup idea and I see them everywhere. My experience as a software consultant likely contributes to this. It&#x27;s my job to go talk to customers, understand their problem and propose a solution.<p>If you want to just build stuff for yourself, go work for a software consulting firm for a while.  It should be a smaller one that&#x27;s interested in tackling smaller jobs.  This way, you&#x27;ll interact with more potential customers.  You&#x27;ll learn a ton about their businesses, because after all; they hired your company to solve their problems. Save as much money as you can.<p>When you have enough money saved up, you can afford to quit your job and focus on something you that interests you because it&#x27;s fun or an amazing opportunity.  I&#x27;ve done this a couple of times so far.  Right now I am splitting my time between a startup and a part time job.<p>I am really glad I have the part time job.  Not so much for the money, but for the socialization aspects.')
