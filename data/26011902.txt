Unlike any, which is explicit, exception caught in catch block is implicit.<p>Personally I prefer to wrap catches into a specific type&#x2F;symbol `UnknownError { data: unknown }`, and discriminated union to indicate success&#x2F;failure on return. fp-ts (<a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;gcanti&#x2F;fp-ts" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;github.com&#x2F;gcanti&#x2F;fp-ts</a>) is my go-to library for this')
