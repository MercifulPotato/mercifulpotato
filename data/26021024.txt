Item(by='fxtentacle', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612397788, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26020872, text='C++ with vcpkg is a bliss to work with. Depending on which triplet you configure, it can build either static or dynamic libraries.<p>And CMake will take care of transitive dependencies for static libraries for you. Just declare it as a PUBLIC link dependency and all downstream projects will link against it automatically.<p>What the article fails to mention is that dynamic libraries can also be an attack vector. Users can replace a .so with a variant that does something you don&#x27;t expect. For statically linked apps, it is much easier to ensure your code has not been tampered with.<p>Also, the CMake integration in CLion is great. &quot;How did things get this bad?&quot; What are you talking about? It&#x27;s a great time to be a C++ programmer! The tools are finally stable and powerful (and not either MSVC or ICC) and there&#x27;s lots of good libraries to choose from.')
