Item(by='dlbucci', descendants=None, kids=[26008227, 26009031, 26008868, 26008542], score=None, time=1612305888, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005330, text='I agree with most of these, but I pretty strongly disagree with #10 (don&#x27;t use `!= null`). The reasoning is that you can use `null` and `undefined` to mean different things, like `null` being &quot;no first name&quot; and `undefined` meaning &quot;haven&#x27;t asked yet&quot;. I agree that strict TS lets you do stuff like that fairly easily, but I think this is a really bad idea, because there&#x27;s nothing that would indicate to a reader of that code the meaning of `null` vs `undefined` for those properties.<p>I feel in that case you should have some type union of `&quot;notAsked&quot; | &quot;none&quot; | { t: &quot;some&quot;, v: &quot;Name&quot; }`, because that would be more clear of what each possible value means. Let `null` and `undefined` be synonyms for `nil`.')
