Item(by='madrox', descendants=None, kids=[26021699], score=None, time=1612403810, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26020104, text='Playing devil&#x27;s advocate, how is Google &amp; Chrome different from, say, Apple &amp; App Store? Thinking about iOS like a browser, arguably what Privacy Sandbox does is not that different from iOS 14&#x27;s AppTrackingTransparency Framework. Both require the operator of the internals not to abuse their position for there to be fair play.<p>The most generous thing I could say is that it <i>feels</i> different. However, Apple has a stronger hold on iOS than Google has on the web. On the web, at least you could switch to another browser to view the same content. Switching off iPhone requires a whole new ecosystem and hardware.<p>I hope people who argue for a certain treatment of Google in this case are at least consistent in the Apple case.')
