Item(by='Green_man', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612290464, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004168, text='As others have stated, it&#x27;s usually more about compatability.  Most (nonportable) headphone amps will have a 1&#x2F;4&quot; plug rather than the 3.5mm, and it makes more sense from the &quot;female&quot; amp side to convert the larger 1&#x2F;4&quot; --&gt; 3.5mm than the other way around. Many home &quot;audiophile&quot; headphones I&#x27;ve used (Audeze or Beyerdynamic) actually have the smaller 3.5mm jack, but because they are so focused on home use (bulky, open backed, expensive, and not very shovable into a backpack), users rarely use them connected to a phone or laptop.')
