People C, D, and E code it.<p>For most interesting-but-not-exceptional ideas you could replace all of these people with others.<p><i>If you actually ran this an experiment</i> in a Monte Carlo-but-real kind of a way, you&#x27;d find some collaborations would do well, some would do badly, some would fail completely, a few might explode (in a good way).<p>How do you quantify the value of the relative contributions?')
