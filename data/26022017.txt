I find articles like this, which are written in a style so the average person can (mis)understand them, but the content requires a lot of technical knowledge.<p>&gt; You do not have a probability of being struck by lightning. Nobody does.<p>I would say the average person does in fact intuitively understand that &quot;the probability of being struck by lightning&quot; refers to the chance of a random person being struck in a given time period (if given) or a lifetime, not themselves specifically (and definitely not if they walk around flying kites in open fields during thunderstorms).<p>If I told someone the chance of them lifting a rock and finding a gold coin is 1*10^(-x)%, they would not lift the rock and proclaim that the coin failed to materialize, they would understand that the actual chance was zero.<p>The patient at the start of the article not asking what are the chances this will work for me, they are asking &quot;for men aged 18-25&quot; what percentage of them are successfully treated with this product.')
