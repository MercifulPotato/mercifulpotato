Item(by='sixQuarks', descendants=None, kids=[26021431, 26024416, 26020692, 26021134], score=None, time=1612391831, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018098, text='I love curation projects.<p>Here&#x27;s a tip if you want to get people to submit more videos.  Include a &quot;Submit Video&quot; button in each area.  When someone knows of a great video for a category, having to sign up for an account is a huge hassle and de-motivator. So just turn the onboarding upside down.  Make it easy to submit a link first.<p>Once the link is submitted, THEN ask them to fill out the Username&#x2F;password&#x2F;email.  At that point, they&#x27;re already invested in the process, already submitted the link, and it&#x27;s a lot easier to finish.')
