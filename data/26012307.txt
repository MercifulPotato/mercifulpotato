Heart palpitations, had to wear a Holter monitor for a week, the works, but everything checked out fine... Skip a few years and then decided to buy my first house. I&#x27;d been staying up for tons of hours trying to bank a bunch of cash for the down payment. Was working on a project that was make-or-break for a local startup, the guy before me didn&#x27;t care enough, but I wouldn&#x27;t let it fail, stayed up for 4 days building it and BOOM, crippling anxiety again. Different though, couldn&#x27;t take full breaths of air this time. But in both cases I wasn&#x27;t getting enough sleep. Got some sleep, talked to my doctor, then my dad, and then I was fine. Used to stay up for 2-3 days during college all the time so I really didn&#x27;t think much of it. Sleep is huge.')
