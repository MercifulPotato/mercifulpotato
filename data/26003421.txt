Item(by='mbreese', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612285103, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001558, text='Like most tech, I don&#x27;t think the thing to think about is the lifetime of the device. <i>Really</i> nice headphones that are &quot;vintage&quot;(?) exist, as evidenced by the comments here. Leaving aside the fact that HN is notorious for having a strong segment of nearly any *-phile group...<p>The question is rarely the lifetime of the device. It&#x27;s usually the lifetime of the interface. Or in this case, a headphone jack. 1&#x2F;8&quot;, 1&#x2F;4&quot;, etc... <i>This</i> is what normally gets obsoleted rather than the device itself. For headphones, the big switch is from wired to wireless. And I think that&#x27;s where you&#x27;ll see the shift. Yes, you can get a bluetooth adapter for traditional headphones, but they aren&#x27;t great, and if you have audiophile wired headphones, you won&#x27;t be happy with the sound. And so, the device won&#x27;t be obsoleted because they fail, but rather the preferred interface changes to something that&#x27;s incompatible. Maybe audio was lucky that there were adapters available for the first shift from 1&#x2F;4&quot; to 1&#x2F;8&quot;...<p>In this regard, I think the audio world is just catching up to their other brethren in the tech world.')
