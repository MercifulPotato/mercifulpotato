Large enough commissions do in fact allow shorting in the form of insurance. The same is true of strawberry farmers &amp; landlords at various places on the size distribution.<p>One of the complaints my farmers market strawberry vendors have is that they don’t have access to the same finance agreements that large producers have. Part of that is because it’s not cost effective enough to short them.')
