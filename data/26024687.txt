Item(by='throwaway2245', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612440677, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26020318, text='&gt; The knowledge base should be primarily in your head.<p>Making it easier to store and recall useful information, without using your brain, is a good thing that has fuelled progress in recent decades.<p>The impermanence of the internet requires you to store useful information in your brain (you can&#x27;t guarantee that it will be stored for when you need it), when really it would be better stored externally.<p>The fact that the internet is impermanent encourages you to treat it as something you need to siphon information from (jam all the data into your head), rather than a safe store that you can depend on.')
