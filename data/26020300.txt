Getting rid of Medicare and Medicaid, replacing them with entirely private systems. The gold standard, going back to it. Cutting spending (actually attempting to do it, not just paying rare lip service to it). Getting more aggressive with the war on drugs. Increasing punishment for crime, putting people into prison for longer sentences.<p>It was George W Bush&#x27;s administration that implemented the successful Housing First program for homelessness assistance, which would have been considered an exceptionally bad welfare state program by the 1980s Republicans.
