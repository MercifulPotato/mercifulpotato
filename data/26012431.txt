Item(by='mytailorisrich', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612348737, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012146, text='In general IP addresses are not personal data in themselves because the controller has no means to link them to individuals: If I operate a website I can log the IP addresses of visitors but I have no way of identifying those visitors (unless e.g. they are customers with accounts and can log in).<p>It is certainly good practice to treat logs as if they were personal data when it comes to handling and securing them but whether they are in fact personal data needs to be considered for each specific case.')
