Sweden&#x27;s death rate is 1,144 deaths per million population. That&#x27;s a bit less than Spain&#x27;s, which is 1,263. But nobody is saying we should follow Spain&#x27;s strategy.<p>By comparison, Germany, with eight times Sweden&#x27;s population, has a death rate of under 700 per million. Canada is 531.
