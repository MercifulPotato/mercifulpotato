Item(by='Someone', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612251004, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25995391, text='So, do you want to have <i>addIntegers</i>, <i>addUnsignedLongs</i>, <i>addFloats</i>, etc. too?<p>You would need to have those or separate operators in some strongly typed languages that do not automatically coerce ints to floats, etc.<p>Also, for floats, <i>a</i>+<i>b</i> == <i>b</i>+<i>a</i>, but you don’t necessarily have <i>a</i>+<i>b</i>+<i>c</i> == <i>c</i>+<i>b</i>+<i>a</i>. So, if you’re strict in that “should not alter the result regardless of the types”, you would have to forbid using <i>+</i> on floats in some more complex expressions.<p>Finally, I don’t see how <i>“operator overloading […] encourages programmers to shove disparate functionalities (often with different axioms) into the same operator.”</i> is much different from “<i>function</i> overloading […] encourages programmers to shove disparate functionalities (often with different axioms) into the same <i>function</i>”.')
