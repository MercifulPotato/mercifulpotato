Science works by proposing a hypothesis&#x2F;model and collecting data to see if the data matches the model. If your hypothesis&#x2F;model is just average, and not world-class, then the most carefully collected data doesn&#x27;t hold much weight&#x2F;value.<p>For e.g. Minor changes in the sound signature&#x2F;frequency response are only audible when you listen to the same music across multiple headphones, and that too when you&#x27;re carefully listening. Also the sound signature can vary when the fit of the headphone changes as per the individual head shape&#x2F;ear shape&#x2F;etc. All this is assuming there is enough manufacturing tolerance to produce headphones with identical sound signatures, etc, etc.<p>Ultimately you&#x27;ll have to come up with a hypothesis&#x2F;model that is better than the current one. Which is not to say it can&#x27;t be done, but its a tall order, but I hope you give it a shot at-least. If nothing else you&#x27;ll have a cool blog post :)')
