We built <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;incidents.sh" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;incidents.sh</a>, a &quot;days since last incident timer&quot; for dev teams to keep track of when the last major incident occurred.<p>You just select the date of the last major incident and the timer will update everyday. Whenever there is a major incident, you can reset the timer which will take it back to 0 days. You can show the timer to others by sharing a public link.<p>Previously, we used a whiteboard (before going remote) for this and as you can imagine, it got quite tedious to update it everyday.<p>Our small remote team found this helpful and I hope you will too.<p>And yes, Simpsons already did it!
