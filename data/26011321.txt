Item(by='betwixthewires', descendants=None, kids=[26016379], score=None, time=1612335914, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25988502, text='I felt that way at first, but when I started to understand the reasoning behind it (explained in the Gemini space FAQ and counties meta articles, which is a lot of Gemini documents) it makes perfect sense.<p>When you&#x27;re reading a document, it is to enjoy information. Whether that&#x27;s learning, recreational reading of fiction, or whatever, a subtle part of the experience is the structure of the document. It gives you context and helps maintain a flow of thought. A reference to an external document should be plain as day and stand out. It should not bleed into any other part of the document.<p>This might sound like an over rationalization, but to get an idea for this, imagine if footnotes or references were tossed in using parentheses wherever they were referenced. The structure of the document is important, sometimes as important as the content.')
