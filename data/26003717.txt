Item(by='angry_octet', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612286436, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001972, text='RISC-V seems to be the equivalent of <i>Go</i> in so many ways, stylistic simplicity at the expense of performance and actual simplicity. That doesn&#x27;t make it bad, just flawed like everything else.<p>It won&#x27;t take over user&lt;-&gt;device centric computing (desktop&#x2F;laptop&#x2F;tablet&#x2F;high end mobile). It might become the ubiquitious IoT processor, but the divergence of chip capabilities (and subsequent OS &amp; ABI fragmentation) means nothing will be interoperable, even at source code level. When I compare that to the extreme interoperability of RPi + Pico Pi devices (even with all their painful flaws) I know which one I would prefer.')
