Item(by='matwood', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612390300, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018627, text='If you&#x27;re watching pro sports, you&#x27;re watching those who are at the top of their craft win or lose. Occasionally there are some epically bad teams, but those are outliers. The margin of victory in most pro sports is very small, where the losing team might make 1 less great play than the winning team.<p>So cheering for a team that hasn&#x27;t won big in a long time doesn&#x27;t necessarily mean watching bad play. They might just perpetually be short that one player or coach. And, as someone whose team is usually bad, when they do win it&#x27;s so sweet.')
