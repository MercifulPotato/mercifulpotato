Item(by='Someone', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612259951, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25996553, text='<i>“The Brythonic tribes of pre &quot;Roman times&quot; and the tribes prior to them traded in iron and much more for millenia with peoples at least as far as the Med sea.”</i><p>Feel free to correct me, but whenever I read about goods traveling this far that early in time, I think it’s more likely “they traded with people who traded with people who... people as far as the Med sea”. Direct trade is possible, but IMHO less likely for the time than a series of trade hops.<p>On the other hand, we occasionally find human remains very far from their place of birth. That shows people did travel that far. Expensive goods likely also were traded over larger distances than cheaper ones because there were fewer buyers for them.')
