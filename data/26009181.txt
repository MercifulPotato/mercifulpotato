Item(by='tptacek', descendants=None, kids=[26009272], score=None, time=1612312916, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008916, text='The money you put up to buy a stock is your money. SEC rules require that it be kept segregated. It is at no point available for your brokerage to post as collateral for its own risks.<p>DTCC protects brokerages <i>from each other</i>. Brokerages post collateral to insure each other in case a brokerage fails, and the customers, at other brokerages, on the other sides of the trades from the failed broker, need to be made whole. The clearinghouse isn&#x27;t insuring against you the customer not being there with the $15 to buy your GME share tomorrow. It&#x27;s insuring against something horrible happening at Robinhood that zeroes out all its accounts, drives it out of business, traps all its staff in a deep sea diving bell, that kind of thing.')
