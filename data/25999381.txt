Item(by='arghwhat', descendants=None, kids=[25999755, 26001079], score=None, time=1612262423, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25998359, text='On Linux, libc developers are in the exact same group as regular developers.<p>libc is not intended to be the official entry point in any way or form, and kernel vulnerabilities and workarounds are not meant to be handled by a libc implementation.<p>That other OSs make libc their official interface is primarily because it&#x27;s the simplest thing to do when kernel, libc and the rest of userspace is co-developed, as it allows for breaking kernel changes and other fun things that are not allowed under Linux ABI guarantees anyways.<p>It is not because it is the most secure choice, or that dealing with syscalls is hard (syscalls are easy and safe to work with). It&#x27;s just that stable ABIs are a lot of work to develop, and this structure is just the simplest for smaller OS communities to develop.')
