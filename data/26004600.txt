When I signed up for an online brokerage years ago you couldn&#x27;t trade on margin, which meant no option trading or day trading since when trading without a margin account, your cash has to clear. You had to go to their office and fill out forms and have a minimum balance.<p>With Robinhood everyone gets a margin account and the brokerage clearly can&#x27;t support it with the tiny balances so many of their users carry. Their business model has forced other brokers to follow by offering free trades and everyone is on a margin account. This is a poor practice.<p>I&#x27;d support requirements like ensuring balances of at least $10k or more to qualify for margin. Robinhood is purposely designed to encourage day trading and other margin trades many users don&#x27;t understand or that the infrastructure (not just RH, but the clearing houses, etc) can&#x27;t and shouldn&#x27;t support.<p>There&#x27;s going to be a whole new world of pump and dumps like never before as so many people are hungry for quick money now and taking risks with their rent money they shouldn&#x27;t be.<p>For what it&#x27;s worth, the original guy betting on GME had a smart and well researched hypothesis and I hope he made his fortune. But the lemmings following have no business in this kind of thing.')
