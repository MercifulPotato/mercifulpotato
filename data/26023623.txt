Item(by='avian', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612426319, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26021772, text='~50 EUR, random no-name USB microscopes are surprisingly good these days. I use them for inspecting PCBs and SMT solder jobs.<p>Model names seem to change daily, but they are all very similar. Standard USB video device-class, so no software necessary. I just use guvcview under Linux.<p>Pick one that has &quot;full HD&quot; in the name - it&#x27;s usually not true 1080p, but in my experience the picture quality is still better than the other ones that will have a PAL-like sensor in it.<p>Also, get one with a stand where you can adjust the height of the scope above the object with a screw.')
