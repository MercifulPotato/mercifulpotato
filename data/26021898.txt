Item(by='lmkg', descendants=None, kids=[26022173], score=None, time=1612406003, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26021299, text='Fingerprinting doesn&#x27;t reveal an individual&#x27;s name or passport number, so it&#x27;s not PII. That&#x27;s why PII is not a very useful concept in general, and why claims about privacy safeguards that over-focus on PII should be met with some skepticism.<p>The laws are catching up this in regard. GDPR uses &quot;personal data&quot; and CCPA uses &quot;personal information,&quot; both of which more generally refer to data that identifies an individual.')
