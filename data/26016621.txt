I have a bit of nostalgia for the brand, myself, though did not participate in any of the stock situation.<p>It&#x27;s a tough spot.<p>(1) 3D Printing -- really good idea; I&#x27;m rehabbing my 3D printer for my kids, this evening, so they can print Among Us characters.  If I didn&#x27;t have a 3D printer, I might consider that sort of option, but I&#x27;d probably go online first.  And that&#x27;s kind of the problem they need to find a way to pivot away from.<p>(2) Gamer Bar --  I think a few have panned this idea.  I love it, but not for GameStop.  Their locations are unlikely to be ideal for restaurant style-businesses.  And while it&#x27;s a different kind of business, they&#x27;d still fall under the same regulations which would require substantial reworking of their retail outlets.  Assuming &quot;bar&quot; means &quot;alcohol&quot;, that&#x27;s going to knock out many of the retail locations -- any where getting a liquor license&#x2F;landlord approval isn&#x27;t possible&#x2F;practical&#x2F;affordable.  I have no idea if this would be popular, or if it would fall victim to &quot;teen dance club&quot; problems[0] ... often the way I imagine things &quot;should be&quot; aren&#x27;t they way they turn out :).<p>(3) Tabletop&#x2F;LAN Parties -- Not sure LAN parties are all that useful these days, but maybe?  Moving into other forms of games seems really logical.  Most hardcore gamers that I know are also into a variety of adult board games and all have disposable income.<p>Assuming the idea is &quot;stay retail and keep many of the retail locations open&quot;, I think the biggest problem is finding a product that they can actually sell.  I know people still buy games in physical formats, but that&#x27;s ending at some point.  All of my game&#x2F;software purchases are digital.  All of my console purchases are online and all of my used&#x2F;pre-owned purchases are craigslist&#x2F;marketplaces.<p>I stopped using GameStop to recoup the value of obsolete games&#x2F;consoles because the prices they offered for my used stuff was never competitive and the cost to purchase (often beat-up) used items from the store were similarly not competitive.  Granted, it&#x27;s been <i>years</i>.  Offering to sell used products on consignment or with a firm, low, commission would change my mind.  Offering both options would cover everyone.<p>Maybe going back to 3D printing -- perhaps providing customizable accessories to a variety of console components (i.e. grips for controllers, knobs with icons on them, etc) in-store might be an option.  I don&#x27;t know if that&#x27;s going to be the end-all, but the cost is reasonable considering it offers a huge inventory on-demand.  Focus on covering accessories that are niche but popular and offer customization since they&#x27;re print-on-demand.<p>[0] Where I live, there were a few notorious &quot;Teen Dance Clubs&quot; that charged cover, didn&#x27;t serve alcohol, and were pretty much the most popular place for the police to visit any night they were operating for all kinds of minor crime.')
