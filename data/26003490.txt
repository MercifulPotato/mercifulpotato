Item(by='abecedarius', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612285357, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001919, text='The article&#x27;s invocation of trust is pretty interesting. &#x27;Trust&#x27; is a word with positive vibes; life is clearly better in a high-trust society than a low-trust one. Extending trust to someone builds relations with them; visibly distrusting them does the opposite. So characterizing faith as about trust goes together with thinking of religion as about community.<p>The article uses these positive vibes rhetorically without ever really contesting, it seems to me, the &quot;[Boghossian says] faith is belief without sufficient evidence&quot;. Its supporting link about faith-as-trust goes to the Catholic catechism saying &quot;Faith is the theological virtue by which we believe in God and believe all that he has said [...] because he is truth itself.&quot;<p>I&#x27;m reminded of &quot;trusted computing&quot; -- a positive framing of something you want to minimize your reliance on.')
