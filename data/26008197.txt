Item(by='Green_man', descendants=None, kids=[26008798], score=None, time=1612306649, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007021, text='yes, but the curve is actual numbers, while I haven&#x27;t seen an effective (or standardized) objective measure of soundstage or detail.  Even though the frequency response curve isn&#x27;t _everything_ that matters, because it&#x27;s both relevant to the sound, and can be measured fairly objectively, I think it&#x27;s an important detail in a headphone review. While subjective impressions could say that &quot;bass is anemic&quot; or &quot;no mids&quot;, I may not know (or disagree with) what they consider &quot;normal&quot;. A measured response curve, combined with subjective impressions, can demonstrate the reviewer&#x27;s preferences within a better context. Reviewers who don&#x27;t show a response curve could instead communicate their impressions of other popular headphones, but market fragmentation (and the cost of nice headphones) makes this less useful imo.<p>Largely, I agree with your sentiment, in that reviews of headphones&#x2F;audio are less useful than reviews of other products. In the absence of objective measurements of every important variable, I find myself resorting to buying brands I&#x27;ve had good experiences with, rather than spec chasing. I wish I had more numbers to use, but in the meantime, I try to buy headphones less frequently.')
