Item(by='room500', descendants=None, kids=[26015973], score=None, time=1612371317, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26015116, text='The marginal tax rate can be ~50% for many high-income earners in CA. If you make ~550k, you would be paying 37% to federal and 11-12% to state.<p>Therefore, when thinking about increasing&#x2F;decreasing my earnings at that margin (raises, changing pre-tax contributions like 401k or FSA, etc), it would be correct to use that 50% number.<p>This is obviously incorrect if you wanted to talk about effective tax rate. But using effective tax rate is also incorrect when changing marginal income. So it depends on context.')
