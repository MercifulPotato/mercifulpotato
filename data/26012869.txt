People in places like the Middle East and Australia are used to boiling hot summers and a couple of degrees hotter will probably be just a little bit more uncomfortable... what will completely change the world is the range of diseases and insects changing drastically. Places like the Amazon and the African rainforests are currently very hard to live in because of tropical disease. These areas are bound to expand  by quite a lot, perhaps reaching places like southern Europe and USA. This, together with the shift in agriculture and sea levels, is what will bring an amount of chaos to the world.<p>Far north places like Scandinavia and Canada might actually benefit a little bit by being able to grow crops that they currently can&#x27;t due to their harsh winters and cold nights all over the year! But their southern neighbours will surely suffer a lot, and that&#x27;s where most of the population currently is.')
