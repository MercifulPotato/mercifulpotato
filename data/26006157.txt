Item(by='Wistar', descendants=None, kids=[26009463, 26008779], score=None, time=1612297586, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004697, text='Not super S100 focused but, in the early 80s, I used an Alpha Microsystems S100 multi-user system with a WD16 processor. Later, the company I worked for, a video production company, upgraded to the 68000 based Alpha Micro. The whole company ran on that thing, accounting, writing and I even wrote a small program allowing it to control a Chyron Scribe video character generator in the edit suite.<p>I remember liking the system very much but admit that I didn&#x27;t have much with which to compare it. I thought their OS, AMOS and AMOS&#x2F;L, was great.<p>Someone I know who knew Bill Gates fairly well at that time told me that Alpha Microsystems was a company that truly scared Gates.<p>In 1986, I met Dick Wilcox, one of the Alpha Microsystem founders as he was buying a Pitts S2B at the Auburn, WA airport.')
