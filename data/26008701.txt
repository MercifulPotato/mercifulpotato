Item(by='totalZero', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612309567, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007570, text='Robinhood Instant&#x27;s model doesn&#x27;t actually require a customer to have settled funds to make that $300 purchase, so Robinhood has to pay for the $300 until the customer&#x27;s deposit clears.<p>Separately, when someone with Robinhood Gold buys shares on margin, that purchase is financed in part with Robinhood&#x27;s funds. Look up &quot;initial margin&quot; and &quot;maintenance margin&quot; to understand why. This involves some risk for Robinhood, because assets can sometimes lose value faster than Robinhood can sell them to preserve its equity.<p>Finally, Robinhood is a clearing member and has to put up money as insurance to keep the clearinghouses capitalized in case some bad juju goes down in the markets and Robinhood hits the skids.')
