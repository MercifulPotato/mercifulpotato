I believe I paid ~5% extra. I paid it, and life went on.<p>I think there&#x27;s a huge misunderstanding around the IRS &quot;going after the little guy.&quot; For you to actually be convicted of tax fraud, the IRS would have to take you to court (expensive) and prove you set out commit fraud (hard to do in most cases).<p>At the point where this is worth it for the IRS to do, are you really &#x27;the little guy&#x27; any more?')
