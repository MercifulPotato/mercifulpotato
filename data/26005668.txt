Item(by='ryandrake', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612295402, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004744, text='Browsers should have more say. In a perfect world, the web developer should not even need to know the browser&#x27;s width or height, whether it&#x27;s a phone or computer, or what kind of input devices are available. The developer should simply provide some kind of semantically tagged content (text and images), a list of <i>suggested</i> colors and fonts, and let the browser make the final call, based on what&#x27;s best for the particular platform and screen size.<p>Somehow we&#x27;ve gotten to this world where the web designer expects almost-pixel-perfect control over the layout of every little thing on the page, user&#x27;s preference be damned. You&#x27;re not laying out a magazine page in a desktop publishing application!')
