Item(by='mason55', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612380725, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014579, text='&gt; <i>Laws and rules are messy and can be exploited, not having any recourse if you get scammed is not necessarily a huge pro.</i><p>Yes, many times things like T+2 settlements on trades or taking three days for a transfer to fully clear are a feature, not a bug.<p>You start with Bitcoin being like cash, where once it&#x27;s gone it&#x27;s gone, unless a court can find the person and get it back.  Then you don&#x27;t want grandma to get scammed, so you build bank-like entities on top of the blockchain who re-create the three day window for transfers.  In theory you could have different entities that have different settlement times as a tradeoff, but the modern system could do that now.  The reason they don&#x27;t is you need both parties to have the same rules or else your bank will get bogged down trying to figure out the rules of every other bank.  Maybe you add an instant, non-revocable transfer like a SWIFT equivalent to go along with your 3-day ACH equivalent and, congrats, you&#x27;ve recreated the modern banking system.')
