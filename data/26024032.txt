Let the time pass like sand through the hour glass. Don&#x27;t try to savior it, don&#x27;t try to appreciate it. Waste it, it&#x27;s a luxury, you&#x27;ll never have this time again. Don&#x27;t feel anxious. Your anxiety is trying to pry the time out of your hands, trying to spin up your brain, let it burn some calories. Think of things to do, more better, faster. But there is you, and there is now. And you can observe. You need to breathe. Let it all go. You&#x27;re going nowhere. You&#x27;re secure in this moment. Dunes may shift and the wind will blow around you. Rain will fall on your head. Your value does not diminish. Not less or more. Feel it, you&#x27;re close. It courses through you, time. Appreciate it for what it is, not for what can be done with it. Just a bit. Let go. You&#x27;ll end up doing soon. Thinking. Parading some words into a line, beating them into submission for a service they don&#x27;t care for. But for just one moment, utterly and completely waste time, know it&#x27;s gone, you had it.
