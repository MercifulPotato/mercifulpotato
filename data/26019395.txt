Item(by='tschwimmer', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612387834, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018074, text='This is an english linguistic practice called metonymy. This practice involves referring to a whole entity by something that it is part of it. For example, Berlin is part (the capital) of Germany. This is a very common practice in discussing international politics, with other examples being Washington, Moscow, and Beijing to refer to the US, Russia and China respectively.<p>More info can be found here: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;Metonymy" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;en.wikipedia.org&#x2F;wiki&#x2F;Metonymy</a>')
