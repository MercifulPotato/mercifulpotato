Item(by='Simulacra', descendants=None, kids=[26011142], score=None, time=1612333221, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26010662, text='Amazon warehouses are one of the few reasons, I think we can all agree, that a union has a place. There is the argument to be made that if someone doesn&#x27;t like the job, go elsewhere, it&#x27;s the free market. True, but I see a lot of these fulfillment centers in small towns. They become a major employer, sometimes eclipsing even the local Walmart. It&#x27;s a place where jobs are hard to come by, so maybe Amazon offers something the desperate, the poor, and the struggling something they can&#x27;t get elsewhere. I don&#x27;t have an answer for it, but shifting a little power away from the corporate monolith and to the citizens of the community it inhabits, sounds like a good idea.')
