The thing is, you cannot drag windows between displays, the app has destroy it, connect to another one and recreate it here. There&#x27;s even no mechanism to detect multiple displays other that user setting up the DISPLAY env variable.<p>Or you can do, as the Xorg does: use multiple screens on a single display. Here, the limitation is, that all screens have to have same DPI.<p>Neither of those are acceptable for modern display server.<p>&gt; it will break screen capture tools,<p>It is a good thing that they are broken, because what they do is snooping on windows that do not belong to them.
