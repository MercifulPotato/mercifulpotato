Item(by='motohagiography', descendants=None, kids=[26015097, 26017444, 26014901, 26015118], score=None, time=1612367782, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014525, text='In many privacy regulations, any unique identifier is treated as Personally Identifying Information (PII). If the hashed password is used as a lookup key, that makes it a unique identifier, and it&#x27;s PII. I do work in environments where agencies are not allowed to share user PII with each other, and therefore you can&#x27;t create single (SSO) identities to login to different services.  The principle is that individuals should be able to compartmentalize their business and service relationships and not have their service providers collude or conspire against them for leverage.')
