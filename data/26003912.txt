Item(by='daniel-cussen', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612287335, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25997964, text='Everyone always says &quot;consult your doctor&quot;, it&#x27;s safe advice, but the consensus from doctors I&#x27;ve spoken to, books I&#x27;ve read, and the fact sheets that come with medication make it very clear that you can totally take a day off.  This is nothing like medication for bipolarity, epilepsy, or HIV where you can&#x27;t miss a dose without terrible consequences.<p>On the other hand, you may forget to take your medication, that can really be a problem.  The rule of thumb is, if you forgot if you took it, it&#x27;s because you forgot to take it.')
