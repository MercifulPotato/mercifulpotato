Item(by='TheOtherHobbes', descendants=None, kids=[26002387], score=None, time=1612280872, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002232, text='In the general case, economic measures fail to distinguish between wealth creation and wealth capture.<p>Health insurance is almost pure wealth capture. The provision of services is actually rationed acutely.<p>Public healthcare is wealth creation. There a trickle-out effect as private sector &quot;insurance taxes&quot; are reduced. But access is also increased, which creates jobs in frontline health care services.<p>There&#x27;s a limited role for private industry in health R&amp;D innovation, overlapping with public funding of original research. But provision is most economically efficient and productive when it&#x27;s state-managed.<p>What it isn&#x27;t is politically efficient. Wealth capture is political capture, and dilution of one creates dilution of the other.<p>This is not a popular idea in some quarters.')
