Item(by='searine', descendants=None, kids=[26019900], score=None, time=1612386696, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018550, text='&gt;What I&#x27;m saying is that nobody has delivered on any of the huge claims about the genome which genomicists made for the last 20 years, specifically in terms of actionable human health.<p>I mean. Sure, sequencing the human genome didn&#x27;t solve our problem overnight, and you can&#x27;t sequence a genome at a vending machine for a nickel to tell your future, but I think there has been an avalanche of medical data derived from the genome and that is only continue to get bigger.<p>Now that we are really starting to figure out the polygenic risks and the single deleterious variants and their links with phenotype, people will have a much better picture of what their future might hold (and how to prevent it).<p>I don&#x27;t think it was ever a bluff. The problem just turned out harder than we thought it was going to be.')
