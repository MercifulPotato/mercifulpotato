We assume the speed of light in a vacuum is uniform across all of space and that is how we can estimate how far away stars are in &quot;light-years&quot;. But that doesn&#x27;t make it faith; nobody is opposed to revising that assumption when better evidence comes in.<p>Biblical faith is specifically &quot;the evidence of things not seen&quot;, so it&#x27;s fundamentally different than an assumption.')
