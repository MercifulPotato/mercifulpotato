Item(by='burkaman', descendants=None, kids=[26008799], score=None, time=1612309773, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26008538, text='I guess I&#x27;m stuck on the phrase &quot;just society&quot;. Your position, which is perfectly reasonable, is that a stable society that achieves economic, scientific, and cultural prosperity is a good society. Some people value having an equal voice in their government above <i>all</i> of those things. It is not just posturing, it is a genuine belief sincerely held by many people. They would consider the Islamic Golden Age to be an unjust society. Other people sincerely believe that the current system in America is working, by their metrics. They don&#x27;t want anything to change. Some people would rather die than live in a society where they can&#x27;t practice their religion, some people think any religion is incompatible with a just society, some people think society can&#x27;t be considered just until everyone follows their religion. Since there is no such thing as objective justice, there is no way to &quot;prove&quot; that any of these positions work. They work if you think they work.')
