Item(by='samatman', descendants=None, kids=[25999966], score=None, time=1612255458, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25994332, text='Real talk: the difference between medicinal and recreational use of amphetamines is about 70 to 95mg.<p>Take 5-30 mg of Dexedrine, you&#x27;ll be focused, a bit euphoric, maybe some jaw tension and anorexia. You&#x27;ll get a lot done. Mild crash? It&#x27;s a risk.<p>Smoke a point of meth and you&#x27;ll be taking apart your toaster in no time. That&#x27;s 100mg for those of you who grew up on the right side of the tracks.<p>I&#x27;m diagnosed ADHD, by the way. I prefer modafinil over amphetamines, I find the high milder, the wakefulness promotion is a nice bonus, it lasts longer too.<p>But this is, to be frank, what we used to call an old wives&#x27; tale. There is no medical basis for saying that stimulants hit ADHD brains differently. It seems to be a combination of wishful thinking, and doctors choosing to not let the general public know that low doses of stimulants will improve their focus and executive function as well.<p>Honestly I think that&#x27;s a pity. Stimulants are a useful adjunct for productive work, at a reasonable dose. America did just fine, for decades, with de-facto legal amphetamine, nor has criminalizing it done much of anything to blunt the epidemic of abuse.<p>The difference between ADHD and the elusive &quot;normal brain&quot; is that a lot of us need our meds to function. I&#x27;ve taken a few months off, even a few years at one point, and looking back it was a disaster. I still take weekends off whenever I can, and the lowest dose I think I can get away with.')
