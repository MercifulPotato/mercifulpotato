Item(by='eldavido', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612246361, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25994372, text='To some extent, you just need to look at the hard data: promotions and raises. See who&#x27;s getting them. Whatever they&#x27;re doing is what&#x27;s actually rewarded.<p>It sounds cynical but I learned too late that a company&#x27;s success is only incidental to your own success as someone who works there. If you think this is too negative, I&#x27;ll just say I&#x27;ve seen a lot of &quot;executives&quot; run companies into the ground, then get great, high-paying jobs at acquirers, because they managed the politics well. It sucks but it&#x27;s life.<p>Most tech management, especially young founders of VC-backed startups (vs. larger companies or owner-operated companies where the owners have a lot of skin in the game) is frankly pretty bad. It&#x27;s just not celebrated or taken seriously the way it is in other industries, or fields (e.g. the military). I&#x27;m not saying this pessimistically, quite the opposite--I&#x27;ve seen a lot of bad management and I think there&#x27;s better out there, but it does seem to be vanishingly rare.')
