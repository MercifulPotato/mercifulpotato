Item(by='okr', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612399680, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26021079, text='I can imagine, OSes in general are not prepared to such reported numbers shrinking. There would be a hole.<p>What if a moment ago my OS has still 64G and then all of the sudden it only has 63G. Where would the data go? I think something has to make up for the loss.<p>For me it makes sense to report logically 64G and internally you do the remapping magic.<p>I wonder, how some OSes deal with a hot-swap of RAM. You have a big virtual address space and all of the sudden there is no physical memory behind it.<p>Hm.')
