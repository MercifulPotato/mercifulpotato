Item(by='DanieleProcida', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612351996, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26011789, text='I don&#x27;t disagree about the importance of introductions.<p>I can&#x27;t count the number of times I&#x27;ve looked at the home page of documentation for some product and it has felt like stumbling into a teenagers&#x27; conversation conducted entirely incomprehensible references, slang and in-jokes.<p>An introduction needs to say what the thing is, what it does and why someone might want to use it. Usually that can be expressed in a few sentences.<p>I don&#x27;t really see it as a mode of documentation in the way that the four components I describe are.<p>I wouldn&#x27;t worry too much though. The framework isn&#x27;t intended as a &quot;do this, all this and nothing but this or you&#x27;re doing it wrong&quot;  final statement on the art of documentation.<p>It&#x27;s more a tool for thinking about what you are writing, for whom, and how you can best write it for them. It&#x27;s to be used however someone finds it useful.')
