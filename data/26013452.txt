Item(by='Closi', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612359506, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26011654, text='I&#x27;ve been a Product Owner and been responsible for the product strategy, feature roadmap, backlog refinement and management of external stakeholders, reporting up through product leadership.<p>I&#x27;ve also been in a company that had &quot;Product Managers&quot; and &quot;Product Owners&quot;, where &quot;Product Manager&quot; was just a more senior Product Owner, and the &quot;Product Owners&quot; reported into the &quot;Product Managers&quot;.<p>All I mean is that - it&#x27;s different things in different organisations, and what one company calls Product Owner another company will call Product Manager. If someone takes a job title saying &quot;Product Owner&quot; and expects that to mean they will definitely be reporting up through technical leadership, in the real world I think they will be surprised to find that&#x27;s quite often not true :)')
