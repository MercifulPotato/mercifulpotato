Is it somehow impossible for people who have free will to traffic in other human beings or force them into starvation? I don&#x27;t see the logic.<p>Of course you are implicitly accusing those who &quot;believe in free will&quot; of saying that being trafficked or starving are the person&#x27;s own fault; but I don&#x27;t see the logic of that either.
