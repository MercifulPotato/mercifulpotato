Item(by='nobleach', descendants=None, kids=[26001639, 26004252, 26003288], score=None, time=1612277451, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001256, text='Yes, and webdevs have been trying for over 10 years now to figure out how to get this right. Has no one just stopped to consider having a browser send a `prefersMobileView` or `viewport:phone` or anything like that? I can remember back a few years ago, an iPhone 6+, turned sideways could get a desktop view on some sites... simply because it met the requirements set by the author. Now, the author&#x27;s assumptions were wrong of course, but all we have to go on, is literally guesswork. We&#x27;re told constantly NOT to use user-agent strings, but honestly, it&#x27;s like we really need something to key off of. I know the A List Apart folks would have us create content that can be viewed on ANY viewport, but I consider that a tad bit unrealistic. I&#x27;ve been out of Front-end dev for a few years though, so perhaps things have changed.')
