Fairly exhausting to keep up with, and tends to be a bit cyclic: consoles are ahead for a few years, then behind, then jump ahead again.<p>I think for most people the decision on PC vs a particular console is:<p>* Which types of games tend to be available on that platform.<p>* Which specific games that they like are available on that platform.<p>* Inertial &#x2F; brand loyalty based on what gaming machine they had on the past.<p>* If they play multiplayer, what their friends have.<p>* How much they like tinkering with &#x2F; get frustrated fighting against computers.')
