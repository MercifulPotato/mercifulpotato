The transition from &quot;programmer&quot; to &quot;architect&quot; results after a few years with the architect losing track of where the rubber meets the road.  This results in worse architecture decisions that are made by a similarly experienced programmer.<p>This effect is so strong that if you&#x27;re interviewing someone who became an architect, make them write code in the interview.  If they don&#x27;t do it really well, you don&#x27;t want them in ANY role.  Because now they can&#x27;t program, they can&#x27;t architect.  All that they can do is sound smart while they make bad decisions.')
