Item(by='asiachick', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612258467, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25994452, text='California rolls are not a thing in Japan where as Ramen and Curry are. They do have various rolled sushi styles but &quot;California roll&quot; in particular and all the various common rolls found in the west (tempura roll, caterpillar roll, rainbow roll, etc...) are practically non-existent here. You can find 2-3 restaurants in all of Tokyo that serve them. I knew 3 at one point, 2 went out of business.')
