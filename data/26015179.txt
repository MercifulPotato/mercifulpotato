Item(by='II2II', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612369436, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014086, text='It all depends upon your needs.<p>If you were originally an Excel&#x2F;Word user who switched to Docs&#x2F;Sheets, for reasons other than cost, you would probably be better off sticking with Google&#x27;s product.  This is particularly true if you use Google&#x27;s products for collaboration.<p>LibreOffice is better for people who are looking for a powerful office suite and primarily deal with printed documents (or handle electronic documents much like printed ones).  It is best compared to Microsoft Office: Writer is very similar to Word, though Writer is better thought out in my opinion; Calc is very similar to Excel, though Excel seems to have better options for data visualization.')
