Item(by='mikepurvis', descendants=None, kids=[26013524, 26013274], score=None, time=1612357424, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012403, text='These laws and regulations are about <i>protecting</i> the commons. You&#x27;re prevented from polluting on public land so that it remains usable by others into the future; you can&#x27;t just set up a high powered transmitter and jam everyone because then all the other radio equipment sharing that space would stop working. If you want to operate a boat on the open water above a certain size, you need the proper lights to ensure that everyone is safe out there.<p>The tragedy of the commons would be if these things weren&#x27;t enforced so that the various commons descended into anarchy and a dumping ground for externalities.')
