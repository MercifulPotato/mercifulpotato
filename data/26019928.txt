Item(by='odomojuli', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612390785, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25997638, text='One of my early childhood memories was crayfishing. You can pretty much put anything on a string and these things will bite onto it. They are not picky. It&#x27;s not even really fishing, it&#x27;s almost as easy as just grabbing them from the water. It&#x27;s a fun activity for kids because it doesn&#x27;t require any skill to get good at.<p>They are an indicator species. They are not known for tolerating polluted bodies of water and quickly die if the water becomes toxic.')
