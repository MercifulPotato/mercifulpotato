Item(by='lotsofpulp', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612279314, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001698, text='That seems like an unnecessary complication of the words “belief” and “knowledge”.<p>Belief = assumption + ego.  I prefer to use the word assumption, it’s much cleaner and means the same thing as belief, without invoking emotions.<p>Therefore I don’t see any difference in assuming or not assuming the existence of god(s), and knowledge of the existence of god(s), which are still assumptions anyway.<p>I’m only seeing the utility of defining the following 3 groups:<p>1) assumes existence of god(s)<p>2) assumes lack of existence of god(s)<p>3) assumes nothing regarding the existence of god(s)')
