Item(by='mikepurvis', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612287541, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26003476, text='&gt; In recent years, we introduced Runner (<a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;gobolinux.org&#x2F;runner.html" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;gobolinux.org&#x2F;runner.html</a>)<p>Clever. Seems like it&#x27;s essentially a way to &quot;pause&quot; the rolling distro at a known-good point, and then use that environment to build whatever high-level stuff it is with your set of frozen dependencies— this would definitely do the thing, and would have been very helpful for the ROS on Homebrew effort.<p>OTOH, how different is this from Debian Sid as the rolling release that is occasionally broken when a new low level dep comes in, with the supported releases of Debian, Ubuntu, and others as the shared pause-points? I think ultimately I find it easier putting the work of finding a set of interoperable versions of things onto my distro maintainer (and in exchange knowing that if I want the cutting edge I&#x27;ll have to do my own backport or go to PPAs), rather than taking on that work myself and hoping for the best or risking getting stuck with a bunch of super-old stuff and no clear migration path forward.')
