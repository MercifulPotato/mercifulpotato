Item(by='TeMPOraL', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612259980, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25998690, text='Still, it&#x27;s important to know the story for two reasons. One, to realize that the &quot;luddites&quot; according to current meaning of the word <i>never existed</i>. Second, to realize that the people historically called &quot;luddites&quot; were fighting for the same reason communist revolutions happened, for the same reason you have a good chunk of world&#x27;s population hating the elites (and arguably for the same reason many prior historical revolutions happened) - and it has <i>nothing to do with technology</i>. It&#x27;s all about economic insecurity, about being poor, suddenly becoming poor, or being afraid of suddenly becoming poor, all while seeing fruits of your labor accumulate in your economic superiors&#x27; laps, and then trickle upwards towards the even more wealthy.')
