Item(by='ako', descendants=None, kids=[26014866], score=None, time=1612367796, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012887, text='Usually any impact a salesperson has over the product roadmap is too slow to have any impact on the sales opportunity.<p>Salespeople usually have more influence on the customers than on the roadmap, so they&#x27;re better of trying to influence the decision making of the customer, than the product.<p>Salespeople focus on their sales targets, trying to change a product roadmap is not a very effective way to reach short term targets.<p>Obviously, a product manager should reach out to sales to try and understand their perspective on the product and the customer needs.')
