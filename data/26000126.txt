Item(by='nly', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612268611, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999843, text='Hosting for most companies should be a commodity.<p>I&#x27;ve seen entire businesses supporting double digit employees run off single $20&#x2F;mo DO droplets, with just the builtin backup&#x2F;snapshot capability used for redundancy. One of these had 50,000 concurrent realtime API users on such a droplet.<p>Ultimately what is most important is knowing your needs and how quickly you can recover from disaster, not how much you spend on infrastructure.<p>Simulating the &quot;Ok we came in to the office this morning and Digital Ocean no longer existed&quot; scenario is more valuable to most small businesses than just throwing money at over-engineered AWS solutions.')
