Item(by='pontifier', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612368885, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014053, text='There is actually a lot that can be gleaned from the lack of observable evidence to the contrary.<p>We have a hard data point in ourselves. We know life is possible. The only question is how common is it. Right now the evidence says &quot;Rare&quot;.<p>Now the task is to figure out why it&#x27;s rare instead of common, and exactly how rare it really is.<p>This means gathering data, forming theories, making predictions, and comparing expectations to new data.<p>This seems like perfectly reasonable science to me.')
