Item(by='timw4mail', descendants=None, kids=[26001234, 26001165], score=None, time=1612274932, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26000405, text='The real problem is that designing for different screen sizes, especially with responsive design, is very difficult.<p>For my personal projects, screen size is the factor used to determine layout. Mobile&#x2F;tablet&#x2F;laptop&#x2F;desktop is irrelevant. My goal with smaller viewports is to make the layout as similar as possible, without the user having to scroll horizontally. Because I don&#x27;t want to change the layout too much, I do not use hidden navigation menus.<p>Hamburger menus are terrible - I see them as a design copout.')
