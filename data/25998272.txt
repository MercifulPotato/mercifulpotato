Item(by='zeroflow', descendants=None, kids=[26007332], score=None, time=1612251583, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25998136, text='PCIe slots come either from the CPU or the Chipset.<p>Typically, there are 16 lanes used per GPU and 4 per M.2 SSD.<p>With older CPUs, you had to either reduce the lanes assigned to the GPU to connect an SSD to the CPU, or go through the chipset.<p>With newer CPUs (newest intel or AMD Zen), the CPU provides 20 Lanes (16+4) for general use and in the case of AMD, 4 additional lanes for the chipset.<p>If you want more lanes connected directly to the CPU, you have to go to HEDT or Server Chips with up to 128 lanes available.<p>See this article for a typical block diagram of a AM4 system.<p><a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.guru3d.com&#x2F;news-story&#x2F;amd-ryzen-3000-new-block-diagram-about-pcie-4-on-matisse-and-x570-chipset.html" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.guru3d.com&#x2F;news-story&#x2F;amd-ryzen-3000-new-block-d...</a><p>Here you see, there are 1x16 &#x2F; 2x8 lanes for GPU, and 4x PCIe&#x2F;SATA combo ports from the CPU which can be used for connectivity.<p>More lanes can be provided by the PCH (Chipset).<p>Another example is in this artice by anandtech, which even lists the slots explicitly in the block diagram. <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.anandtech.com&#x2F;show&#x2F;14657&#x2F;the-asus-pro-ws-x570ace-motherboard-review" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.anandtech.com&#x2F;show&#x2F;14657&#x2F;the-asus-pro-ws-x570ace...</a>')
