Item(by='Person5478', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612285923, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26003398, text='I have an analogy I like to use.<p>No one in their right mind would put an engineer on a factory line because said engineer built the machine.  Yet people are completely ok handing off tasks to developers and asking them to do the mechanical part of the job.<p>In both cases you&#x27;re only extracting half the value from that person that you could be.<p>That back and forth with your PM isn&#x27;t wasted time, it&#x27;s you digging in to better understand, and in doing so are given the opportunity to identify and make suggestions due to the expertise you have that the PM doesn&#x27;t.  I&#x27;ll go one further.  Your company should endeavor to put you face to face with the &quot;customer&quot; making the request if it&#x27;s practically possible for exactly the same reason, and your PM shouldn&#x27;t be involved at all outside of setting the priority for the work itself.  This is of course ideal circumstances and there are always reasons why the reality can&#x27;t match exactly, but in general this is what you should be endeavoring to do.')
