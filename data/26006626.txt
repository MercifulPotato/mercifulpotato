Item(by='andrewmcwatters', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612299939, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005221, text='Comparatively, making games is harder than making gaming hardware. It&#x27;s incredible that I find fewer and fewer great games on the market these days when both the barrier to entry is lower, and the available technologies are so much more powerful.<p>But I think the problem results from the above. The barrier to entry is so low that entrants don&#x27;t make many fun games (there&#x27;s just more of them), and the available technologies to saturate usage of are so expensive to fully utilize and staff for that making a fun game falls by the wayside (too few games produced by those that can make great games, and of those, most of them are not even good).')
