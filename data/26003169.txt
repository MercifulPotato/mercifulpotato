Item(by='dreamcompiler', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612284157, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999291, text='&gt; Reducing technical debt by rewriting everything from scratch (almost?) never works.<p>Agreed but this is one of those &quot;almost&quot; exceptions.<p>1. Is the code built to 40+ year old requirements, many of which are irrelevant today?<p>2. Is it hard to make it work for today&#x27;s requirements without widespread fundamental changes?<p>3. Is the code a huge pile of foo that&#x27;s damn near impossible to navigate, much less understand, much less debug?<p>4. Would a hypothetical program that solved <i>today&#x27;s</i> requirements (with an architecture where the most important backwards compatibility features could be hooked in as needed) be smaller, simpler, and easier to maintain by newcomers?<p>If the answer to all of the above is &quot;hell yes!&quot;, you might want to think about a wholesale rewrite.')
