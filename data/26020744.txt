Again, this is disconnected from <i>value</i>.<p>You can see prevailing wages for established companies at levels.fyi . I&#x27;ve no idea how big &quot;small&quot; is but in silicon valley, middle stage startups pretty consistently pay about $175-$225k plus equity.')
