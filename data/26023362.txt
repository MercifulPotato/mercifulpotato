Item(by='hntrader', descendants=None, kids=[26023450], score=None, time=1612423383, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26023342, text='It is.<p>Prices for basic services are lower in tipping cultures, ceteris paribus, since staff wages are a component of price, and staff wages are lower in a tipping culture since they make part of their income from tipping.<p>Hence, the cheap customer benefits to the maximum in a tipping culture since the prices they&#x27;re paying are artificially lower as they&#x27;ve been subsidised by tippers. That&#x27;s why it&#x27;s a transfer of wealth from the generous to the cheap.<p>It&#x27;s got little to do with whether the generous decide to tip more in the presence of the cheap (although that may be a small part of it), it&#x27;s about the indirect and hidden subsidisation through service price.')
