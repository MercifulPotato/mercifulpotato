Products succeed by doing well in a specific market and branching out from there to cover more use cases. RISC-V is not going to succeed in the Desktop &#x2F; Laptop &#x2F; Server CPU markets because the designs are nowhere near as specialized as what AMD&#x2F;Intel (along with Apple I guess..) are putting out.<p>Where RISC-V can show its value is in the IoT &#x2F; Embedded markets. There are not high requirements for speed or efficiency, cheapness and ease of integration is going to win out there. If RISC-V can win in those markets the designs made using it can improve over time to take on markets with higher requirements.')
