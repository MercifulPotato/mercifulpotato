So Google kills off third party cookies, but then creates some new “more private” workaround, perhaps relying on some kind of trusted servers (called “FLEDGE” in the article).<p>And then, who gets to run those servers? Even if it’s not Google, it’s probably not just <i>anyone</i>, right? And would you suppose it would be free for anyone to join this list of approved advertisers? No small-fry browsers allowed. Good luck getting out of that Catch-22.<p>And of another thread where it came up that if you’re trying to get a search engine off the ground, a large chunk of the internet will probably block your crawler bot, so good luck building up that search index.<p>Not all these things are Google’s doing (well, not that last one anyway)... but it sure starts to feel like the open web is closing up around us.')
