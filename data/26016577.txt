There are a lot of things that are potential bottlenecks because the stuff you need isn&#x27;t produced at that scale and there aren&#x27;t unlimited numbers of people that have the right experience. And with producing a vaccine it is extremely important that the production results in exactly what you intend to produce, to ensure it is safe and effective. That part is done by designing processes, carefully checking that they work and then strictly adhering to them. That is not an activity that is easy to scale up.')
