Item(by='ColonelPhantom', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612273195, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999071, text='Not so long ago, I had to use screensharing from Chromium, which I tried from Fedora (I think 31 or 32, it was less than a year ago). It didn&#x27;t work; all I got was a blank screen. Logging out and back in to Xorg fixed it. That convinced me that, at least at that moment, Wayland was simply not ready.<p>Apart from that, I depend quite a lot on things like xdotool, for which I&#x27;m not aware of a (generic) Wayland alternative.<p>If these issues are solved, I may try it again, but overall I&#x27;m pretty sceptical; around mid-2019 I tried GNOME on Wayland and there were some pretty wild rendering bugs that I didn&#x27;t have on Xorg. The compositor being responsible for all rendering also meant that when doing something like opening the app menu, the cursor started lagging, but that&#x27;s probably more of a GNOME problem, but it still concerns me.')
