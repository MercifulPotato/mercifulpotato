Item(by='rmah', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612295654, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005240, text='This is going to sound like a rant, but I really don&#x27;t mean it that way.  I&#x27;m not trying to make a value judgement.<p>GNU picked the term &quot;malware&quot; on purpose.  I think the purpose was to (slightly) confuse, connect a known bad thing with commercial software, and spur further dissuasion.  I doubt they intended to fearmonger though.  That said, your intuition that they picked it to &quot;get the free software conversation going&quot; is, IMO, spot on.<p>They did the same thing with &quot;free&quot; in &quot;free software&quot;.  When people misinterpreted what they meant, they used that as an opportunity to ... explain their point of view.  Many here may not remember when conversations like &quot;it&#x27;s free software&quot; ... &quot;no, not just free in cost, but free as in freedom&quot; ... &quot;yes, I know it&#x27;s cost free, but you can charge money if you want&quot; ... &quot;yes, I know no one charges anything, but it&#x27;s really about freedom!&quot;  was a common conversation in tech circles.  I suspect a similar goal for &quot;malware&quot; now.<p>If clarity was a primary goal, they would have, IMO, picked different words.')
