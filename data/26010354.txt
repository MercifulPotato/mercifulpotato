Item(by='tylerhou', descendants=None, kids=[26010754], score=None, time=1612323607, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26009131, text='During a short squeeze, assets are &quot;mispriced,&quot; and their prices have high volatility. Having a &quot;correct&quot; and &quot;stable&quot; price for assets is fundamentally important to any market as the market&#x27;s intended purposes are to allow society to &quot;efficiently&quot; allocate capital and let participants hedge risk. &quot;Wrong&quot; and highly variable prices inhibit both goals.<p>Of course short squeezes (like any asset mispricing) can be good for individual market participants: but on net for all participants, they are not. That&#x27;s why regulators step in when assets are mispriced, and they have attempted to&#x2F;successfully prosecuted those who have intentionally created short squeezes.')
