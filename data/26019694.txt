Item(by='nextos', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612389431, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26016408, text='I think typing your HLA class I and II genes is the single most valuable thing you can get now from your genome. It&#x27;s also pretty likely to remain extraordinarily valuable even if whole-genome sequencing prices drop to nearly zero.<p>HLA associations with autoimmune disorders are extraordinarily strong. Same applies to infectious diseases, vaccine efficiency and checkpoint inhibitor efficiency.<p>While you can type HLA with classical techniques, the only really reliable way is really to use long reads.<p>Same applies to CYP enzyme superfamily, where variation is linked to some rare drug toxicity events for example.<p>We should all know our HLA and our CYP genotypes. Why 23andme does not even attempt to impute HLA is beyond my understanding.')
