Item(by='prvak', descendants=None, kids=[25998600, 26000874, 25999294], score=None, time=1612252516, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25992782, text='I wrote a meal scheduler - <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;gitlab.com&#x2F;dustin-space&#x2F;meal-scheduler" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;gitlab.com&#x2F;dustin-space&#x2F;meal-scheduler</a>. It tracks ingredients we have at home, it has a database of recipes, it knows where we can buy ingredients online (for delivery).<p>It schedules meals for my shared household.\nI can give it a request like: &quot;next week I want to cook 4 vegetarian meals with 3 portions, and 1 vegan meal for 8 people for big shared dinner, and I want &lt;=2 of those to be noodles.<p>It thinks for a bit with a planner (implemented in Minizinc - <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.minizinc.org&#x2F;" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;www.minizinc.org&#x2F;</a> - with OR-Tools for a solver), and out pops a plan, like: &quot;OK, you need to check you still have 500 g of potatoes, ..., then buy 1 kg of carrots, 1 liter of coconut milk, and here&#x27;s links for the online store; I also checked those ingredients in stock; I also need cayenne pepper, you&#x27;ll have to get it yourself because I don&#x27;t know how to get it online&quot;.<p>The planner can understand things like &quot;there&#x27;s risotto rice, jasmine rice, arborio rice, etc.; some recipes ask for specific type of rice but some recipes are OK with any kind of rice&quot;. And there&#x27;s more things I plan to support in the future, like ingredients that allow &quot;use this ingredient or this other ingredient&quot;, or optional ingredients.<p>When we get a plan, we then check we have all the needed stock, and if we don&#x27;t, we update the stock and replan. And when it&#x27;s done, it generates an ICS file with calendar entries we add to our shared calendar (&quot;today we&#x27;re cooking gnocchi with pesto for 4 people, here&#x27;s link to the recipe&quot;). It also generates stickers, which you print on sticker paper, and you put them on the ingredients that will be needed to cook. The stickers ensure someone doesn&#x27;t accidentally eat too much of them before we need them.<p>It&#x27;s given us a lot of utility for our household. It lets us make tasty fancy meals without the toil of planning. It&#x27;s quite rough around the edges - no fancy UI, lots of &quot;you have to enter this textproto here and there&#x27;s next to no validation&quot;. But I&#x27;m slowly improving it in my spare time to make it more convenient and useful for us.<p>Right now, the published part of it is not useful, it only includes like 8 recipes that we manually entered. But I&#x27;m trying to design it so that it can eventually be a platform, and ideally easy-ish to use for households.')
