Item(by='mLuby', descendants=None, kids=[26018237, 26021919], score=None, time=1612379708, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017369, text='I&#x27;m a huge fan of The Expanse, Atomic Rockets, and Kerbal Space Program, so I felt like I should have loved Children of a Dead Earth too. But try as I might, I just couldn&#x27;t find it fun.<p>I have a feeling that if the game controls were slightly more abstract, it might be better. Less &quot;burn 100m&#x2F;s retrograde&quot; and more &quot;intercept target&quot;, &quot;pull alongside target&quot;, etc.<p>But part of it might just be that realistic spaceship combat isn&#x27;t very exciting; it&#x27;s not conducted at human-relevant distances and timescales, it&#x27;s all over in the blink of an eye, and it&#x27;s hard to judge whether your salvo of nuclear missiles will beat their swarm of laser drones.<p>The Expanse does a much better job of making ship-to-ship combat interesting, but I think they only manage that by ignoring some of the more pedantic hard sci-fi rules (stealth ships in S1E4 &quot;CQB&quot;), and they contrive battle settings (Thoth Station in S2E2 &quot;Doors &amp; Corners&quot;) that are more interesting than CoaDE&#x27;s open space.<p>Still glad CoaDE was made, and it&#x27;s worth a few bucks for the experience. :)')
