Item(by='nickjj', descendants=None, kids=[26023482], score=None, time=1612398436, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018219, text='I&#x27;ve had a Crucial 256GB SSD (MX100) since early 2015 and I use it with Windows 10. WSL 2&#x27;s file system is on there along with Docker, which I&#x27;ve been using full time since then. That means all of my source code, installing dependencies, building Docker images, etc. is done on the SSD.<p>The SMART stats of the drive says it&#x27;s at 88% health out of 100%, AKA it&#x27;ll be dead when it reaches 0%. This is the wear and tear on the drive after ~6 years of full time usage on my primary all around dev &#x2F; video creating &#x2F; gaming workstation. It&#x27;s been powered on 112 times for a grand total of 53,152 running hours and I&#x27;ve written 31TB total to it. 53,152 hours is 2,214 days or a little over 6 years. I keep my workstation on all the time short of power outages that drain my UPS or if I leave my place for days.<p>Here&#x27;s a screenshot of all of the SMART stats: <a href="https:&#x2F;&#x2F;twitter.com&#x2F;nickjanetakis&#x2F;status&#x2F;1357127351772012544" rel="nofollow">https:&#x2F;&#x2F;twitter.com&#x2F;nickjanetakis&#x2F;status&#x2F;1357127351772012544</a><p>I go out of my way to save large files (videos) and other media (games, etc.) on a HDD but generally most apps are installed on the SSD and I don&#x27;t really think about the writes.')
