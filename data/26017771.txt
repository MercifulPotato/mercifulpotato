Item(by='klodolph', descendants=None, kids=[26018829, 26017908, 26017957], score=None, time=1612379508, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26017481, text='I didn’t go into great detail of the reasons, but the jobserver doesn’t address the problem except for the most trivial cases—the core problem is that you can’t cross to a recursive make invocation via multiple edges.<p>This is fairly common in larger projects, so you end up having to do some hackery to manually sequence make invocations if you want to use recursive make (which is pretty awful).<p>Honestly, for large projects, Make is an insane choice, notwithstanding the fact that people who are <i>sufficiently savvy</i> at Make can sometimes make it work. (If your tools are bad, you can make up for it with extra staff time and expertise.)')
