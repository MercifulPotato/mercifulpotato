Item(by='DCKing', descendants=None, kids=[26003192], score=None, time=1612282897, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002751, text='I would agree here, if you could make a reasonable case that the problems trace back the protocol. But my point here is that practically all issues at hand happen <i>outside</i> of what Wayland is used for.<p>Wayland is a display protocol. Practically all of the pain people are experiencing has nothing to do with how Gnome&#x2F;KDE&#x2F;Sway really display things differently. The pain everybody experiences is that the realization that suddenly we need a new input stack too, a new way to screenshare, a new way to do clipboard access, a new way to do a bunch of other things. All stuff that <i>also</i> needs to made, standardized, and achieve maturity outside of the new display protocol.<p>It&#x27;s tempting to call that fallout of other stuff that needs to be changed &quot;Wayland&quot; as well, but I&#x27;d say that misses the point completely and calling it that won&#x27;t help anyone get this stuff fixed.')
