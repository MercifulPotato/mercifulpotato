Item(by='floatboth', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612321238, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004048, text='No, xf86 naming is still there, but you&#x27;re kinda misunderstanding what a driver is. These xf86 things are tiny pieces for the xorg server. (Irrelevant with Wayland, obviously.) The real &quot;meat&quot; of the driver is in two places: Mesa and the kernel. The kernel module is the hard part. You can install the higher level parts without it, they just won&#x27;t be useful. You can have the xf86 thing, and libdrm_nouveau, and Mesa dri_nouveau, they don&#x27;t even require any porting, but they&#x27;re absolutely useless without the kernel driver, they wouldn&#x27;t have anything to talk to.')
