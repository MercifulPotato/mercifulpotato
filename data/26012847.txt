Item(by='KMag', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612353712, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012764, text='No, he didn&#x27;t beat the S&amp;P, but holding some investment in the S&amp;P and some in his fund beat the S&amp;P in risk-adjusted returns (Sortino ratio, Shapre ratio, etc.).<p>As I mentioned, look up portfolio optimization in Post-Modern Portfolio Theory or Modern Portfolio Theory for details, but the gist is that<p><pre><code>  Var(aX + bY) = a^2 * Var(X) + b^2 * Var(Y) + 2ab * Cov(X,Y)\n</code></pre>\nHis fund was essentially uncorrelated with the S&amp;P, so Cov(X,Y) was approximately zero.  MPT uses Var(aX + bY + ... )^0.5 as its risk measure, and PMPT uses downside_variance^0.5.')
