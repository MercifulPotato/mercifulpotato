Item(by='Ari_Rahikkala', descendants=None, kids=[26013026], score=None, time=1612324340, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005919, text='Hello, my name is Ari and I&#x27;m a type addict.<p>Thankfully, the impulse to waste time trying to come up with the most precise typing possible for everything hasn&#x27;t been as strong with TypeScript as it used to be with Haskell for me. In part that&#x27;s because TS&#x27;s type system is so ridiculously expressive that I can say what I want to say without spending too long on it anyway, in part it&#x27;s because the system&#x27;s proud unsoundness and the ability for typings to simply be wrong means that I know not to stake my life on the types anyway. Besides, in my experience, the more precise I try to make the types at an interface, the more I need to cast in the internals. Better to find a balance that keeps both reasonable.')
