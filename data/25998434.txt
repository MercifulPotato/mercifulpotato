Item(by='simonh', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612253623, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25998209, text='He dropped it when Edwin Hubble showed that the galaxies are moving away from us and that the universe is expanding. At that point the notion of a constant value that imposes a static universe became untenable.<p>Note that it wasn&#x27;t so much that the constant as such became untenable, but if the purpose of introducing it is to create a static situation and the situation is showed not to be static, then the reason for selecting the given value goes away and you have to wonder whether you need it at all.')
