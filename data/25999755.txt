Item(by='rwmj', descendants=None, kids=[25999958], score=None, time=1612265339, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25999381, text='<i>&gt; libc is not intended to be the official entry point in any way or form, and kernel vulnerabilities and workarounds are not meant to be handled by a libc implementation.</i><p>Depending on how you define &quot;workarounds&quot;, glibc is full of those.  For example stat(2) is very much not the same syscall now as it was back in the 1990s.  In some cases glibc will do a runtime test to see which syscall variants are supported by the kernel and implement workarounds (I even saw a case where this caused a bug in some programs).')
