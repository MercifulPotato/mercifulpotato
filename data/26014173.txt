I&#x27;ve had a couple classes at work where my company paid $5-20,000  (500-1000 per student depending on how long the class is for) for a guy to come in and give a one week class. As a student in the class I got a copy of the book (someplace on my bookself, I probably opened it a couple times, but never read it). The book is free with class.<p>I talked to several contractors who said the key to their high value contracts was the book they wrote.  Write a book on X and you can make $500&#x2F;hour doing X at other companies.<p>Without writing the book you don&#x27;t get hired to give a class on the book. Without writing a book you don&#x27;t get to consult on the subject. Once you write a book you are automatically an expert. You still need to sell yourself, the book is part of your marketing materials, but it isn&#x27;t enough alone to get those high priced gigs.')
