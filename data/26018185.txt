Item(by='stakkur', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612381389, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018113, text='Whenever this topic arises, I always find myself returning to the idea of &#x27;hire lightly, fire lightly&#x27;.<p>That you can &#x27;measure talent&#x27; by arbitrary nonsense like whiteboarding or regurgitation of CS curriculum has been demonstrated as a fallacy so often that it <i>should</i> be understood by now--but it isn&#x27;t.<p>So: change the shape of the talent pipeline. Hire lightly, give them a chance, evaluate, learn. Fire if necessary. Sure, go ahead and look for patterns and potential metrics, but don&#x27;t overthink it.')
