Item(by='steviedotboston', descendants=None, kids=[26015509, 26015789], score=None, time=1612369945, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26014788, text='Yeah, this is a big problem with the ideology that the FSF pushes. They make it out to be that &quot;freedom&quot; (how they define it, that is) is the only criteria on which to make technical decisions. In the real world that doesn&#x27;t work, and there are a number of other factors to consider.<p>For example, would it be a better choice for a school to choose to use a free software tool over something proprietary even though the proprietary one has better accessibility support for disabled students? RMS would probably say &quot;yes&quot; because he doesn&#x27;t care about people, but anyone with a half-working moral compass is able to realize that accessibility is more important that being able to modify the source code in this situation.<p>I think the FSF would have a much better time rehabilitating their image after the RMS&#x2F;Epstein shitshow by pivoting away from their uncompromising position (which lets be honest, was just RMS bullying people into parroting) towards one that encourages people to consider software freedom one very important aspect on which to make decisions, but not the only one.')
