Item(by='kaycebasques', descendants=None, kids=[26004359], score=None, time=1612288886, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002656, text='I review a lot of technical software documentation. This is one of my go-to resources for explaining how docs should be organized. These ideas are generally accepted among software technical writers; you may just hear them use slightly different words for each doc type. E.g. I use &quot;overview&quot; instead of &quot;explanation&quot;. I&#x27;ll also mention that this framework isn&#x27;t comprehensive; there are other types of critical documents that aren&#x27;t explained here, such as release notes. I think the key breakthrough with Divio&#x27;s framework is getting authors to think about docs in terms of desired goals and outcomes: learning-oriented, problem-oriented, etc.<p>Edit: I suppose you could argue that release notes fall under the category of reference-oriented, but when I look at the guidelines for reference docs it&#x27;s clear that the Divio people were thinking about API references.')
