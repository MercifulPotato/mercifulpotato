Item(by='m12k', descendants=None, kids=[26023557, 26023542], score=None, time=1612425331, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26012189, text='I struggle with balancing the urge to putter like the author puts it, with doing &quot;expansionistic&quot; things like marketing or building features that are valuable to new customers rather than existing ones. The &quot;correct&quot; business analysis will say the business gets much more out of new acquisition channels than micro-optimizing the product further, that I shouldn&#x27;t fall into the &quot;build it and they will come&quot; mentality. But my first instinct will always be to want to just create the best possible product for my existing customers - it&#x27;s what brings me joy, and it&#x27;s really hard for me to designate any suboptimal state of the product as &quot;good enough for now&quot; and focus on acquisition instead. For now I try to balance it by alternating doing one or the other in each sprint - that way I never neglect puttering completely, but I also force myself not to do it exclusively like I&#x27;d probably do if I just based the decision on personal motivation.')
