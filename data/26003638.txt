They ruined their best marketing window, and they didn&#x27;t trumpet the change loud enough when they finally made everything available to anyone.<p>They&#x27;ve basically screwed up marketing on this in every way possible, and now they aren&#x27;t even committed to making first-party games, unlike every other console.')
