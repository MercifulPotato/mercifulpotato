Item(by='EdwardDiego', descendants=None, kids=[26023842], score=None, time=1612427870, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26022934, text='&gt; Autopilots are great and can do much, but not everything (yet?)<p>Biggest issue with autopilots is that, well, they&#x27;re part of the machine, the human comes in very important when the machine is partially crippled - we&#x27;ve already seen what happens to automation when critical sensors fail in the 737 Max.<p>&gt;  You can’t blame a machine but you can blame a person when an accident does happen. Accidents can be happily resolved as “pilot error” without having to call back the entire fleet of aircraft.<p>That&#x27;s not really the case, air accident investigations won&#x27;t blame the pilots just because they&#x27;re there. They&#x27;ll happily point out design defects, or maintenance failures, or pilot error, if it was actually a contributing cause.<p>Of course, plane manufacturers love being able to blame the pilots when their autopilot engages Kill All Humans mode.')
