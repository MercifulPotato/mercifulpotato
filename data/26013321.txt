Item(by='thinkersilver', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612358300, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26007654, text='It helps to instrument the journey of your work from jira all the way to build, deployment, run and monitoring (observation).<p>From that you can get measurements on how long each stage takes and the duration of each transition.<p>From there you can compare team A and B. The transition times is where the human time cost usually sits.<p>Just getting the time when a Jira or feature is raised, to the time it is picked up to the time of the first commit to the time of the first test and final build does already give you valuable insight.<p>The points you raised towards the end can be answered if observability of your  CI&#x2F;CD pipeline is actually in place or at least a place to start a line of inquiry.<p>Naturally the blockers will be aggregated into some of the values but as you work through the journey, they will start clustering at certain stages and maybe highlight a significant problem that needs to be addressed.<p>There&#x27;s a wealth of data being left on the table that can help inform management decisions.')
