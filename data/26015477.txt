Item(by='lazide', descendants=None, kids=[26016354], score=None, time=1612370598, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26015216, text='Home charging often doesn&#x27;t need a faster rate.... because you&#x27;re at home. Same with office charging. Where the faster rate is usually required is where you are somewhere you DON&#x27;T want to spend any significant period of time (like on the side of the road in the middle of nowhere, or on a road trip and don&#x27;t want to stop longer than it would take to use the restroom&#x2F;grab a snack). Those places are also places that already tend to have tons of specialized infrastructure and are co-located in places to maximize convenience (you know, gas stations, tow trucks).<p>Gas cars don&#x27;t have the advantage of being able to charge overnight when you&#x27;re sleeping, or during the day when you&#x27;re in meetings or hacking away on something - so in this sense, it&#x27;s a solid &#x27;pro&#x27; for electric vehicles.<p>It would be pretty easy to setup large scale energy buffers at the equivalent of an electric gas station - from stationary li-ion battery banks, to flywheel energy storage, to supercaps - if spikes were a major issue.<p>With California (and others) having excess capacity during the day due to Solar, it also allows better arbitrage opportunities, though not clear it would offset the costs.')
