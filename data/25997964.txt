Item(by='bootstrapping', descendants=None, kids=[25999066], score=None, time=1612247353, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=25995602, text='&gt; Take regular breaks from the medication<p>As others have said, this is definitely something you must discuss with your personal brain care specialist first.<p>What others have not suggested, and again this is something I have been able to arrange in conjunction with my shrink, is medication switching. Since each has slightly different benefits, I use a different medication on a workday than at the weekend or holiday&#x2F;social time.<p>In this regard I&#x27;ve been fortunate to have access to a healthcare system, and specialists, that respect the individual and aren&#x27;t reflexively invoking a) cookie-cutter solutions, or b) contrived histrionics about substances with abuse potential.  The only raised eyebrow came from my pharmacist: &quot;You&#x27;re on both of these?&quot; &quot;Yes, but on different days, not together.&quot; &quot;No worries&quot;.<p>NB: I&#x27;m hesitant to specify which medications, because individual responses vary dramatically, and advice gleaned from one person&#x27;s experience may be exactly wrong for another.')
