Item(by='astrange', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612306975, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005188, text='I have inherited speakers before, but not headphones. Those don&#x27;t last after years of sweat&#x2F;travel, the internals can break down, and the quality of headphone design has gone up with things like planar magnetic technology.<p>It&#x27;s worth getting old headphones if they were the real top of the line like Stax electrostatics, since you can&#x27;t afford new ones, but those were especially poorly built back in the day.<p>As for open headphones like AKG, people praise them for critical listening but I&#x27;ve found they&#x27;re not good in realistic situations where there&#x27;s any background noise at all. I can&#x27;t get a separate soundproofed listening room in my apartment, so if the fridge decides to turn on, I can&#x27;t hear the bass in an open headphone anymore.')
