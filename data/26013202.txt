Item(by='orbifold', descendants=None, kids=[26017199, 26014535], score=None, time=1612357053, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26011105, text='One thing that helped me manage anxiety was to monitor my caffeine consumption more\nclosely. I found that if I went over a certain threshold (&gt; 4-5 cups of espresso)\nmy overall functioning was pretty severely impacted. Sometimes this resulted in me\nwaking up super stressed and not relaxed at all in the morning and not getting \nenough sleep overall. Now I have a strict no coffee&#x2F;tea after 14:00-15:00 policy. If I feel particularly stressed after a work day I go for a run to really exhaust my remaining energy.<p>I also tend to make lists ~1-2 hours before going to bed, categorised by project, \nfor the next day, so that I can let go of all the tasks that I can&#x27;t accomplish right now.\nI really like Things (paid app for macOS &#x2F; iOS) for that purpose, but also toyed \nwith org-mode and task-warrior.')
