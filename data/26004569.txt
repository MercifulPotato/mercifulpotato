Item(by='napkin', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612290147, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26001558, text='I worked as a techie at a radio station for some years. We maintained (repaired on site) around ~20 pairs of professional headphones for studio usage, used by many hundreds of members of the station. Some of these headphones lifetime definitely exceeded a decade even with heavy use, requiring minor repairs e.g. cables and jacks.<p>Personally, I used one pair of ath-m50x&#x27;s for about ten years. I just replaced it with some Beyerdynamic&#x27;s, which I expect to also last at least ten years.<p>All of the Bluetooth headphones and speakers I&#x27;ve bought have lasted less than a few years. Half the time the batteries fail (and often aren&#x27;t user-replaceable), the other half it&#x27;s some component I&#x27;m unable to diagnose myself.')
