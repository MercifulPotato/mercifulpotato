Pass in the first batch of arguments to the constructor, and store them as fields. Access them from the method (which plays the role of the &quot;returned&quot; closure) when it&#x27;s called later.<p>(This is effectively the same as &quot;closure conversion&quot; in the literature, but we&#x27;re taking advantage of the implicit-receiver convention to hide the parameter by which we&#x27;re passing the prepared fields.)')
