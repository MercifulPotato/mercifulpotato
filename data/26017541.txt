Item(by='_jal', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612378429, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26016686, text='I still heavily use RSS, but usually unsubscribe from feeds that truncate (unless it is a very long article).<p>It isn&#x27;t a religious thing - the vast majority of the time, the truncation is way too short to properly advertise the content. The mystery doesn&#x27;t move me to click-through, quite the opposite - it is annoying, so I remove the annoyance.<p>I understand the tradeoffs for folks who want to be paid via ads, but the utility just isn&#x27;t there for me - I do not lack for reading options, and if something is really good, it&#x27;ll find me some other way.')
