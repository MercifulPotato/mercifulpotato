Item(by='jmull', descendants=None, kids=[26021472], score=None, time=1612397733, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26018056, text='The scammers need to iterate to discover the loopholes, learn how to best exploit them, and to adjust when the platform they are exploiting closes a loophole.<p>A cost per app release drives a stake into the general approach. It doesn’t stop scammers but makes it much harder to do on a larger scale.<p>Meanwhile, legit developers spend hundreds or thousands or more hours on a good app. $100 in the first world just isn’t that much. At the very least the opportunity cost (e.g., the money that could’ve have been made if the time had been spent in a more convention job) easily dwarfs the amount. In lower-income places, then a commensurate amount makes sense.')
