Item(by='NotPavlovsDog', descendants=None, kids=[26012602, 26012599], score=None, time=1612349787, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005758, text='&gt; Instead of measuring some approximation of engineering output, software teams should measure actual, observable metrics that directly correlate to effectiveness.<p>I prefer to measure value created and partially tie it to compensation, directly and indirectly.<p>For a dev team for a trading platform, we had an index of income created by product, approved by team and stakeholders, that affected total compensation paid to team.<p>This is a specific example. The general principle is let tech teams make decisions and compensate them for value created, at least partially.<p>Otherwise, when you don&#x27;t want to share your money, &quot;measurement of productivity &#x2F; effectiveness&quot; comes in. Because when you measure money, people ask, &quot;why am I not getting more when I make you more&quot;? But if you&#x27;re not measuring money, why do a business?')
