Item(by='oconnor663', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612285252, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26002249, text='Weird social effects of being online:<p>If we were in a room of 100 people, and two folks got into a shouting match, everyone would start listening. And then a group decision would get implicitly made. Is it a personal argument, best to stay out of it? Or is someone getting attacked unfairly, and it makes sense to intervene? <i>Everyone</i> in the room starts watching, and <i>everyone</i> starts thinking about what the group is going to do.<p>Online is nothing like that. If someone behaves like a complete asshole on one thread, most people don&#x27;t see it. Especially if the asshole is &quot;some random guy&quot; with no standing in the community. Since there&#x27;s no real drama, the incident doesn&#x27;t come to the community&#x27;s attention. The person on the receiving end of the abuse never gets to have that moment that you&#x27;d have in a room of 100 people, where you know everyone sees you and supports you, and maybe you get some &quot;points&quot; for keeping your cool. There&#x27;s no points for that online, just silence. I think it can feel very isolating and frustrating, and I&#x27;m not sure how to address it.')
