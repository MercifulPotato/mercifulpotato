There usually is a vaccine against the current strain of influenza, which usually hovers around 45%² efficacy (tops out at ~60%).<p>With covid, we have a mortality rate of something between 0.3% and 3%³. The CFR of influenza includes - to the best of my knowledge, since i can&#x27;t find any sources - vaccinated people. So without vaccination, influenza would have a CFR around 0.8% * (100% &#x2F; 45%) ~= 1.78%. This is within the error bars of covid19 CFR, since there was no vaccine until now.<p>I don&#x27;t want to downplay covid19.
