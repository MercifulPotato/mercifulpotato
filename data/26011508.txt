Item(by='ehaughee', descendants=None, kids=[26013213], score=None, time=1612338402, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26009956, text='The key mental shift (for me anyway) is that if a system can be brought to its knees by a single person, then the system is very likely flawed.  You need to design a better system when the flaw in the system is the people.  What that often looks like is changing&#x2F;instituting processes such that quantitative measure (metrics, checklists, etc) governs decisions (thereby removing much, but importantly not all, of the human element), or you design processes in such a way that one person is not in charge of making the decision (the &quot;two person rule&quot;, CRs, leadership approval).  There are of course other tools but these two are pretty common in my experience.')
