Item(by='WorldMaker', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612378333, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26005330, text='Regarding the &quot;bangbang&quot; operator: I agree that the syntax is overly cute and frustratingly confusing to new users of the language, but I still find I often want a &quot;not falsy&quot; operation. I&#x27;ve moved to using the `Boolean(variableToCheck)` pattern. It&#x27;s certainly far more characters to type (without autocomplete) than the pithy double not &quot;bangbang&quot;, but benefits from being explicit in what it is doing and benefits from some small performance fast paths in some JS engines over !!. (Micro-optimizations like that are rarely a good primary reason to switch to a different tool, but it&#x27;s a nice side benefit from all the other reasons including and especially making the code easier to read.)')
