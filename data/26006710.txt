Item(by='gamblor956', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612300214, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004227, text='I don&#x27;t know why you keep bringing up this &quot;bill of attainder&quot; nonsense.<p>A bill of attainder is &quot;a legislative act that singles out an individual or group <i>for punishment without a trial</i>.&quot;  Legislation that singles out an individual or group for other purposes is <i>not</i> a bill of attainder.<p>This bill singles out Facebook and Google, but it does not subject them to criminal punishment. It is therefore, <i>by definition</i> under common law tradition (and also under U.S. law), <i>not a bill of attainder.</i>')
