Item(by='mikepurvis', descendants=None, kids=[26003476, 26006472], score=None, time=1612284774, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26003194, text='Sounds a bit like how Homebrew works (or used to, anyway). Based on my experiences there, one of the big issues with just keeping all versions of everything is that you end up with problems when you&#x27;re trying to build&#x2F;use packages with a large pool of dependencies, especially when the dependencies are interlinked.<p>We used to get problems like this when building ROS on homebrew where you&#x27;d have a bottled version of Gazebo (a robot simulator) which had been linked against specific versions of boost, python, opencv, whatever. And then a new version of one of those dependencies would come out, and something else in the tree would update ahead of Gazebo; now suddenly you&#x27;ve got Gazebo plugins which crash with ABI issues and you have to go spelunking to find out which binaries are linked to what and where the conflict is.')
