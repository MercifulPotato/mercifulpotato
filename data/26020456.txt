Item(by='woodruffw', descendants=None, kids=None, score=None, time=1612393639, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26019103, text='To be absolutely clear: what Homebrew is doing here is consistent with what <i>every other system package manager does</i>. If you&#x27;ve ever done `do-release-upgrade` to bump your Ubuntu LTS, you&#x27;ll have run into the exact same problem.<p>The only difference is that Homebrew doesn&#x27;t have a stable system distribution to pin to (we&#x27;re not Apple, and we have no say over macOS&#x27;s development), so we make upgrade decisions for packages like Python based on their stability with <i>our</i> packaged ecosystem. It&#x27;s a poor practice to use your system Python distribution for any sort of virtualized development environment, and Homebrew behaves identically to every other system package manager in that regard.<p>Please keep in mind that we&#x27;re a community of volunteers answering to millions of users. Your idea of &quot;user hostile&quot; is our idea of &quot;simple for the sake of preserving our sanity.&quot;')
