Item(by='Geminidog', descendants=None, kids=[26006552], score=None, time=1612295593, title=None, item_type='comment', url=None, parent=26004676, text='The disparity within AWS is the result of common inevitability that arises with managing technology built on over a decade of scaffolding. For sure there is a central planner within the company trying his best to put everything together into a cohesive whole. Technical debt is the most common name for this issue.<p>An &quot;Architect&quot; can&#x27;t solve this problem anymore than an &quot;Engineer&quot; can fix the problem of technical debt because neither person can see the future. It&#x27;s like pointing at everything wrong with a piece of technology and saying an &quot;Architect&quot; could&#x27;ve fixed that in hindsight.<p>&quot;Architects&quot; are not the solution to technical design and technical debt.<p>An engineering manager can place all engineers in a room and ask them to come up with an &quot;architecture&quot; that is the same if not better than anything a single &quot;Architect&quot; can come up with, and this &quot;Architecture&quot; will never solve the problem of technical debt for the same reason why we can&#x27;t predict stock prices.')
